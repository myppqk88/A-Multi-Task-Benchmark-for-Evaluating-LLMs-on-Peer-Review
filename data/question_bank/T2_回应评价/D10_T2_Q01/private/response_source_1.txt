Reviewer 1
The sample size is relative small in this study. There is a need to provide a priori power analysis for three experiments, separately.
We have calculated the power analysis. We used the Cohen’s approach:
Cohen, Jacob. 1988. Statistical power analysis for the behavioral sciences. 2nd ed. Hillsdale, N.J: L. Erlbaum Associates.
We use contingency table in experiment 1. Therefore, we used the approach for Chi-square tests: Cohen’s w (effect size). In experiments 2 and 3 we use anova, therefore we apply the Cohen’s f for measuring the effect size. Cohen suggests that effect size index values of 0.1, 0.3, and 0.5 represent small, medium, and large effect sizes respectively.
Experiment 1: Considering w=0.5, with N=12 observations the power is 0.3. The power is not high. Power of 0.8 would need 38 observations.
Experiment 2: The power of test is 0.8 for the left direction, and 0.9 for the right direction. This levels of power can be considered as good.
Experiment 3: The power of test is 0.5. Power of 0.8 would need 14 subjects, instead of 8.
Regarding differences between blind and sighted people, the results are not conclusive and more research is needed with more subjects
How the threshold of vibration for each participants should be explained. The data about participants' vibration threshold should be provided also.
The threshold of vibration is not measured. Each subjects regulates the vibration level. We asked the subjects to regulate the vibration in order to feel it with comfort. The following text is added to the manuscript:
Subjects choose vibration intensity according to preference. All subjects The vibrotactile signal was perceived well by all participants.
It is highly suggested that authors should narrate how the 12 patterns of vibration stimulation were chosen/selected.
This is a relevant question. Thanks to this question we notice that there is a lack of information about the reason for selecting the patterns. The following text is added to the manuscript:
The patterns are chosen in order to analyse differences between: (i) stimuli applied in the anterior region vs. dorsal region, (i) stimuli applied in sequences vs. fixed area
In addition, authors should make hypothesis about which kind of vibration stimulation may best provide information regarding direction based on previous references/findings/possible mechanisms.
This question is related with the previous one. The literature consulted so far provides information about regions where to transmit tactile information successfully. We have not found information about differences between patterns to indicate direction: dorsal vs. anterior, fixed point vs. sequence.

L104 “This methodology gives a score of 0-100 for each vibration pattern”. Who gave the score of 0-100? Participants? How did they do it?
The analytic hierarchy process (AHP) is a structured technique for analysing decisions, based on mathematics and psychology. It was developed by Thomas L. Saaty and has been extensively applied. One of the applications is the selection of one alternative from a given set of alternatives. The subjects do pairwise comparisons. For example: A01 is better than A02 to indicate turn left. The AHP transforms pairwise comparisons in a score. We have expanded the explanation.
