Line 83 notes 'aiming for positive factors' could this perception that hope is a
positive, future oriented practice skew the results? Hope can also be identified in
previous literature as a burden or a 'foolish counsellor' (plato) its important for
the researchers not to define hope based on perception alone.

Answer: We have revised that part of the methods section to clearly reflect what we meant, as our aim from the beginning was to ask the participants which of the four factors of IHS contributed most to the intensity of their specific hope – and therefore a negatively phrased factor could never be chosen as their first option.  We agree that theoretically hope can also have negative consequences on the quality of life on individuals, and we did not define it on ours, or the participants perceptions, we only asked the participants to report which factor influenced their score the most.
