Thank you very much for your thoughtful comment. We acknowledge that reporting exact p-values can be sufficient in many cases and that a Bonferroni correction may not always be strictly necessary. However, in our manuscript we performed multiple correlation analyses simultaneously, and thus opted for a conservative approach to control the overall Type I error rate at 5%. By using the Bonferroni-adjusted threshold (0.05/6 = 0.008), we help readers interpret the statistical significance of these tests in a consistent and transparent manner. Thank you again for your suggestion and for helping us refine our presentation of the results.
