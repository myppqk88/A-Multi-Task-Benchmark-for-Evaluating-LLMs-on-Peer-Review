Article: What type of social media posts about sustainable construction is better for audience engagement?
DOI: 10.12688/openreseurope.17079.1
Version: 1
Reviewer: Esra Kurul
Status: approved with reservations
Date: 2024-03-06
Report ID: 38366
============================================================

There is very little engagement with the literature. This is at the beginning of the paper and is mainly concerned about communciation. However, the realities of scientific communication through social media have not been explored.
The structural characteristics of the 'networks' of the InnoRenew CoE on these three different channels, have been provided at the beginning of the Materials and methods section. This demographic data has not been used to evaluate the 'measures of engagement' on this different platforms.
The discussion is mainly based on the number of impressions, shares and comments. The differences between these 'measures of engagement' by different users has not been established in enough depth. For example, there is a claim that "users may not be comfortable commenting on topics connected to sustainable construction, or that they are not interested in engaging in conversations on social media to develop new relationships." As far as the paper is concerned, this correlation is now based on any evidence.
