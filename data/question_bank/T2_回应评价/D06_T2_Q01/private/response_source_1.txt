Author Response to Review 2 (v1)
Author: author
Date: 
============================================================

The authors are appreciating the critical view of the manuscript and acknowledge the identified importance of the topics addressed by the reviewer. The modifications of the manuscript following the recommendations of the reviewer have improved the manuscript and addressed the feedback given by the reviewer. All the changes are marked with track changes in the Manuscript file.
Point 1: There is very little engagement with the literature. This is at the beginning of the paper and is mainly concerned about communciation. However, the realities of scientific communication through social media have not been explored.
Answer 1: We acknowledge the importance of engaging with the existing literature comprehensively, therefore we addressed this comment by rewriting the Discussion in order to be more engaged with the literature presented in the Introduction. In the Introduction section we provide findings of existing literature on the studied field. The identified gaps led us to create the main objective of our study. We connected the findings form the existing literature with the main findings of this study in the Discussion section.
Point 2: The structural characteristics of the 'networks' of the InnoRenew CoE on these three different channels, have been provided at the beginning of the Materials and methods section. This demographic data has not been used to evaluate the 'measures of engagement' on this different platforms.
Answer 2: This was not the goal of our study, however we provided a description of the audience at the InnoRenew CoE social media channels, since we are aware that the characteristics of the audience is important for the engagement. We rewrote the Discussion section in order to enhance this aspect as a limitation of our study, and added the recommendation for future studies.
Point 3: The discussion is mainly based on the number of impressions, shares and comments. The differences between these 'measures of engagement' by different users has not been established in enough depth. For example, there is a claim that "users may not be comfortable commenting on topics connected to sustainable construction, or that they are not interested in engaging in conversations on social media to develop new relationships." As far as the paper is concerned, this correlation is now based on any evidence.
Answer 3: We acknowledge that knowing the exact characteristics of the audience, the possible audience relapse and similar issues is a limitation of our study. We rewrote this part in the Discussion section to highlight it better. As for the quoted finding of our study regarding users not being comfortable to comment on topics on social media, this is the interoperation of the result which is also in line with previous studies that are cited in the manuscript, e.g.
(
Ellison et al., 2014
)
