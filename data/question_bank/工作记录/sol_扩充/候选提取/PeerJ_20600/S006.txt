Experimental design
The manuscript provides details of the dissemination of the questionnaire online.1. To clarify the number of students. On page 11, it is stated that 60 students are in the course, and then the same-sized target was set at 163.

The total number of eligible students enrolled in the 4th and 5th years of the pharmacy program was 280. The sample size was calculated using the Raosoft sample size calculator (margin of error 5%, confidence level 95%), yielding a target of 163 participants. Although one of the courses used for initial recruitment included 60 students, invitations were extended to all eligible students across both years through Blackboard announcements and reminders.

No inferential statistical tests were performed, as the study aimed primarily to provide descriptive insights into students’ attitudes. Future research may explore correlations between demographic factors and AI perceptions to identify potential predictors of awareness and readiness.

Basic reporting
This manuscript describes a survey approach to understand the perspectives of pharmacy students in Saudi Arabia on the introduction of AI into pharmacy practice and education. This is a contemporary question, and the manuscript adds useful data the complement several previous studies. Greater clarity with the comparisons with these studies would be achieved by a better presentation of the results. There is also a need for a stronger linkage of the findings in this manuscript to previous findings in the Results and Discussion sections.
Experimental design
One concern with the study is that the survey was conducted between January and April 2023. With the rate of movement of AI and the ever-changing perceptions and understanding of this area, this data is almost in the category of “old”. They are still worth publishing, but the authors should note this issue in the limitations section.The definition of the exclusion criteria is inappropriate. These criteria are listed essentially as the opposite of the inclusion criteria. This is not a proper definition of exclusion criteria. These criteria should refer only to the exclusion of subjects (for some reason) within the included group. Please correct this section. The exclusion criteria can probably just be deleted.
