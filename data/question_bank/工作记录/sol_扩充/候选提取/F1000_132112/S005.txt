I suggest that the authors include research questions that were addressed in the study. Research question(s) should be well motivated by the literature review (and introduction) and presented separated from the rest of writing to be more visible.
In the methodology section, it should be made clear how the data were collected and analyzed to answer the research question(s).
Without reference to the research question(s), it is difficult for this reader to appreciate whether the study has achieved what it was supposed to achieve.
