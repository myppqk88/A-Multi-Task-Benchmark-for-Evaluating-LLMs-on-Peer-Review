Author Response to Review 1 (v2)
Author: Famala Eka Sanhadi Rahayu
Date: 2025-05-09
============================================================

Dear Reviewer
We sincerely thank the reviewer for the insightful and constructive feedback provided. In response to the comments, we have carefully revised the manuscript to enhance its clarity, structure, and alignment with the study's objectives. The revisions undertaken are as follows:
1. Addition of Explicit Research Questions (Separated and Clearly Visible)
To address the reviewer's suggestion, we have inserted a new section titled
“Research Questions”
immediately following the Introduction and preceding the Literature Review. This section explicitly states the two research questions that guide the study.
Revision Inserted:
Research Questions
This study is guided by the following research questions:
What types of emotional geographies (sociocultural, moral, professional, physical, and political) are experienced by Graduate Preservice Student Teachers (GPSTs) during their teaching practicum?
How do these emotional experiences influence GPSTs' teaching beliefs, teaching styles, and professional development during their practicum?
2. Stronger Motivation of Research Questions from the Introduction
We have refined the concluding paragraphs of the Introduction to more clearly highlight the research gap and to lead logically into the formulation of the research questions.
Revision Inserted at the End of Introduction:
Although prior studies have examined teachers’ emotional experiences with parents (Chen & Wang, 2011; Sanhadi Rahayu & Asanti, 2021), students (Hargreaves, 2000), colleagues (Hargreaves, 2001a), and principals (Lassila et al., 2017), little attention has been given to preservice teachers’ emotional geography during practicums. Thus, the current study addresses this gap.
3. Clarification of Data Collection and Analysis in Relation to the Research Questions
To enhance clarity, we have explicitly detailed in the Methods section how the data collection and analysis procedures were designed to address the research questions.
Revision Inserted into Instruments Subsection:
This study used semi-structured interviews based on Hargreaves' five emotional geography dimensions. The interview structure was designed specifically to explore the emotional geographies (RQ1) and their impact on teaching beliefs and styles (RQ2).
Revision Inserted into Research Procedure Subsection:
These interviews were designed to gather in-depth qualitative data needed to answer the stated research questions regarding types of emotional geographies experienced and their influence on teaching beliefs and practices.
Revision Inserted into Data Analysis Technique Subsection:
A phenomenological approach was adopted because it best supports exploring participants' lived experiences related to emotional geographies, directly aligning with the research questions.
The data analysis systematically addressed the types of emotional geographies encountered (RQ1) and their influence on GPSTs’ professional development (RQ2).
We hope that these revisions have sufficiently addressed the reviewer’s concerns and improved the overall clarity and rigor of the manuscript. All changes have been clearly marked in the revised manuscript for ease of review.
