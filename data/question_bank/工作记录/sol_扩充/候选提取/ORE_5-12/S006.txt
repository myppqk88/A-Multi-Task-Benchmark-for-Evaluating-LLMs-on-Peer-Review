The authors have made substantial revisions that successfully address the major concerns raised in my initial review.
The revised manuscript demonstrates significant improvement in scientific rigor and practical applicability:
-
The addition of a dedicated Methods section with clear survey design details;
- The expansion from 9 to 13 responses is modest but I acknowledge the effort.
-
The expanded background section now provides good coverage of FAIR and TRUST principles with extensive literature review and examples from related modeling communities.
-
Table 1 provides concrete examples and recommended tools for each protocol step, transforming abstract recommendations into actionable guidance.
-
Figure quality improvements and data format enhancements address reproducibility concerns.
Minor Limitation:
- The sample size remains modest (n=13), though the authors have appropriately contextualized this within their target IAM modeling communities.
The revised manuscript successfully addresses my concerns and provides a valuable resource for promoting open science practices in integrated assessment modeling.

Areas of research: FAIR and Open Science with a focus on Climate Science and more recently Climate Change Adaptation.
