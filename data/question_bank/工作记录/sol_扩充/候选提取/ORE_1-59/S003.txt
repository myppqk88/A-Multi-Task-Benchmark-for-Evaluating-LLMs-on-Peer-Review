============================================================

We thank the authors for the revisions and the responses to our comments. We've thoroughly read the revised version as well as the comments and responses from both the reviewers and the authors.
The reviews of the previous version had requested a number of clarifications, identified a number of methodological concerns and provided sensible suggestions for addressing them. Unfortunately, none of the major issues have been rectified in the revised version of the article. Specifically, the authors still:
fail to provide most of the requested clarifications in the article
draw inferences about participants' cognitive processes from the first author's behaviour
base their conclusions on inappropriate statistical analyses
As a result, it remains unclear what we can learn from the reported study.

Areas of research: SB: language/text comprehension, literary linguistics, poetic form(s); SV: metrics, literary linguistics, Italian literature
