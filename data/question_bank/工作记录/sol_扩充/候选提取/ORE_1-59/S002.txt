I saw that the authors addressed all my modification requests and suggestions in their new version, and I therefore gladly approve the paper. I am aware that other reviewers might have some reservations against certain methodological aspects, but I am not able to judge the internal discussions here, and independently of those comments, I think that the study merits endorsement, since it discusses a very interesting topic and an interesting experiment that was carried out in order to investigate the topic. If there are fundamental flaws in the argumentation, the future will expose them, but this does not mean that this study does not have a scientific value in itself.
