The author did a good job responding to my questions and suggestions. While the sample size is small and the analysis is simple, I think the findings are informative to those who are concerned about the need to concentrate efforts on one or a few program areas in order to provide the greatest impact on society.
The question is an interesting one and this study provides some information about current practices.
