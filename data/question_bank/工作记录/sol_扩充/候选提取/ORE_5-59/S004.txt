============================================================

Thank you for the opportunity  to comment this work. It nicely summarises the activities and results of a project aimed at providing citizens with the necessary knowledge and skills required to assess GDPR compliance. This is indeed a crucial topic in today’s world.
I have a few suggestions in an attempt to clarify and add some information.
First, I would like to encourage the authors to briefly describe the initially planned activities for the project that had to be cancelled due to the advent of the pandemic. Was it possible to find a good replacement for all the cancelled activities (including the most important ones)?
Second, I have several concerns about participation in the ‘massive open online course’ (MOOC) developed by the project. The number of participants is not very large.  How can one explain this? Are there any mechanisms that have been put in place to ensure participation among the more vulnerable segment of the population? Has the outbreak of Covid-19 influenced the demographic composition of the participants?
Third, to evaluate the effectiveness of the MOOC developed by the project, it would be important to test whether participants were able to correctly apply the knowledge and skills learned in this course. Can/has this be done?
Fourth, it would be interesting to know whether the results of the investigations (over 1350) made by citizen scientists have led to any tangible results (e.g., companies correcting errors in GDPR compliance or companies paying more attention in complying with GDPR).
Overall, it is a nice research, but more information about the actual impact of the project could be presented.
