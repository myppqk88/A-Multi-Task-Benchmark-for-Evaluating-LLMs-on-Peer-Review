============================================================

First of all, congratulations to you and your team for the interesting work done. When I carried out my research paper on the challenges and opportunities of space tourism in Spain (Llorente, 2021[ref-1]), I found some of the difficulties that you may have had both in finding experts and in finding subjects for the sample.
That said, I advance you some ideas that I think may be useful to review some aspects of the work:
It would be interesting to know better how the documentary review was carried out, what keywords were chosen to carry out the search and what sources beyond those indicated were used.
I think the presentation of the procedure carried out to validate the semi-structured interview could be improved, as well as the choice of experts instead of including it only at the end in the acknowledgments.
It would have been interesting if the survey included a section on attitudes towards innovation in the space industry Llorente (2021[ref-1]) in line with the theory of planned behavior Ajzen
et al.
(1991[ref-2]).
They could be made more visible and include a summary of the details of the statistical methodology used to validate such a small sample, including the degrees of significance and the statistical tests performed.
Also, as the article points out, some of the most relevant countries in spatial innovation are not represented or are very little represented, such as Spain (1%), Italy and Germany (6%), and especially France (0%), perhaps the most relevant. In future studies it should be expanded to be able to give generality to the conclusions presented.
I hope I have given you some clues to help you improve the paper.
