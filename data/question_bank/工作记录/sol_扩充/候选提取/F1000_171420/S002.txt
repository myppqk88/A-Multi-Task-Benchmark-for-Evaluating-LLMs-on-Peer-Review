Strengths:
The research gap is clearly and strongly identified from the abstract to the introduction. The author uses a global theoretical framework connecting Universal Design for Learning (UDL); IEP/PPI; and Inclusive Pedagogy. The comparison between Oman and Indonesia is consistent across five dimensions: service models, teacher training, curriculum adaptation, community involvement, and cultural attitudes.
Suggestions:
The author could add a critique of the existing literature to justify the need for this comparative study and identify specific gaps that have not been addressed by previous research.
The analysis process does not yet provide the procedural details required by qualitative research standards. The author could explain the inclusion and exclusion criteria for documents, how to determine source credibility, and the analysis steps used, such as coding, categorization, or triangulation techniques, to enhance methodological transparency. It would be beneficial to include a document analysis flowchart and academic rationale for selecting the five dimensions of analysis to enhance the robustness of the method.
