============================================================

Thank you for your valuable suggestions.
In response to your feedback, we have included a discussion of critical media literacy (CML) and ecomedia literacy in both the introduction and discussion of the manuscript, which addresses the intersection of fake news and climate disinformation.
Key works by Kellner & Share (2019) and Lopez (2023) were used to support this analysis.
We also made a clear distinction between misinformation (unintentional spread of false information) and disinformation (intentional dissemination of false information) at the beginning of the manuscript on your recommendation, using definitions from the European Commission (2018; 2020).
