============================================================

Summary:
The manuscript examines the environmental, social, and economic implications of offshore wind energy (OWF) in the Mediterranean Sea, with particular attention to fixed-bottom and floating offshore wind technologies. It highlights ecological impacts, socio-economic considerations, material supply risks, and legislative and governance challenges, providing a qualitative assessment of the sector. The manuscript contains a substantial amount of relevant information and demonstrates awareness of current offshore wind technologies, policy frameworks, and Mediterranean regional context.
Constructive Feedback:
Citation Gaps:
Several significant claims are presented without supporting references. For example, statements about biodiversity risks, EMF and thermal effects, artificial lighting impacts, reef/reserve effects, fisheries disruption, tourism impacts, and strategic dependencies on rare earth elements lack immediate citations.
Legislative and governance assertions regarding Mediterranean EEZ disputes and maritime militarisation require region-specific references.
Structure and Clarity:
The manuscript occasionally repeats information across sections, particularly regarding turbine technologies and environmental impacts. Structuring the “Environmental Challenges” section with enumerated points and separating fixed-bottom versus floating OWFs improves clarity, as suggested by other reviewers.
Flow between sections could be enhanced through clearer transitions or schematic diagrams illustrating interactions between environmental, socio-economic, and technical factors.
Technical Accuracy:
Some statements could mislead readers regarding commercially available technologies. For instance, two-bladed floating turbines and extremely large (>15 MW) turbines exist primarily at the prototype stage. Clarifying commercial readiness would strengthen the manuscript’s credibility.
Regional Context:
While the manuscript references the Mediterranean broadly, more precise information on suitable locations for fixed and floating OWFs and their environmental sensitivity would increase relevance.
Socio-economic impacts, including tourism and fisheries, should be supported by region-specific evidence to distinguish Mediterranean conditions from global generalizations.
Recommendations for Improvement:
Provide references for all significant claims, especially those highlighted above.
Use enumerated lists and sub-sections to clearly differentiate environmental impacts of fixed vs. floating offshore wind.
Clarify which turbine technologies are commercially deployed versus experimental or prototype.
Include region-specific socio-economic and ecological evidence where possible.
Consider including flowcharts or schematic diagrams to illustrate interactions and cumulative impacts.
Points That Must Be Addressed to Make the Article Scientifically Sound:
Ensure all claims are supported by appropriate, current references.
Distinguish clearly between commercial and prototype technologies.
Enhance regional specificity to reflect Mediterranean conditions accurately.
