Keywords
Some of the keywords, such as Emergency Remote Teaching (ERT) and Digital education are just imposed, and nothing is said about them in the text.
Introduction
The introduction needs to be captivating and focus on a substantiation of the chosen research interest, focus or problem.
The researchers need to identify a gap in existing research about the research interest, focus or problem in the study, as implied in the title. Studying “Assessment of the use of Open Educational Resources at five European Library and Information Science”- you need to put a reader on a clear picture of what studies have been conducted in LIS context based on OER. In so doing you will be in the position to identify existing gaps.
Researchers may succeed in substantiating the existence of the chosen research problem (i.e. the existing gap in research) through valid evidence obtained from recent literature.
Briefly outline of the problem exposing the reader to the problem you want to investigate. Here you deliberate on the phenomenon under investigation looking at what the literature says about it. In this section, you need to provide a global picture of what is happening under the topic that is being studied. For example, when writing about OER in LIS sectors ; you need to provide a discussion globally for example, choose at least 4 countries from the West including Asia (UeSA, UK, China, Africa, Brazil,  India etc.). Thereafter discuss what is happening at the national levels (countries) including locally (the area where your study will be conducted).
Statement of the problem
At this stage, the study research problem is not well articulated. With the introduction and background as basis, discuss the research problem in no more than four full sentences. Make sure that the research problem directly links with the information contained in the preceding introduction and background. Convince readers that what you intend to investigate is a real problem and is worth researching.
Significance/value of the study
The OER topic is often associated with the field of Education. Therefore you need to create a strong justification on how OER potential in the field of LIS. Briefly discuss the value that the proposed research will have for the study of science in general, the particular discipline(s) and/or field(s) of study within which the study is demarcated, as well as its value for relevant stakeholders (as applicable). In a nutshell, will this study make any contribution to national or international debates? Will it make any contribution in terms of theory (theory underpinning your study)?/impact within the disciplinary/interdisciplinary context; any contribution in stimulation further research?
Consider infusing Theoretical Frameworks underpinning the study.
Place the proposed research within an appropriate theoretical framework that will guide the study. What theory or theories underpin your study? (Provide relevant literature – in this case, you can use any relevant literature regardless of the date). Limit the number of theories to be used but dig dipper into the theories you have opted to use. You need this theory as you are formulating the research questions. At this stage is difficult to understand how your research question were formulated. You need the theoretical framework or conceptual framework when you are reviewing literature. In this paper, justice is not done concerning the review of literature.  You will need this theoretical framework when discussing your results or doing the writing up.
Methodology

Issues of privacy,

Findings
In this section, you provided a lot of quotations. You need to narrate your results. We want to hear your own voice when presenting results rather than imposing quotations. Presenting findings guided by the theoretical framework or conceptual framework always creates a better logic in this calibre of research.
Conclusion
Reframe the inclusion of citations in this section.
