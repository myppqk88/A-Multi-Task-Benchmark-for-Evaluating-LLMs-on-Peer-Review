Status: approved with reservations

============================================================

Overall, this study provides insight into OER adoption during ERT and highlights the needs for institutional support and guidance to improve awareness and help build the skills for faculty to adopt and adapt OER to their local context. The study provides additional insights into faculty/student use, and provides practical suggestions, consistent with the current literature, on how to overcome barriers to adoption and creation of OER in a global context. I have a few suggestions below on how to improve the clarity of the discussion and methodology sections. Just a note that there are some typos/grammatical errors throughout, and a repeated paragraph in the literature review (starting with “A systematic review by Otto et al).
Study design
Methodology section: I was looking for better alignment between the chosen qualitative approach and a theoretical framework. Content analysis is a qualitative method (not framework for analysis) so a few sentences in this section would be helpful. Interviews and focus groups are data collection techniques (I don’t believe considered methodologies) – so these this needs to be clarified.
It was not clear how you came up with your general coding scheme – did the themes emerge through inductive coding, or were these overall themes identified previous to coding? Earlier you state you used inductive coding, but it was not clear based on your final paragraph in the methods section (it states that there were six thematic sections in the questionnaire). Perhaps add a sentence or two to clarify your approach.
Discussion
The discussion was not always clear. For example in the second paragraph I think your overall point was that across all five institutions, one factor, digital literacy (digital and computing competency), likely determined OER adoption/uptake, and that this uptake was generally quite low. This was not clearly stated, so a bit of rewording could strengthen your arguments.
Barriers to OER paragraph – provide a bit more detail about “legal uncertainty” – is this related to copyright? You don’t mention legal uncertainty as a barrier within your results section – so perhaps unpack that more here to align with your findings (if appropriate).
“One of the guidelines recently established by
UNESCO (2024)
is in line with this need, as it promotes supporting and maintaining peer networks to share OER in different subjects and languages.” – briefly outline what this guideline states for your readers.
Early in your discussion you indicate that OER uptake was low, and later indicate that there was limited readiness…but in the advantages section, you highlight how faculty and students indicate they are easy to reuse, adapt and access – can you add a bit of discussion about these contradictory overall results (perhaps highlight this in your discussion and/or conclusion sections).
There were a few typos (first paragraph “curses” should be “cases”).
You could also refer to some of the institutional adoption of OER studies to help ground some of your recommendations in your conclusion.[1,2]
https://www.ecampusontario.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/2019-08-07-skimore-oe-policy-report.pdf
