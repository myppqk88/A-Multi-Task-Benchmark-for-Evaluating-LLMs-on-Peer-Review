Article: It’s complicated: staff experiences of academic integrity at a Mauritian university
DOI: 10.12688/f1000research.167245.1
Version: 1
Reviewer: Belle Louis Jinot
Status: approved with reservations
Date: 2026-01-06
Report ID: 439572
============================================================

This study is about the academic staff and supporting staff's perceptions of academic integrity and what factors make academic integrity challenging. It adopts a qualitative research method. However, there is a lack of literature review, particularly on the factors that make academic integrity challenging in Higher Education Institutions. Some of the references are outdated.
References to the African context are somehow insufficient. The qualitative data interpretation lacks discussion, and it seems too narrative. The use of I makes the study too personal and sometimes the researcher is too judgmental or emotional in the statements (for example, the use of ..I appreciated...).
There is a lack of ethical consideration as the research site is identified in the section under Discussion.
