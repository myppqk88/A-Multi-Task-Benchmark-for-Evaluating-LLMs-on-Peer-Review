Dear Mirko, thank you for your review! It is very valuable to us for the next versions of our database. We thought about possible explanations why some instrument types are dominant in sectors. A possible reason is that price instruments are easiest to model, so that we need to consider a certain bias due to the methods of the underlying research. This will help to consider limitations when drawing conclusions from analysis of our database.
