Article: Sex difference in body image, exercise motivation and social comparison among Instagram users: a cross sectional study

Reviewer: Vandana Gaur
Status: approved
Date: 2024-05-06
Report ID: 271051
============================================================

The study effectively explores the relationship between Instagram use and body image, exercise motivation, and social comparison, shedding light on potential sex differences. Methodological rigor and clarity enhance its credibility. However, further analysis on the influence of other variables could enrich the discussion. Overall, a commendable contribution to understanding social media's impact on body perceptions.
