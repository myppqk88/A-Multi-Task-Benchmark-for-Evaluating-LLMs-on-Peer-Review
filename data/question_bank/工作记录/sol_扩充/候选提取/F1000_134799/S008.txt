Article: Sex difference in body image, exercise motivation and social comparison among Instagram users: a cross sectional study

Status: approved

============================================================

The abstract effectively summarizes the study's purpose, methods, results, and conclusions. It provides a clear snapshot of the study, highlighting the key findings regarding sex differences in body image, exercise motivation, and social comparison among Instagram users.
The methods section is well-detailed, allowing for replication of the study. The cross-sectional design is appropriate for the research questions posed. The sample size is adequate, and the recruitment methods are clearly described.
The results are presented clearly, with appropriate use of tables and figures to illustrate key findings. The statistical analyses are robust, and the results are interpreted correctly. The study effectively highlights significant sex differences in body image, exercise motivation, and social comparison among Instagram users.
The discussion section provides a thorough interpretation of the results, linking them back to the research questions and hypotheses. The authors discuss the implications of their findings in the context of existing literature, highlighting how their study contributes to the field.
Overall Assessment:
This study provides valuable insights into the sex differences in body image, exercise motivation, and social comparison among Instagram users. The methodology is sound, and the findings are clearly presented and interpreted. The paper makes a significant contribution to the literature on social media's impact on psychological and behavioral outcomes.
