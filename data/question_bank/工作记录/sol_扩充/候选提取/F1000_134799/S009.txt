Dear Ma’am,
Thank you very much for your thoughtful and comprehensive review of our manuscript. We are pleased to hear that you found our study on “Sex differences in body image, exercise motivation, and social comparison among Instagram users” to be valuable and well-conducted.
We appreciate your positive feedback on various aspects of our study, including the abstract, methods, results, and discussion sections. We are glad that you found the abstract to be an effective summary of our study and that the methods section was detailed enough to allow for replication. Your acknowledgment of our sample size, recruitment methods, and the robustness of our statistical analyses, feedback on results presentation and discussion session is very encouraging.
Thank you again for your insightful comments and for appreciating the significance of our contribution to the literature on social media's impact on psychological and behavioral outcomes. We value your feedback and are encouraged by your positive assessment.
Sincerely,
Vasudha K.G.
