============================================================

This is an important study on a topic that has not received ample attention from scholars and practitioners. In this sense, the paper is timely and relevant.
For the most part, I like the article and believe it makes important contributions to the literature. Methods are adequate.
There are a few challenges:
More information can be given about the crowd crush in the introduction and in order to set the context of the paper.
The literature review needs some work. Some concepts are mentioned but they are not explained: e.g., interruption, flight, craze, open area, hidden area, blind area, and unknown area.
The literature review section could be reorganized to improve flow and ease of reading. For instance, the desire to meet in person could be put earlier in the paper or at the end of the literature review. The paragraph regarding Almutairi's work go go earlier, and then all of the findings could be mentioned about crowd crushes. Then, the discussion about how to deal with them can occur.
Finally, the title might be misleading. The paper is not necessarily about the crowd crush per se, but about what governments, businesses, voluntary organizations, and other community groups did before, during and after.  The title could reflect this priority.
Overall, I like what I read. Just a few suggestions to make it better.
