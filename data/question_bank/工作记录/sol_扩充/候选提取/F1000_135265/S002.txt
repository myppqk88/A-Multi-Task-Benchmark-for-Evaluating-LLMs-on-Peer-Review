Status: approved with reservations

============================================================

The paper concerns the issue of crowd crushes. The specific event of  Itaewon Halloween crowd crush, in Korea is focused.
The issue is very relevant and the paper is interesting.
The current version presents some lacks in terms of literature review and methodology.
In relation to the literature review, I suggest the author to consider the proposed emergency planning framework and in particular the preparedness with exercises.
In this context, it is important to recall the evaluation methods for evacuation planning and the contribution of training action for risk reduction.
As far as concerns the methodology, I suggest the authors to provide more details about analysis for aiding the potential reader to understand the proposed method.
Finally, it is important to support the conclusions with some quantitative analysis and research's perspectives.
Suggested references
Russo et al (2014) Urban exposure: Training activities and risk reduction. WIT Transactions on Ecology and the Environment, 191, pp. 991-1001
