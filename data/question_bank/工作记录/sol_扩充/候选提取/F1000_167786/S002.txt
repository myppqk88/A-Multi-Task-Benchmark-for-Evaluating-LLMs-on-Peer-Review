============================================================

The article presents relevant findings on performance monitoring in Ugandan private universities. However, to be scientifically sound, it must address methodological clarity (sampling procedure, response rate), improve theoretical integration, and present more robust qualitative evidence. Greater detail on instrument validity, ethical safeguards, and alignment between findings and recommendations is required.
