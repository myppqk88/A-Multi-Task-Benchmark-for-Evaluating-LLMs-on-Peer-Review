Author Response to Review 1 (v1)
Author: Mohammed Arebo
Date: 2025-04-15
============================================================

Thank you for your detailed and constructive review of our manuscript. Your comments have been instrumental in enhancing the novelty, methodological rigor, and presentation of our work. We have addressed each of your suggestions, as explained below.
1.
The abstract must contain the main contributions.
Response: Thank you for your valuable comment. The abstract has been revised to include the main contributions of the study, particularly the non-linearity test and the analysis of macro-prudential regulation.
2.
I think the introduction should state the novelty of the paper compared to other papers published in the field. Author(s) should be more explicit about how their methodology or results fill identified research gaps to strengthen the justification for publication.
Response: Thank you very much for significant comment. The introduction now explicitly states the novelty of the paper by comparing it with other studies and highlighting how our methodology and results address identified research gaps.
3.
The paper demonstrates an adequate understanding of the relevant literature in the field. However, I have seen that no hypotheses are mentioned, so it is better to mention the hypotheses and show the reader that you have tested them.
Response: Thank you for this significant comment. We updated the literature review with the most recent studies and segmented it to focus on the key variables: (i) financial inclusion and banking stability, (ii) non-linear relationship between financial inclusion and bank stability, (iii) the moderating effects of macro-prudential regulation. This restructuring allows for clearer hypothesis formulation and empirical relevance.
4.
Please give reasons for the choice of methodologies used, why choose the two-stage PCA method rather than the entropy weighting method to construct the Financial Inclusion Index?
Response: Thank you this valuable comment. We have provided a rationale for choosing the two-stage PCA method over alternatives, such as the entropy weighting and CFA method, for constructing the financial inclusion index.
5.
At the same time, I think that the conclusion part of the paper should be extended with contributions, limitations, and future research directions.
Response: Thank you for your significant comment. The conclusion has been expanded considering contribution. Policy implications, and limitations and future research directions presented in separate section.
