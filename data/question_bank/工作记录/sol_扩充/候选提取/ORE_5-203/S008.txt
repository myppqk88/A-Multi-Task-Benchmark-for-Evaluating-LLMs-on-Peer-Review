Dear Authors,
While the methodology is improved in Version 2, a few points could be sharper:
Expert Panel Selection:
The criteria are good, but stating the
number
of publications or years of experience would add objectivity. The gender balance (8 women, 4 men) is noted, but a brief mention of geographical distribution (beyond "diverse institutional contexts") would strengthen the claim of European representativeness.
Co-design Synthesis:
The process of merging "similar outcomes" and using "internal Delphi surveys" is described but could be more transparent. A brief example of what made outcomes "similar" and what criteria were used in the internal ranking would be useful.
About the discussions:
The discussion could be strengthened by providing a concrete example of
how two or three different learning outcomes might be assessed together in a single, complex task
(e.g., a community project) to demonstrate how the "gestalt" can be preserved in practice.
The section on digital badges is somewhat optimistic. It could more forcefully address the significant risk of
trivialization
—where profound competencies are reduced to a checklist for a badge. A deeper critique of the potential conflict between the micro-credentialing logic and the transformative, holistic aims of ESD would be valuable.
Furthermore, the abstract could be even more impactful by stating the
number
of experts (12) and stakeholders (48) involved, highlighting the scale of the validation process. Another recommendation is to
add a visual element
to the results, such as a chart summarizing the coverage analysis findings.
