Thank you very much for your feedback and valuable comments. I agree with your observation of the ‘dark side’ of the digital environment – particularly the fragility of data preservation – or the new surveillance regimes. But I also think, in line with your suggestion, that digital memory-processes could teach us a lot about pre-digital memory processes – with an adequate combination of computational and traditional methods.
I have changed the term “revolutionized” and removed the section on the mediated nature of memory.
Many thanks again for your review and for your work on the archive, which has been essential for thinking about digital memory.
