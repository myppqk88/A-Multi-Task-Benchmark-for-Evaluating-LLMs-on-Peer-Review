============================================================

This data note provides a good overview of a broad preliminary survey to evaluate the extent of RRI practices among five research performing organisations.
What is unclear to me  is to what extent the participants understood the concept of RRI, and if it was explained to them clearly as part of the survey?
According to the RI  literature the main dimensions of RRI are anticipatory governance, inclusivity, reflexivity, responsiveness and responsible impact.
Stilgoe, J., Owen, R., Macnaghten, P., 2013 (Ref -1). Developing a framework for responsible innovation. Research Policy 42, 1568–1580.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.respol.2013.05.008
Naughton, B., Dopson, S., Iakovleva, T., 2023 (Ref-2). Responsible impact and the reinforcement of responsible innovation in the public sector ecosystem: cases of digital health innovation. Journal of Responsible Innovation 10, 2211870.
https://doi.org/10.1080/23299460.2023.2211870
I agree that, Gender Equality, Open Access Publications, Public Engagement, Research Ethics and Integrity, Science Education and Outreach can form part of  RRI but I would like to see a clearer link between these topics and the established RRI dimensions. I would suggest the authors include some text regarding what RRI is and to spell out which RRI dimensions the study topics are connected to.
