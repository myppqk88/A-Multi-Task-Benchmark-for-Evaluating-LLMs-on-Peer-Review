============================================================

Thank you for the positive and helpful review. The manuscript has been revised in line with the main suggestions.
Methodological transparency
The methodological approach is now presented more explicitly, including case selection, evidence sources, and analytical steps.
Why three trade-offs
Clearer justification has been added for focusing on the three selected trade-offs, and the scope implications of this choice are now acknowledged more directly.
Limitations and transferability
The limitations discussion has been expanded, with more explicit reflection on contextual influences and on transferability beyond similar programme environments.
Cross-case comparison
Comparison remains qualitative in this version; no harmonised effectiveness metrics were introduced due to the exploratory design and heterogeneous evidence base. This is now stated as a limitation and identified as a priority for future research.
Implications for practice
Practitioner-oriented guidance has been refined to make the identified patterns more actionable under real-world constraints.
