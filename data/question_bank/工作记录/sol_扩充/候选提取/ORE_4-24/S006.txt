============================================================

The research idea is innovative, and I appreciate how the authors are trying to do the operationalization with some acknowledged limits. My suggestion is to have different sections between Introduction and Theoretical Background (Literature Review). The current introduction section reads more like a theoretical background. I would suggest having an introduction that gives a non-technical audience a general overview of the topic. This is important as the authors are trying to do interdisciplinary research (e.g., communication, social media, sustainability, and construction).
I am also curious about how the authors address research questions. In my opinion, the research questions resemble specific and testable hypotheses. If my guess is right, with the same spirit of reaching a non-technical audience, I would suggest breaking down the theoretical background per hypothesis.
Kudos for your hard work.
