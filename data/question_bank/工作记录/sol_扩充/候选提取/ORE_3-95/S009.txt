Thank you for your additional feedback on this article, which helped me clarify the points you raised. Please find below how I addressed them:
I have specified the context in which the interviews took place. They were indeed done as part of the larger PhD project, and so the model questionnaire contains descriptive questions as well as more interpretative ones. This is now mentioned in Methods, at the end of the third paragraph.
This also helps explain how I went on with my data analysis, focusing on descriptive elements and sorting them using ANT's
linear
(i.e.,
intermediaries

interference

mediators

Peer-reviewed summaries of ANT (i.e., Crawford, 2005; Nikolova, 2008; Cooren, 2009; Hassard & Alcadipani, 2010; Czarniawska, 2016; Michael, 2027a, 2017b; Blok, 2019; Primo, 2019) are referenced throughout the ANT framework part, should the reader want to read more about it.
I hope these modifications are in line with your expectations, and that they make the article fit for publication.
