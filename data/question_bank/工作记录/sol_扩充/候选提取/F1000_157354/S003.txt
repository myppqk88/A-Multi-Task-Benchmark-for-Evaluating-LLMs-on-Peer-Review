Author Response to Review 1 (v1)
Author: Muna Al Kalbani
Date: 2025-02-10
============================================================

Thank you for your insightful feedback. In response to your first comment regarding the literature review’s scope and relevance, I have significantly revised the introduction and literature review to address the areas you highlighted:
I expanded the discussion on the
role of student evaluations
in teaching quality assessment, incorporating more recent literature.
I included additional studies on the
cross-cultural adaptation
of psychometric tools and the
reliability and validity
of the SEEQ in various contexts.
The
transition between global and local applications
of SEEQ was clarified, highlighting the need for further validation in Oman.
I added
more recent and regionally relevant sources
to enhance the timeliness of the discussion.
These revisions strengthen the theoretical framework and align the findings more closely with existing research.
In response to your second comment:
I added a
justification for the quantitative methodology
, emphasizing its suitability for statistical analysis and generalizing results (Creswell & Creswell, 2017).
I provided a
rationale for selecting Muscat College
, explaining how its diverse disciplines, course sizes, and academic years offer a representative sample.
I elaborated on
ethical considerations
, detailing data protection measures, secure storage, and the non-collection of identifiable information.
Also, I revised the methods section to include:
A
detailed explanation of the translation process
, specifying how discrepancies were addressed.
A
justification for the sample size parameters
, referencing standard guidelines for confidence intervals and statistical power.
Additional information on
CFA fit indices
, specifying thresholds for CFI, GFI, TLI, and RMSEA (Bentler, 1990).
additionally, In response to your third comment:
I added a discussion on the
limitations of Cronbach’s alpha and CFA
for cross-cultural validation, highlighting challenges with language nuances and cultural differences.
I provided a
rationale for selecting AMOS v26
, discussing its advantages over alternatives.
I acknowledged potential
biases related to sample size
and the single-institution focus, emphasizing this as a limitation.
In response to your fourth comment:
I clarified that
the translated SEEQ questionnaire and raw data
will be available upon request.
I included
information on sharing the adapted version
for future research.
I noted the
availability of ethical approval documents and consent templates
for review.
In response to your suggestions, I have made the following revisions:
I incorporated
recent and regionally relevant studies
to strengthen the introduction and discussion.
I provided
clearer justifications for methodological choices
.
I elaborated on
sample size, ethical considerations, and translation
.
I added a
critical assessment of statistical tool limitations
, focusing on cultural context.
I clarified
access to supplementary materials
, including the dataset and translated questionnaire, to enhance transparency.
