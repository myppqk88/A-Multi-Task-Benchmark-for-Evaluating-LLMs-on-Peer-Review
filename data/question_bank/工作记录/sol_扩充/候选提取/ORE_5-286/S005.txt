============================================================

The paper aims to conduct a longitudinal single case study of the Enterprise Europe Network (EEN), a pan-European SME support network, to answer the research question: h
ow do performance management systems evolve within public sector networks?
The paper adopts a qualitative research design using mostly secondary data sources supported by primary data following an ethnography type research. The topic of the paper is relevant and significant. It is well written in general. However, the paper also has some shortcomings that should be addressed in a revision:
- The literature review is well organised and critical. Research gaps and problems in practice are presented clearly, which is very good. Indeed, we need further research into performance management in networks, ecosystems and inter-organisational settings, which are increasingly essential to make collaborative innovation and transformation happen.
-
Data sources:
The primary data sources must be clarified. The authors claim to use memos, field notes and observations in various meetings. However, this type of data is not sufficiently referenced or used to support the findings.
-
Qualitative data analysis
: Data analysis is explained regarding the coding exercise, but more evidence should be provided to support the key findings. For example, direct quotes from the interviews/ meetings or short illustrative examples from direct observations can be used to provide further evidence for the findings. Also, inter-coder reliability practices should be explained. Who conducted the data analysis? Only one researcher? Or multiple researchers?
-
Contributions:
The Figure 3 does not show the evolutionary phases with links to the contextual triggers in a convincing way. This framework can be developed further that addresses the research question and reflects the Discussions better. Second, implications for policy should be expanded as this section is very brief in the current paper.
Finally, good luck with developing this work further.
