============================================================

We thank Reviewer 1 for their constructive feedback.
The revised manuscript now clarifies and strengthens the primary data foundations of the study by explaining more explicitly how researcher memos and archival documentation were generated and used.
Section 3 distinguishes primary from secondary sources and indicates that memos and field notes from governance meetings, working groups and training events constitute core evidence supporting the findings.
Section 4 and Table 2 now include concrete empirical excerpts that illustrate partners’ discussions, evaluation feedback and interpretive tensions, thereby making the connection between raw data and analytical categories more explicit.
The methodological section has been expanded to explain the qualitative analysis more clearly. It now specifies how open, axial and selective coding were conducted by the first author and how the co-authors reviewed coding structures and category definitions to strengthen analytical robustness.
This addresses the reviewer’s question regarding inter-coder reliability and the role of multiple researchers. The generic framework has been refined to present more convincingly the links between contextual triggers, the PMS lifecycle and the building blocks.
Figure 3 itself has been refined to depict these three layers and their interrelations more clearly, with arrows indicating how external triggers feed into lifecycle phases and how these phases reshape the building blocks over successive cycles.
The Discussion section articulates more explicitly how this framework answers the research question. Finally, the implications for policy and management have been substantially expanded. The revised manuscript discusses how policymakers can use PMS design to steer network behaviour, how governance arenas support learning and coordination, and how managers can foster appropriation and balanced efficiency across consortia.
Overall, the revised paper offers clearer methodological transparency, stronger empirical grounding and a more fully developed contribution that directly addresses Reviewer 1’s concerns.
