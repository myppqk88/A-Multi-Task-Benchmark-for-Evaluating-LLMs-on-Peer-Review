Author: author

============================================================

We thank Reviewer 2 for their insightful comments and positive assessment of the manuscript.
In response to the suggestion to further develop the theoretical framework, the revised version expands the discussion of key concepts in performance measurement and performance management, incorporating contributions from Bititci (2015) and Smith and Bititci (2017).
The paper also now acknowledges and integrates parallels with bottom-up PMS development approaches, including Garengo and Biazzo’s circular methodology, which helps contextualise and deepen the interpretation of the observed redesign processes.
To address concerns about the descriptive nature of the findings and the need for clearer methodological transparency, Section 3 now provides a more detailed account of data collection procedures and the composition of the primary qualitative material.
The revised manuscript specifies how memos, field notes and archival sources were generated and used, clarifies the structure of the qualitative data corpus and explains how the longitudinal nature of the case is grounded in these materials.
Section 4 and Table 2 now incorporate additional empirical excerpts, making the evidence-interpretation link more visible and addressing the reviewer’s concern regarding robustness and replicability.
Finally, the revised Discussion clarifies more directly how PMS in the Network evolve not only through shifts in what is measured but through managerial processes of appropriation, coordination and evaluation. This helps rebalance the analysis toward the “management” dimension highlighted by the reviewer.
Overall, the revised manuscript strengthens its theoretical grounding, methodological transparency and empirical substantiation, directly addressing the points raised by Reviewer 2 while preserving the integrity of the longitudinal case design.
