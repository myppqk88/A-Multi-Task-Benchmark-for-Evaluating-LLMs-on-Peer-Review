Dear authors:
Here are a few comments on the article:
- Firstly, the abstract should follow the "Introduction. Objective. Method. Results. Discussion".
- On the other hand, the writing is nice and clear.
- Regarding the methodological part, the article does not detail the search process and the selection of the sources analysed. It would be advisable to design the study according to a systematic review where the inclusion and exclusion criteria are detailed (e.g. language, date of publication, type of source...). The lack of systematisation in the search and selection of sources may bias the results obtained.
- In line with the above, numerous studies have been developed in recent years that have analysed the right to education of immigrant children and/or interculturality in the European Union and which have not been covered in this article. To name but a few, we recommend reviewing the INNO4DIV project [Ref 3] & [Ref 2] and some authors (e.g. Jordi Pamiès [ref 4] &[ref 5], Adrián Neubauer ,et.al., [Ref 1] & [Ref 6]].
- The conclusions presented are too brief and are not put in dialogue with other authors.
- Finally, future lines of research are included, but not the limitations of the study itself.
I hope these comments will help you to strengthen your work in the future.
