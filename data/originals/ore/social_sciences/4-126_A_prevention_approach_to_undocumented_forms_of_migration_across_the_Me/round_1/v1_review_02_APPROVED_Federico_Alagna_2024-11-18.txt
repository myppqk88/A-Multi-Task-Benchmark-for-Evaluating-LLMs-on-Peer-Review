Article: A prevention approach to undocumented forms of migration across the Mediterranean Sea: a critical assessment from Italy
DOI: 10.12688/openreseurope.17607.1
Version: 1
Reviewer: Federico Alagna
Status: approved
Date: 2024-11-18
Report ID: 44700
============================================================

I have read this article with great interest. I find this piece very well researched, structured and written. The article explores a relevant and timely issue, shedding light on important dynamics in the governance of undocumented migration across the Mediterranean. As such, I am convinced that this article makes an important contribution, especially by translating the existing findings into clear policy recommendations.
My only concern represents the originality of findings vis-à-vis existing scholarship. In my opinion, the real added valued of the findings presented in this article is that they are the result of a comprehensive study, in which different aspects of the same phenomenon were considered together. Existing scholarship has already widely addressed the different stages of undocumented journeys and the various actors involved, but this has often been done in the context of separate studies, specifically focusing only on one segment (e.g. on the decision to leave; on the decision to resort to a facilitator; etc.). What is innovative here is that all these aspects are considered together, and the whole trajectory is reconstructed.
Also in light of the scientific literature review conducted by the authors, I would recommend that they explicitly engage with the most significant works that have dealt with the various aspects addressed in their article (e.g. the ethnographic studies conducted by Gabriella Sanchez, Luigi Achilli, Julien Brachet and Joris Schapendonk), also by shortly elaborating on how their findings support/refine/challenge previous research.
I hope this suggestion can be of help to the authors and I congratulate them on their work.
