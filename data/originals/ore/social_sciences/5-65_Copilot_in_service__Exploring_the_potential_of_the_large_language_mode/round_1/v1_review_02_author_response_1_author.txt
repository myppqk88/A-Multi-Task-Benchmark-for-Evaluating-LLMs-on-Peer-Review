Dear James C L Chow, Thank you very much for reviewing our paper and suggesting improvements, as well as for sharing valuable resources. Please find below our responses to the points raised:
1. “The authors should include a brief introduction to AI, NLP, and LLM at the beginning of the Introduction section”.
Following your recommendation, we made revisions to the Introduction section.
2. “The authors need to justify why they chose to test the Copilot chatbot instead of other popular bots such as Gemini and ChatGPT. Additionally, they should specify the version of Copilot used in this study”. We explained our choice of Copilot at the beginning of the Method section. Since the versions of the chatbot constantly evolve, we provided references to the specific date when Copilot was used for data collection, indicating the exact version employed. We also referred to the datasets by mentioning the dates of data collection, which correspond with the version of Copilot used. Please kindly find the introduced changes.
3. “When discussing the medical applications of LLM-based chatbots, please reference more recent studies, such as Chow et al. (JMIR Cancer, 2025, 11(1), e66633 and JMIR Bioinformatics and Biotechnology, 2024, 5, e64406”).
Thank you for the suggestion! We included the reference.
4. “In the discussion of ethical issues related to the chatbot, the authors should include important references such as Chow et al. (Front Artif Intell, 2023, 6, 1166014)”.
Thank you for the suggestion! We included the reference.
5. “Before the conclusion, there should be a section on limitations and future prospects, highlighting the limitations of this evaluation study and suggesting areas for future research”. Thank you for the comment. We have incorporated these sections as part of the Method section to better emphasise the connections between the chosen methodology, its limitations, and future research.
Sincerely, Authors
