Article: Copilot in service: Exploring the potential of the large language model-based chatbots for fostering evaluation culture in preventing and countering violent extremism
DOI: 10.12688/openreseurope.19612.1
Version: 1
Reviewer: James C L Chow
Status: approved
Date: 2025-03-12
Report ID: 51991
============================================================

This study evaluated the effectiveness of an LLM-based Copilot chatbot in preventing and countering violent extremism. After reviewing the work, I found it both topical and timely. However, I have a few minor concerns:
The authors should include a brief introduction to AI, NLP, and LLM at the beginning of the Introduction section.
The authors need to justify why they chose to test the Copilot chatbot instead of other popular bots such as Gemini and ChatGPT. Additionally, they should specify the version of Copilot used in this study.
When discussing the medical applications of LLM-based chatbots, please reference more recent studies, such as Chow et al. (JMIR Cancer, 2025, 11(1), e66633 and JMIR Bioinformatics and Biotechnology, 2024, 5, e64406).
In the discussion of ethical issues related to the chatbot, the authors should include important references such as Chow et al. (Front Artif Intell, 2023, 6, 1166014).
Before the conclusion, there should be a section on limitations and future prospects, highlighting the limitations of this evaluation study and suggesting areas for future research.
