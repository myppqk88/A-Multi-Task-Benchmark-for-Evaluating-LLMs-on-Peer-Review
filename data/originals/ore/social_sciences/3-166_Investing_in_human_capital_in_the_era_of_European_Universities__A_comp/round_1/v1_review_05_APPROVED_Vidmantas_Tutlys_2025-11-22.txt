Article: Investing in human capital in the era of European Universities: A comparative analysis of skill mismatches across the regions of the ERUA countries
DOI: 10.12688/openreseurope.16470.1

Reviewer: Vidmantas Tutlys
Status: approved
Date: 2025-11-22
Report ID: 63275
============================================================

The article presents highly interesting and valuable contribution to the research and practice. The provided findings on geographical aspects of skills mismatches are particularly valuable for the articulation of the EU, national and regional strategies and policies of the human capital development.
Research parameters are well defined; research methodology is well founded. The findings are relevant and presented in the well-structured way. The article speaks to the broad audience of researchers, policy makers and other stakeholders.
The only missing aspect is consideration of the implications of the national and region-specific institutional settings and models of skill formation and employment for the skill matching in the analysed regions. But this could be treated as one of the future research areas.
