============================================================

The revised article titled "Automated news in practice: a cross-national exploratory study" by Samuel Danzon-Chambaud represents an original and important contribution to the study of news automation in journalism. The revision addressed the major concerns raised in the first review round. It is now clear that the study uses an exploratory approach to assess the current state of automated news across 18 news publishers in Europe, North America, and Australia.
The study's methodological combination of Hallin and Mancini's media system typology and actor-network theory (ANT), a science, technology, and society approach popularized by researchers such as the French sociologist Bruno Latour, makes it an exciting addition to the journalism studies literature on automated journalism. ANT is particularly well suited to take into account the increasingly strong non-human agency technologies, such as news automation, assumed in the complex socio-technological networks where contemporary newsrooms operate.
Based on the clarifications and substantive improvements applied by the author in the revised manuscript, I can recommend this study for indexing without further modifications.

Areas of research: digital journalism, news audiences, media sociology
