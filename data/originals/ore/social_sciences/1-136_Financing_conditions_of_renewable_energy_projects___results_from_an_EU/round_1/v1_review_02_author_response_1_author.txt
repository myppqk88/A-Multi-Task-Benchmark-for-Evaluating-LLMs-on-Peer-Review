Author Response to Review 2 (v1)
Author: author
Date: 
============================================================

We would like to thank the reviewer for their positive feedback on our work and are very grateful for their constructive comments and suggestions. They most certainly helped to improve the quality of our data note. Please find our answers below.
The data note is a timely work for a well understanding of financial conditions in Europe. It is always challenging to gather data about financial aspects (e.g. debt or equity costs) of renewable energy projects as it could be commercially confidential. I think the research teams did a nice job to ensure the quality of data collection. And it also takes necessary steps to protect the data from its interviewees. I only have several suggestions for further consideration.
I do think project sizes matter a lot for the financial conditions of renewable energy projects. It would be helpful if the data note could clarify the sizes of projects that they are surveying.
Author Response: We added the following sentence: "It should be noted that for the focus countries, the data relates to the following project sizes: for onshore wind (Germany 5-36 MW, Denmark 20-100 MW, Greece 2.7-83 MW, Portugal 500 MW, Spain 70-500 MW), solar PV (Greece 10-50 MW, Portugal 500 MW, Spain 500 MW), offshore wind (Germany 140-600 MW)."
Since “others” ranks as the second largest group of interviewees. The note should elaborate more whom are covered under others besides consultants.
Author Response: We included a more complete definition for the category "other", as follows: "Under this category, we included among others 1) (independent) consultants and experts, 2) RES associations and chambers, and 3) RES plant operators."
The data collection made a division between focus and non-focus countries but the data collection covered all these countries. I would appreciate if the authors could explain the rationales behind this distinction
Author Response: We included a more substantiate explanation of the criteria used for selecting the focus countries in the manuscript and adapted the structure of this part to make it clearer.
