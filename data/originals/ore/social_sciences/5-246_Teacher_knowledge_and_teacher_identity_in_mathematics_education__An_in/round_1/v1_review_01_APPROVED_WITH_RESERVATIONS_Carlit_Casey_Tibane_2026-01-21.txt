Article: Teacher knowledge and teacher identity in mathematics education: An interdependent model

Version: 1
Reviewer: Carlit Casey Tibane
Status: approved with reservations
Date: 2026-01-21
Report ID: 66613
============================================================

The manuscript presents a theoretically robust and well-structured review that engages substantively with contemporary literature on mathematics teacher professional knowledge and identity. The topic is discussed in a largely comprehensive manner within a clearly defined theoretical boundary, offering an integrative synthesis rather than an exhaustive mapping of all possible perspectives. Factual statements are generally accurate and supported by relevant and up-to-date citations; however, some higher-level interpretive claims and model-specific propositions rely primarily on authorial synthesis and would benefit from clearer signaling as conceptual in nature or from the addition of supporting references where available. The review is written in clear and coherent academic language appropriate for a specialist readership, although its conceptual density and extensive use of specialized terminology may limit accessibility for broader audiences. To strengthen the manuscript further, the authors are encouraged to clarify the methodological positioning of the review as a theoretical or integrative synthesis, explicitly delimit the scope of the literature considered, and include brief signposting or summarizing statements in longer sections to enhance readability.
The article presents a strong and credible theoretical contribution, and its central arguments and conclusions are largely justified and well aligned with the current literature. However, it would benefit from targeted revisions to strengthen methodological transparency, clarify the conceptual scope of the review, and improve accessibility for a broader readership.
