1. Methodological positioning of the review and support for factual statements
We fully agree. In the revised manuscript we have clarified, at a higher level of prominence, that the paper is an
integrative theoretical synthesis
rather than an exhaustive mapping or a formal “systematic review” in the strict PRISMA sense. As follows:
Abstract (methodological positioning):
We added an explicit statement positioning the article as an integrative theoretical synthesis and clarifying that it combines a structured search and thematic synthesis without claiming exhaustive coverage.
Methodological approach (early signposting):
We added a short “positioning” subsection that explains the rationale for an integrative synthesis in relation to model construction and conceptual re-organization.
Supporting methodological references:
To avoid reliance on authorial assertion, we incorporated established methodological sources on integrative/theoretical reviews and review typologies, which justify the approach adopted.
Because the main contribution of the manuscript is conceptual (i.e., the construction and articulation of an interdependent model linking teacher knowledge and teacher identity), an integrative theoretical synthesis is the most appropriate methodological form. The revised wording is intended to align the reader’s expectations with the actual goals and procedures of the review.
Furthermore, Reviewer #1 (Dr. Carlit Casey) states that the manuscript could benefit from clearer signaling as conceptual in nature or from additional references where available. This is because of a couple of ungrounded statements. So, we agree and have implemented two complementary improvements (a) and (b):
(a) Clearer signaling of epistemic status
Epistemic-status paragraph for the model:
We added an explicit statement clarifying that the relational links within the model are
conceptual propositions

(b) Additional supporting references where feasible
For claims about the integration of disciplinary, didactic, and contextual knowledge—and the role of situated cognitive skills in practice—we reinforced citation support with widely recognized international and Spanish scholarship.

These changes maintain the manuscript’s theoretical contribution while aligning the presentation of claims with standard scholarly expectations: statements are supported with citations, and conceptual claims are transparently framed as propositions emerging from synthesis.
2. Delimitation of the Scope and accessibility
R#1 aims for clarification of the scope, so we have strengthened the manuscript’s scope statement and made the boundaries of the corpus more explicit and reader-visible:
Databases, timeframe, and language:
We state clearly that the corpus was delimited to peer-reviewed journal articles indexed in Scopus and Web of Science, published in the 2015–2025 timeframe, and written in English or Spanish.
Search strategy and selection:
We clarify the search strings, screening process, and inclusion/exclusion criteria, and we report the initial records and final corpus used for synthesis.
Rationale for delimitations:
We explicitly justify why these boundaries were necessary to maintain conceptual coherence and ensure feasibility for an integrative synthesis aimed at model construction.
These additions increase methodological transparency while preserving the manuscript’s theoretical intent. They also directly address the concern that the review might otherwise be read as claiming a level of exhaustiveness that was not intended.
Furthermore, we’ve also addressed the “conceptual density” issue by improving readability without diluting the specialist-level rigor:
Signposting:
We added short roadmap sentences at the start of selected longer sections to orient the reader to the section’s function in the overall argument.
Summarizing closures:
We inserted brief “In sum…” statements at the end of longer conceptual sections to consolidate the key takeaways before transitioning.
Terminology management:
Where possible, we reduced repeated technical phrasing and clarified transitions between knowledge-focused and identity-focused constructs to support readers who may be more familiar with one literature than the other.
These revisions are intended to preserve conceptual precision while making the synthesis easier to navigate for a broader readership across the mathematics education community.
