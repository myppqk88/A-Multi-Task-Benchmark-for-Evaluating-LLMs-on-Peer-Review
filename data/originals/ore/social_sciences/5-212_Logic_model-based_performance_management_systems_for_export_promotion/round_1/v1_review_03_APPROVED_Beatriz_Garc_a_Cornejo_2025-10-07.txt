Status: approved

============================================================

The authors have carefully addressed all major suggestions raised in the review. The revised version now includes a clearer definition and visual representation of the Logic Model, expanded context on Spanish EPAs, an explicit discussion of multi-actor accountability and the risk of decoupling, and clarifications on the methodological procedures. In my view, one of the most valuable improvements is the rewritten conclusion, which now directly answers the research questions and illustrates the practical use of indicators through the European network case. This significantly strengthens the paper’s contribution. The discussion has also been enriched with reflections on benchmarking and sustainability, while the issue of efficiency ratios has been resolved by focusing on robust FTE-based indicators. Although the comparative table of KPIs was not included, the authors acknowledge its value and propose it as a direction for future research. Overall, the manuscript has improved in clarity, analytical depth, and transparency. I consider it suitable for acceptance in its current form.

Areas of research: Management accounting, management and control systems in health care, financial and efficiency analysis, agricultural economics
