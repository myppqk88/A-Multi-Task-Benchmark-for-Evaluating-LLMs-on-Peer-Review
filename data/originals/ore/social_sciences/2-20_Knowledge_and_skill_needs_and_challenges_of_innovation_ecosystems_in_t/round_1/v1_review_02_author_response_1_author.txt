Author Response to Review 2 (v1)
Author: author

============================================================

We would like to thank the reviewer for their comments and recommendations
that helped us improve our paper. The manuscript has been revised accordingly in an effort to address them. The revised manuscript has been resubmitted.
In more detail, as the reviewer's suggestion for the analysis of the Likert scaled data. As the reviewers' comments reached the authors at a point at which the analysis could not be performed with a more appropriate model, the respective analysis has been removed entirely from the manuscript. In addition, the literature has been updated based also on the input of the 2nd Reviewer. In principle, the literature presented in Section 2 "Related Work", aimed to fuel the generation of the initial baseline framework of skills and knowledge, as well as the timely need for such an assessment. The authors strongly believe that performance is an integral part of our rationale for creating a baseline of knowledge and skills framework, as it supports the fact that knowledge and skills are an essential contribution to the success of innovation intermediaries' support service delivery towards local space innovators. To further enrich the flow of the manuscript, the specific research questions have been introduced in the Introduction, whereas the Results and Discussion section has been enriched to better outline the contribution of our work to the literature. Finally, a Limitations section has been introduced to the manuscript to address the comment on the sample distribution and country representation.
