============================================================

This paper provides new empirical evidence and knowledge and skills in the space sector for European countries. I have several suggestions and comments which are listed below.
Main points:
The dependent variables are Likert scaled. Ordinary Least squares (OLS) is not appropriate to analyse the Likert scaled data. Likert scaled variables are usually analysed using the ordered probit or ordered logit model or structural equation modelling (see a standard econometrics textbook, for instance Wooldridge or Greene). Given the sample size, I suggest to use the ordered probit or ordered logit model.
The quoted literature only partly motivates the empirical model. This paper is about competencies and skills in the space industry, and not about performance.
The empirical model and the specification are not satisfactory. One type of skill cannot be related to other types. Of course, different types of skills are correlated. I cannot see the control variables. Have you included country dummy variables or firm size?
Please outline the contribution of the work to the literature a bit more. Please elaborate on what is already known in the literature and what is the research gap. I also suggest clearly formulating the specific research questions the study focuses on.
The conclusions are very generic. There are a few implications for stakeholders. More work is needed here.
The online survey is not presentative. 62 per cent of the respondents are coming from three countries. Are the results different for countries in Eastern Europe? Please provide information on the universe of the respondents (source).
There are too many diagrams and tables and very little text. Please look at the study again and revise it.
Specific comments:
Abstract: Please make the abstract more interesting for the readers. Please be more specific. “….several interesting insights for the future of the European space innovation have been identified…” This is too generic.
There are several typos and the language is not always smooth. Example: Countries in the CE and SE Europe, be they with emerging or existing space…”
Introduction: The paragraph on total factor productivity growth of the CE and SE countries does not fit.
Please provide descriptive statistics of the dependent and independent variables. I can see that the means are reported but not the standard deviation.
Please also elaborate on the sampling design (stratified random sampling) and the treatment of non response.
Figure 3: Please change to a table. A graph based on 15 observations does not look good.
Table 6: add -values.
The decimal system in English is the dot (e.g. 1.0) not the comma (1,0). Please adjust all tables (see for instance Table 3).
The authors should give information about the software command. The software package (Gretl) is given, but not the command.
