The research titled “Disinformation as an obstructionist strategy in climate change mitigation: a review of the scientific literature for a systemic understanding of the phenomenon” presents a detailed study of the publications that address a topic of special relevance and relevance, such as disinformation about climate change and the social impact it produces. As noted in previous reviews, the article stands out for its methodological approach, theoretical foundation and justification of the findings. In this sense, the authors have appropriately incorporated most of the observations made after the first review: distinction between disinformation and misinformation; definition of critical media literacy; clarifications, justifications and terminological changes at different points of the work.
On the contrary, we believe that the paragraph dedicated to Hahn's criticism of Al Gore's book should be revised again, since the wording continues to be confusing. Specifically:
- “He portrays the denier lobbies…: By “he” do you mean Hahn or Gore?
- “Who are portrayed are romantically”…: Is it possible that about an “are”?
- ... with disinformation” / are romantically”…: There are two closing quotes in a row
To avoid recurring references to the work of Almiron & Moreno (2022), it is suggested that you consult two publications whose content is related to this topic:
- Heras, F..et.al., 2018 (Ref 2)
- Abellán López , M. Á.et.al., ( 2021 ) (Ref 1)
