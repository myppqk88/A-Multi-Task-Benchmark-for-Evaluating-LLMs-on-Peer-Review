============================================================

I find that this note is a useful guide to a systematic literature search. It should help the researchers to conduct their literature reviews.
Although the purpose of this note is purely technical, still it would be very interesting and useful for the readers, if the author could explain in a few words what "Affective Economies" is.
A concise explanation of the FAIR approach is necessary.
If I correctly understand the FAIR approach, the references must be easy to find. However, my experience with SCOPUS and Web of Science databases shows me that their use is not very easy and may require paying a fee which can be an insurmountable obstacle in particular for the researchers from the Global South. I hope that I am mistaken. But if it is true, what are the ways to overcome this challenge.
Is exclusion of studies written in other languages than English is fruitful? It can lead to information losses, which are especially relevant when the countries beyond the Majority World are the focus of your study.
