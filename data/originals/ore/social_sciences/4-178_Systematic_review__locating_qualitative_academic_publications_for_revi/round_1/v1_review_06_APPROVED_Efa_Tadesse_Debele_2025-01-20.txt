============================================================

The article is fascinating. If the author included some articles from low-income countries or Africa like Private Rental Housing Market in Ethiopia, which was published by Heliyon, the overall article standard and scientific soundness would be critical. The article conveys very important scientific ideas and the potential to be replicated.
Major Points
Strengths:
Focus:
The article addresses urgent and troubling issues in the private renting sector.
Methodology:
Methodological protocols for rental housing research are thoroughly investigated.
Relevance:
Emphasis on tenants' and landlords' experiences and interactions.
Methodological Rigor:
Employed meticulous methodological approaches.
Scientific Soundness:
The article maintains a high standard and conveys important scientific ideas.
Criticism:
Scope Limitation:
The article could have included more information on the private rental housing market in low-income countries in Africa, where the housing market's institutionalization is in danger.
Minor Points
Notability:
The author’s decision to open the methodology for scrutinization, replication, and adjustment depending on the needs and purposes of researchers in the area is commendable.
Replication Potential:
The article has the potential to be replicated due to its comprehensive methodological approach.
Overall, the article is a robust and well-constructed article, though it would benefit from a broader geographic focus. Great work in highlighting its strengths while suggesting areas for improvement!
