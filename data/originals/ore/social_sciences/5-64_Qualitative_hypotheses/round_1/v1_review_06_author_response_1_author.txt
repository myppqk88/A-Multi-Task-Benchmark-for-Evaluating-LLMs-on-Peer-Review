Thank you for the helpful and review feedback. I have taken each point into consideration and respond to them below one by one. As a general note, I have tried to maintain the encyclopedic format, i.e. keep the text accessible to a wide range of readers and not make it longer than necessary. For this reason, the discussion of some aspects, such as epistemic ones, remains relatively light but is now explicit.
“Consider further clarifying how QHs practically differ from general expectation”  I have clarified the differences with comparisons to concepts, which are related to QHs. Ethnography, grounded theory, and reflexive thematic analyses are noted.
“Strengthen the bridge between the conceptual background and the specific operationalization of QHs” Epistemology and other aspects of qualitative traditions are now addressed in more detail.
“Use clear subheadings”  I have revised the section structure.
“The suggestion to cite Dehalwar & Sharma (2023, 2024) is noted”  I read one of the sources, and I agree it serves as a general reference to hypotheses testing; it is now cited to support a claim. In particular, the suggested citation from Maxwell was very helpful, and it is given more space to clarify QHs.
“Use hypothetical examples or case studies to illustrate.”  An example has been added.
“Refine the tone for consistency with scholarly writing. ”  I have updated language, but some of the personal (first person) tone has been intentionally left due to the content involving a personal element.
“Consider concluding with specific recommendations for researchers or editors regarding when and how QHs might be integrated into research design, preregistration, or reporting practices.”  The mode of integration (writing QHs into preregistrations and RRs) has now been made more explicitly throughout the text. I considered ending the text with additional recommendations, but concluded that it is currently premature and we need more actual use cases before recommendations can be given.
One more thank you for all valuable feedback and helping to improve the work.
