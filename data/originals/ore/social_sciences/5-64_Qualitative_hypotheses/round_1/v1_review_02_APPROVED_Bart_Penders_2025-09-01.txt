The revision (revised version = version 2) is accompanied by a very brief note, leaving a lot of the changes to be diagnosed by the reader. I would recommend the inclusion of a more extensive discussion of changes next time.
The conceptual reframing of the Qualitative Hypothesis (QH), its relative positive compared to Quantitative Hypotheses, meant for testing, positionality statements and theory and literature in general, has been greatly improved in this version. This extends to the in-text use of an example, displaying what QHs could look like and what they could do in practice.
The claims the essay makes were and still are modest. In this state, I can recommend its acceptance.
