Article: What type of social media posts about sustainable construction is better for audience engagement?
DOI: 10.12688/openreseurope.17079.1
Version: 1
Reviewer: Florin Nechita
Status: approved
Date: 2024-07-24
Report ID: 42014
============================================================

The research is relevant for communicating about sustainable construction in a research project dissemination context. As most research dissemination projects have either no communication budgets or very limited budgets, this study is also relevant from this perspective. The researchers presented their case study by communicating three types of content distributed across three different social media platforms. However, most communication campaigns on social media platforms have budgets for running A/B testing of different messages and testing the effectiveness on each channel. Therefore, the results of this study are limited to research project dissemination in a zero-budget context. It is assumed that there was a zero budget for communication on social media, as this information is not provided. The authors should include as a limitation the fact that testing the video content was not considered in their experiment.
