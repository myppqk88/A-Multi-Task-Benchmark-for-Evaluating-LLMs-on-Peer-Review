Thank you for the revisions and responding to my comments. Regarding the new paradigm, maybe making the sub-title clearer will help readers understand your intention.
The current title is "Introduction: a new paradigm in educational robotics". Instead, maybe you can say "a new paradigm in educational robotics for inclusive approaches". This way, it is clear that the new paradigm is not for educational robotics but to provide an inclusive learning experience for the learners.
On page 6, the top of the left column, I would change the sentence to:
"Of course, some of the criteria in Table 1 can be influenced at some extent by the pedagogical options that a teacher makes. For instance,
regarding the performance of Lego Mindstorms against the 1st criterion (“enables students to build DIY robots”), a teacher who is familiar with how to integrate maker approaches in her/his teaching may ...."
