Thank you very much for your thoughtful and detailed review, which I found immensely constructive. I have carefully addressed your comments as follows:
Wider Rights Framework
: I acknowledged that the Open Letter focuses primarily on Articles 7, 8, and 45 of the Charter but also inserted a clear new sentence explicitly stating that broader rights (such as the right to life, non-discrimination, rights of persons with disabilities, children’s rights, etc.) must be integrated as MEDiate evolves. Specifically, in Section 3.2.3 (United Kingdom testbed), I inserted a new paragraph discussing how the MEDiate DSS includes safeguards (access controls, human-in-the-loop oversight) when processing personal data for evacuation decisions, and how automation must not obscure the ethical stakes involved, such as the rights of persons with disabilities under the CRPD.
Supporting References
: I systematically reviewed the text and inserted numerous additional citations where previously assertions were unreferenced, particularly in the literature review and case study sections.

Clarifying DSS Operation

The ethical oversight mechanisms for automated decision-making are now clearly spelled out.
Terminology
