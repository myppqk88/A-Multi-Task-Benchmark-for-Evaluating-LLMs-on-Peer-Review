Clarification of EMF limits and precautionary advice:
We acknowledge your concern regarding our formulation of precautionary information. We understand that from a technical and regulatory standpoint, compliance with EMF exposure limits is already based on precautionary principles. However, in some countries, including Germany and Greece, there is additional public health information that informs about the possibility of minimizing exposure as a general precaution, in addition to the limits that are in place. This information is not legally binding but communicated by institutions like the German Federal Office for Radiation Protection (Bundesamt für Strahlenschutz, BfS). To avoid misunderstanding, we have revised the relevant passages to more clearly distinguish between legally enforced limits and voluntary precautionary behavior encouraged by health authorities.
Scope of the paper:
We fully agree that our paper is not intended as a technical analysis of EMF exposure. Rather, it is focused on the social perception and behavioral responses related to EMF and mobile communications infrastructure. On the role of pseudo-science in public perception:
We find your observation about the challenge of distinguishing legitimate research from pseudo-science highly relevant. While this was not a focus of our current paper, we agree that misinformation and pseudo-scientific narratives can significantly affect public debates and trust in regulation.
