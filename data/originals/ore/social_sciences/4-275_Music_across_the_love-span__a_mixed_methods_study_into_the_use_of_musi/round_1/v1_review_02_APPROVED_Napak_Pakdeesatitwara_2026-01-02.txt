============================================================

Thank you for your invitation to review this quality research. The authors implemented a mixed methods survey to investigate the roles of music in different stages and aspects of romantic relationships. They uncovered musical affordances in the area of romantic relationships.
I find the study findings novel and have practical implications for music therapists who work with the relationship areas of mental health, especially in younger adults. I consider this study holds great value and is worthy of indexing.
It seems that this manuscript has already been refined, as demonstrated in its consistency and thoroughness. The discussion shows that quantitative and qualitative findings were brought together to complement one another. The style of integration is similar to the convergent design, and the role of qualitative data here is mostly to provide expansion to the quantitative findings. Nonetheless, the mixed methodology, where the data were integrated, could be more explicitly described in the method section. Generally, the important part of a mixed methods study is data integration and meaning-making of the integrated findings. I recommend that the authors consult
(Creswell, J. W., & Plano Clark, V. L. (2018). Designing and conducting mixed methods research (3rd ed.). Sage.) This book offers guidance on data integration and how to report a mixed methods study that the authors may consider. The book may also prompt the authors to see other relationships between both sets of data (e.g., confirmation, divergence, explanation).
My only suggestion is to clarify the methods of data integration in the methodology section and highlight the meaning derived from integrated findings (as already shown in the discussion) in the conclusion, and if possible, in the abstract.
