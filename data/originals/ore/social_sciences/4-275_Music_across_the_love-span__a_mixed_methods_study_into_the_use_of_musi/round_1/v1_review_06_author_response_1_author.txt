Dear Dr Bode,  Thank you very much for taking the time to review our manuscript and for your constructive comments. We have submitted a revised version, which should be available online soon. Below, we summarise how we addressed your comments.    Introduction Thank you for your comments regarding terminology and references. In response to your comments, we made the following changes:
We replaced all instances of “romantic love” with “love in romantic relationships” and provided a brief definition, noting that this encompasses both romantic and companionate love.
We added Kowal et al. (2024) to support the validation of Sternberg’s model.
In the section on romantic relationship stages, we integrated more recent literature while retaining foundational sources as meaningful frameworks. We clarified that these models serve as heuristics and acknowledged that relationship trajectories can be non-linear or more diverse. We also noted that many couples start as friends rather than experiencing a distinct attraction phase.
We added a brief definition of “mate choice”.
Methods
Thank you for highlighting issues related to sex and gender. In this study, participants were asked to report their gender identity (“Which gender do you identify with?”). We have now clarified this in the Methods section. In the survey, we used the options 1 = female, 2 = male, 3 = non-binary and 4 = other. We acknowledge that labelling the options as “woman, man, non-binary and other” would have been preferable and we will take this into account when developing future questionnaires
Regarding age, correlations with age were already reported in Table 2 in the previous version. No significant correlations were observed, except for a small, non-significant positive correlation between age and the perceived relevance of music for commitment (r = 0.18). However, this may simply reflect the fact that older participants were more likely to be in a long-term committed relationship. Further research is needed to disentangle the effects of age and relationship duration on the perceived importance of music in romantic relationships.
We hope these revisions address your comments. We remain open to any further feedback.
