Thank you very much for your constructive and insightful comments. I have thoroughly revised the paper in light of your feedback, and I believe these changes have significantly strengthened the review. Below, I address each of your comments and explain the adjustments made.
Introduction: Structure and Clarity
I have restructured the introduction to improve clarity. The concept of "big data" is now introduced with a more thorough explanation of its characteristic elements, including a clearer distinction between structured and unstructured data. 

I have also reconsidered the analogy of data being “the new oil,” and, following your advice, I have removed  such statements.
Regulatory Framework: Broader Perspective
The section on the regulatory framework has been expanded to offer a broader and more comprehensive discussion of the legal challenges associated with TDM, particularly from a data protection standpoint. I have added references to opinions from public agencies and supervisory bodies to provide a more holistic view of the regulatory landscape.
Data Protection Issues: More Depth and Careful Source Selection
The data protection section has been substantially reworked to include more authoritative and recognised sources, as you recommended.

Additionally, I have expanded on the concept of "meaningful research," elaborating on how data protection regulations address the processing of data for scientific, research, or statistical purposes.

I have also incorporated references to the EDPB Guidelines 4/2019 on Article 25 (Data Protection by Design and Default), as you suggested.
Data Mining, Profiling, and Inferences
I have significantly expanded the discussion on the challenges and risks of data mining, particularly regarding profiling and the processing of inferred data.
Legal Aspects of Data Scraping
I have added a paragraph discussing the legal aspects of data scraping citing the sources that you had recommended.
Fundamental Rights: Bias and Discrimination
I have reworked the section on fundamental rights to more thoroughly address the critical issues of bias and discrimination in data mining.
Footnotes and Cross-References
I have revised the footnotes and cross-references to improve their accuracy and clarity. This includes correcting any inconsistencies and ensuring that all references are properly cited and linked to the relevant sections of the paper.
Once again, thank you for your detailed feedback. I am confident that the revisions made in response to your comments have strengthened the paper both in terms of structure and substantive content.
