Reviewer Chen stated that the ‘open letter’ would have benefitted from a more critical approach towards governance models of research consortia. Indeed, the EU-funded type of research consortia were the starting point of our analysis. These have a grant agreement and consortium agreement which must meet certain criteria and has narrowly defined or perhaps one should say, rigidly defined, responsibilities of the partners vis-à-vis each other and the funding agency as described in the Description of Action (DoA). There is no choice here if one wants to receive the funding. Other situations of research cooperation were out of scope.  We clarified that.
