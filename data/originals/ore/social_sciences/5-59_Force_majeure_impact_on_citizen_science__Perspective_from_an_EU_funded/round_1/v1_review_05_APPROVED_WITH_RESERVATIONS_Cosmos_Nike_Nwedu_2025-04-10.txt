Thank you for the opportunity to review the article entitled “Force majeure impact on citizen science: Perspective from an EU funded project”. The article addresses a significant topic of organisational and societal relevance by analysing challenges and solutions to conducting citizen science and research innovation activities during a pandemic. More specifically, it focuses on an EU-funded international project concerning GDPR compliance that occurred during the COVID-19 pandemic, which started in late 2019.
While the article is generally well-structured, clearly written, and offers some original insights, I have the following comments and/or suggestions for the authors.
1. The final line of your abstract below is muddled with the use of the word "rethought". Please paraphrase for more clarity.
“Overcoming challenges imposed under force majeure conditions, a citizen science project funded under the EU Horizon2020 research and innovation scheme SwafS15-2019, rethought experiential learning through volunteering screen time”.
2. There are different Sections and/or Subsections in the article that are currently unnumbered. I believe that appropriately numbering them will enhance the clarity of its structural design.
3. You made the following claim in the last paragraph of your “Introduction”:
“… we found that COVID-19 restrictions around the globe restricting citizens’ interactions with families, friends and outdoor activities, detracted from time to spend on volunteering computer work”.
How does the above claim imply that individuals who volunteer online cannot do so from their homes, assuming they have personal computers available? Please substantiate your claim with more explanation.
4. Although the quality of your language is generally satisfactory, there are a few instances where significant grammatical errors occurred and non-plain languages used. These must be addressed. For example, the first line/paragraph of the Section titled “
GDPR compliant project website design
” reads as follows:
“Some Internet access issues affected home working in the early days the project during … “.
The paragraph above tends to have a grammar issue that needs to be corrected. In other words, a preposition is missing there. More so, it is essential to replace the word “chime” in the first line of the last paragraph of the Section titled “
The emergence of COVID-19 in late 2019
” with a plain word. Please double-check your punctuation, grammar, and syntax throughout the article.
5. You should provide a brief explanation of force majeure, preferably following the first line of the introduction, and also show why COVID-19 serves as an exemplary case of force majeure.
6. Finally, the article, also in Section 2, includes the following quote in the second paragraph:
“Twenty countries, representing approximately 50% of the global population, account for over 80% of the estimated global excess mortality for the January 2020 to December 2021 period. These countries are Brazil, Colombia, Egypt, Germany, India, Indonesia, the Islamic Republic of Iran, Italy, Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Peru, the Philippines, Poland, the Russian Federation, South Africa, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, Turkey, Ukraine, and the United States of America (USA).”
I do not believe that Nigeria's inclusion among the countries representing 80% of global excess mortality due to COVID-19 is accurate. Firsthand experience suggests that Nigeria was one of the African nations that experienced significantly fewer deaths from the virus.
