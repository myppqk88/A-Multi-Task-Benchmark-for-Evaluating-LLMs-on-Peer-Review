Status: approved with reservations

============================================================

Overall, the paper presents an intriguing topic with the potential for engaging insights, yet falls short of delivering a comprehensive analysis. The introduction provides a broad overview but lacks specificity, with some content that could be better suited for footnotes. While the paper's premise holds promise, its execution disappoints upon closer examination.
One glaring issue is the ambiguity surrounding the selection method, described as coincidental, which raises concerns about the study's rigor. Methodological limitations are evident but not acknowledged, contributing to the lack of clarity in the methodology section. Key details, such as the number of interviews conducted and whether data stratification was employed, are conspicuously absent, undermining the study's credibility.
The revelation in the results section that only two video interviews were conducted is troubling, as this sample size is insufficient for drawing meaningful conclusions or facilitating comparisons. Additionally, the diverse backgrounds of the participants, hailing from Ukraine and the Republic of Congo, coupled with disparate motives for seeking asylum, further complicate any attempts at meaningful comparison.
Furthermore, there are no  clear links between the literature and the results, particularly regarding the application of theoretical concepts like social networks, ethnic, and gender networks. The research questions posed at the outset remain largely unanswered, leaving the reader with a sense of incompleteness. The absence of a conclusive section compounds this issue, denying the opportunity for a synthesized interpretation of findings.
Moreover, the decision to include fieldwork descriptions within the results section seems misplaced and could have been better integrated into the methodological session for a more coherent presentation.
In summary, while the paper's subject matter holds promise, its execution is not up to expected standards. Addressing these shortcomings through improved methodological transparency, clearer theoretical application, and a more cohesive structure would significantly enhance the paper's scholarly value.
