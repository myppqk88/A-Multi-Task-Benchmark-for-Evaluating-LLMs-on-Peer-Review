Author Response to Review 1 (v1)
Author: Siti Masyithoh
Date: 2025-12-31
============================================================

Dear Ms. Rasmitadila,
We sincerely thank the reviewers for their careful reading of our manuscript and for their insightful, constructive, and encouraging comments. We greatly appreciate the recognition of the strengths of this research, particularly regarding the clarity of the research gap, the coherence of the comparative framework, and the integration of global theoretical perspectives such as Universal Design for Learning (UDL), Individualized Education Programs (IEP/IPC), and Inclusive Pedagogy.
We have carefully addressed all suggestions made by the reviewers. Specifically, we have strengthened the manuscript in the following ways:
1. Critical Engagement with the Literature
We revised the final section of the introduction to provide a more explicit critique of existing studies. This includes clearer identification of unresolved gaps in previous research, particularly the limited number of cross-regional comparative studies between Southeast Asia and the Middle East and the lack of connections between global inclusive education frameworks and everyday implementation practices in non-Western contexts.
2. Methodological Transparency and Rigor
The Methods section has been substantially expanded to clarify the qualitative document analysis procedures. We now explicitly explain the inclusion and exclusion criteria for document selection, the strategies used to assess source credibility, and the analytical steps employed, including categorization, thematic comparison, and triangulation across policy documents, implementation reports, and academic literature. Furthermore, we have strengthened the academic basis for selecting the five analytical dimensions by directly linking them to the reviewed theory and previous empirical findings.
3. Clarity and Replicability
Revisions were also made to improve clarity, coherence, and transparency throughout the manuscript to support interpretive validity and facilitate replication by other researchers.
We believe that these revisions have significantly improved the robustness, clarity, and scientific contribution of the manuscript. We are grateful for the valuable guidance from the reviewers, who have helped refine the conceptual and methodological quality of this study.
Thank you for the opportunity to revise and resubmit our work. We hope this revised manuscript meets the journal's standards and look forward to your further consideration.
Sincerely,
Siti Masyithoh
(one of the authors)
