Article: Requirements for set-off in Japanese law
DOI: 10.12688/f1000research.161862.2
Version: 2
Reviewer: Rebecca Parry
Status: approved
Date: 2025-08-09
Report ID: 394786
============================================================

This article provides a systematic doctrinal discussion of set-off under Japanese law.  Since this is an area of law that attracts limited discussion internationally this will be an important account, covering key statutes and case law.  It will be helpful for anyone who is aiming to understand the operation of set-off both in insolvency and outside of it in Japan.
In terms of readability, an introduction should be added to explain to the reader the aim of the paper.  This can be used to highlight the important laws and what has influenced their development.  Also to highlight the contribution that this article makes to the literature.   I believe that it is the only recent source that will discuss set-off under Japanese law in English.
The article includes a good number of references.  It is good practice to indicate where texts are in the Japanese language and to give the Japanese titles.
The material would have been enhanced with a greater comparative element but is still valuable for the reasons already given.
