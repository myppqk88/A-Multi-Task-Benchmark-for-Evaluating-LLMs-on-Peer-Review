Article: Financial inclusion and stability in Ethiopia using bank-level data: A two-step system GMM estimation

Reviewer: Hicham Sadok
Status: approved with reservations
Date: 2025-02-11
Report ID: 362883
============================================================

The article examines the impact of financial inclusion on banking stability in the Ethiopian context. It addresses an important issue that deserves analysis and investigation. However, some elements deserve improvement:
1-To clarify in the introduction the two key variables of the study, namely what is meant by financial inclusion and banking stability: these two concepts are too broad and must be defined precisely.
2- From the conceptualization of the key variables, it would be more structuring in the literature review to focus on the work related to these two concepts in order to be able to ultimately deduce ideas or hypotheses to study in the empirical part: a segmented literature review like what has been done does not allow us to know what the usefulness of this step is for the future!!
3-It would be useful in the empirical part to detail a little more on the stylized facts of the banks studied.
4- At the level of dependent variables, namely, financial stability, the Z score is not sufficiently significant for stability, it rather designates profitability. However, if the latter is important for stability, it is not sufficient. Why not consider the ratios of the base as a criterion of stability instead of the Z ratio?
