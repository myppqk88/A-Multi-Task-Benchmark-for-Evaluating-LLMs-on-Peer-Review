Author Response to Review 3 (v1)
Author: Mohammed Arebo
Date: 2025-04-15
============================================================

Thank you for your thorough review and constructive feedback on our manuscript. Your insightful comments have significantly helped us improve the clarity, rigor, and overall quality of the study. We have carefully addressed each of your suggestions, as detailed below.
1.    The authors must add a specific policy recommendation in the abstract section.
Response: We appreciate your valuable comment. We added a specific policy recommendation to the abstract section.
2.    The study's novelty should be highlighted by comparing the current study with previous studies in the field.
Response: Thank you for your important comment. We highlighted the novelty of the study by comparing it with previous research and explicitly stating how our methodology and results address identified research gaps.
3.    The theoretical framework should be added.
Response: Thank you for your meticulous comment. We enriched the theoretical framework to provide a stronger foundation for the study.
4.    The literature review section should be enriched with the most recent studies.
Response: Thank you for this significant comment. We updated the literature review with the most recent studies and segmented it to focus on the key variables: (i) financial inclusion and banking stability, (ii) non-linear relationship between financial inclusion and bank stability, (iii) the moderating effects of macro-prudential regulation. This restructuring allows for clearer hypothesis formulation and empirical relevance.
5.    In the methodology section, justify the methods used in your study by comparing them with the existing field methods.
Response: Thank you for your valuable comment. We have justified the methods used in the study by comparing them with existing approaches (specifically with FE and RE) in the field. The methodology section, previously included in the results, has been repositioned to the Model Specification subsection under the Methods section. Additionally, we have introduced the non-linearity test and Lind and Mehlum’s (2010) U-test in this section to enhance methodological rigor. Lastly, we have provided a rationale for choosing the two-stage PCA method over alternatives, such as the entropy weighting and CFA method, for constructing the financial inclusion index.
6.    The graphical presentation of the methodology will be easier for the authors to understand.
Response: Thank you for your comment. A graphical representation of the methodology has been added to improve clarity and accessibility.
7.    In the results and discussion section, compare your results with the existing field studies.
Response: Thank you very much for this significant comment. The results and discussion section now includes comparisons with existing studies to contextualize our findings.
8.    The conclusion, policy implications, and limitations should be written under separate headings.
Response: Thank you for your significant comment. In line with the journal guidelines and other reviewer comments, the conclusion restructured into separate sections for policy implications, limitations, and future research directions, with the study’s contributions explicitly highlighted within the conclusion.
9.    An English language check is required to avoid grammatical and spelling errors.
Response: Thank you for your significant comment. A thorough English language check has been conducted to eliminate grammatical and spelling errors.
