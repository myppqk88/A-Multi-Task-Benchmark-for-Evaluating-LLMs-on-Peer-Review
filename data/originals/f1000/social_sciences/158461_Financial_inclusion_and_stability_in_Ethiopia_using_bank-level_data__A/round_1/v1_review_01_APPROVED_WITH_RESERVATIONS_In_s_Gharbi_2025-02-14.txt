Thank you for inviting me to review the current manuscript.
From my point of view, this is a very interesting topic and a good attempt. I advise the author(s) to continue. Some comments should be taken seriously. I hope that these comments will help to improve the document even more.
1. The abstract must contain the main contributions.
2.  I think the introduction should state the novelty of the paper compared to other papers published in the field. Author(s) should be more explicit about how their methodology or results fill identified research gaps to strengthen the justification for publication.
3.  The paper demonstrates an adequate understanding of the relevant literature in the field. However, I have seen that no hypotheses are mentioned, so it is better to mention the hypotheses and show the reader that you have tested them.
4. Please give reasons for the choice of methodologies used, why choose the two-stage PCA method rather than the entropy weighting method to construct the Financial Inclusion Index?
5. At the same time, I think that the conclusion part of the paper should be extended with contributions, limitations, and future research directions.
