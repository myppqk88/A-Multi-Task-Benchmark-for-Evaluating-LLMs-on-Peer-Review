Article: Regional Innovation System (RIS) as a means for development: Policies, opportunities and challenges in Ethiopia.

Version: 1
Reviewer: Elpida Samara
Status: approved with reservations
Date: 2025-02-11
Report ID: 360804
============================================================

The article requires substantial revisions to strengthen its overall structure, clarity, and coherence. These improvements are crucial for the manuscript to meet the indexing standards and increase its potential for successful acceptance. Specifically, the following areas need significant attention:
1.
Conceptual Clarification:
The focus of the paper should be clearly defined—whether it addresses regional innovation systems (RIS) or national innovation systems (NIS). The current overlap between these two perspectives creates conceptual ambiguity. Adding a section that explains the distinctions and linkages between the two systems is highly recommended.
Alternatively, if the focus is on the national level, the title and literature should reflect that appropriately.
To enhance the coherence and academic rigor of the study, I recommend the following adjustments:
1. Add a dedicated section that defines and differentiates the concepts of Regional Innovation Systems (RIS) and National Innovation Systems (NIS). This section should explain the distinct features, theoretical frameworks, and interconnections between these two systems.
2. Clarify how the regional perspective relates to the national system in Ethiopia, and explicitly state whether the focus is on regional innovation within the broader national framework or purely national-level innovation policies.
3. Alternatively, if the primary focus is on the national system, the title and literature should be adjusted to reflect that. The author should replace the current references with relevant literature on national innovation systems and national development strategies.
4. If the intention is to maintain the focus on the regional level, the analysis should shift to regional innovation dynamics and regional development frameworks, ensuring alignment with the core concept of Regional Innovation Systems.
2. Methodological Enhancement:
Incorporating a SWOT-TOWS analysis, as outlined in the article Samara, E., Kilintzis, P., Grammenou, A.
et al.
Towards Transformative Innovation Policy in Territorial Governance: Mapping the Relevance of RRI Elements.
J Knowledge Economy
(2024 [Ref-1]).
https://doi.org/10.1007/s13132-024-02320-1
, would provide a more structured and practical framework for developing improvement strategies. This methodology will help ensure that the recommendations are specific, actionable, and policy-oriented. Presenting the proposed strategies in a comprehensive table will also enhance the clarity and accessibility of the findings.
By incorporating TOWS analysis, the author could:
1. Align internal strengths with external opportunities to propose proactive strategies.
2. Mitigate internal weaknesses by leveraging external opportunities or reducing threats.
3. Ensure a policy-focused approach with clear, tangible outputs that are relevant to stakeholders and decision-makers.
3. Policy Strategy Integration:
The suggested strategies should be consolidated into a coherent section or table that highlights key priorities and links them to the identified challenges and opportunities. This will create a stronger, policy-relevant narrative that aligns with international best practices in innovation system analysis.
These revisions will significantly improve the article’s impact, making it more relevant for both academic and policy audiences. Careful attention to these recommendations will enhance the paper’s conceptual consistency, methodological rigor, and practical relevance, ensuring its readiness for indexing.
