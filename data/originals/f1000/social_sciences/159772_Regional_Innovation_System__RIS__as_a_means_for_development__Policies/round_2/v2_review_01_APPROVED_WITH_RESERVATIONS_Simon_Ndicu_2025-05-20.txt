============================================================

Thank you for the opportunity to read and review this thought provoking paper. A commend the authors for focusing on NIS especially from developing regions, where the concept of NIS has not been adequately investigated,
By and large the paper has adequate scope, in my reviewers report, I will concentrate on the areas of improvement.
The
introduction part
can be enriched further, after stating the objective of the papers, the authors, need to provide further justification on why the objectives are important why should we care about the study objectives. Secondly, the authors need to mention their unique contribution in this area of study. Lastly, towards the end of the introduction sections, it’s good to give us a glimpse of the paper organization, sections in the paper, methods, data and variables. So that by the time we are done reading introduction part we are able to grasp whole picture of the study.
Literature review
It seems the literature review has been divided into two parts
The
National Innovation System (NIS)
, and
Science, Technology, and Innovation Policies (STIP) in Emerging Economies
My observation is that the literature on NIS is very brief mainly focusing on the importance of NIS. I advise the authors to look at the composition of NIS.  The current literature is at quintuple helix (recent works of EG Carayannis). I expect the authors discuss how the five helix are interconnected in Ethiopia.  Secondly, the authors need to mention on the measurement of NIS productivity by discussing the inputs and outputs of NIS (literature by Global innovation index GII). Lastly Factors hindering effective functioning of NIS, ought to be mentioned here in general.
On the Science, Technology, and Innovation Policies (STIP) in Emerging Economies part
I expected the authors to discuss here regional and local  policies like ASTII ,  STISA 2014-24 and Agenda 2063, and describe how Ethiopia is catching and implementing this policies . I also expected to hear about Ethiopia STI policy implementation framework. Is there a commission STI, a national innovation agony or even a national research fund in Ethiopia?
Methods
I suggest a table showing the four categories of the documents received, and the objectives would have been clearer. The readers may want know which documents were reviewed per study objective.
Ethiopia, Kenya, and Rwanda Experience
I did not see any justification provided for comparing innovation in these three countries given the fact that they don’t belong to the income level!! . I advise the authors to compare economic peers or all countries in eastern Africa region including others like Uganda Tanzania Sudan etc. the authors can also compare Ethiopia with other lower income countries in sub-Saharan Africa.
Secondly, I advise the authors to use graphs to compare the countries, visual graphics,/maps are very effective in countries comparison.
Findings/in terms of opportunities and challenges
These finding I suggest they be presented in form of a table for clarity and focus – each opportunity or challenge be accompanied by the document reviewed.
