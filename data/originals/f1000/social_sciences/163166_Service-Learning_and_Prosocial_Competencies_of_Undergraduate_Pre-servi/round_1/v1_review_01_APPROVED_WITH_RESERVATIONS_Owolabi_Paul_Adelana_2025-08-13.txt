Abstract
This study is timely, particularly in addressing the gap between higher education and employability in its context, and in the context of globalization in general. I also commend the integration of service-learning as a pedagogical tool to enhance prosocial skills. Overall, the study contributes to teacher education discourse but requires refinement for clarity and coherence. For instance, the abstract needs to clearly articulate the theoretical framework guiding the study and a more concise presentation of key findings. The sampling strategy appears somewhat inconsistent (purposive random versus convenience). Overall, the abstract is good.
Introduction
The authors have presented a good and well-structured introduction, though a more succinct four-paragraph presentation would have been better. Overall, the introduction proposes service-learning as a practical solution aligned with national goals (this is good as also stated in the abstract). However, the introduction houses some lengthy sentences which if reworked, could give clearer articulation to the introduction. Also, the citation format needs to be consistent – for instance, in the second line of the fourth paragraph, a citation is written as  (
Mtawa, Fngwa, & Wilson-Strydom (2019)
, whereas the bracket before 2019 should have been a comma. Please, check all to ensure that they are well-presented. In addition, some long sentences might be rewritten to give conciseness to the introduction. An example of such sentence is:
The curriculum in some teacher education programs often does not align well with current educational demands and societal needs, resulting in teachers who may be knowledgeable in their subject areas but lack the interpersonal skills necessary to foster a supportive and inclusive classroom environment.
Paragraphs 3 and 4 of the introductions have given some kind of problem statement to the study. The standalone “Statement of the Problem” could be integrated into the main Introduction to give further standardized view to the Introduction.
Methods
The methods section is well-structured, presented and robust. This section demonstrates methodological rigour through a sequential explanatory mixed-methods design. The use of both quantitative and qualitative data strengthens the study’s validity and depth. Sampling strategies are clearly described, though, as I pointed out in the review of the abstract, the combination of purposive random and convenience sampling could be better justified. Instrument reliability is adequately reported (r = 0.79), and ethical procedures are commendably thorough. The six-month data collection period and detailed analysis techniques (paired t-test and thematic analysis) are appropriate for the research aims. The methods section is concise and clearly written.

Discussion
The discussion section effectively interprets the findings and connects them to existing literature, reinforcing the value of service-learning in enhancing prosocial competencies. It clearly articulates how improvements in communication, teamwork, empathy, cultural competence, and social responsibility contribute to employability. However, the section is repetitive in some places (example shown below) and could benefit from better synthesis of ideas. I also suggest a more critical reflection on limitations and broader implications for policy and curriculum reform. I hope this will help to enhance this section’s scholarly depth presentation.
Examples of Repetition
Communication Skills
“The significant improvement in communication skills among undergraduate pre-service teachers due to the service-learning program highlights the effectiveness of this pedagogical approach.”
“The significant improvement in communication skills observed in this study suggests that pre-service teachers who participate in service-learning programs are more likely to meet the communication demands of their future roles…”
I suggest that these two sentences repeating the same idea about communication skill should be merged. They could also be streamlined.
Recommendation
I suggest that this section written in narrative prose format.
Conclusion
Th conclusions the authors presented are well-supported by the results of this study. The conclusion needs to acknowledge the limited statistical significance in empathy gains and potential limitations in result generalization to strengthen the credibility and scholarship of the conclusion.
