Revised Amendments from Version 2
This version of the article includes substantial revisions in response to peer review feedback. The literature review and results sections were restructured to classify the broad categories of “financial knowledge” and “financial behavior” into more specific subtopics—such as budgeting, student debt, fintech adoption, and retirement planning—thereby allowing for a clearer identification of underexplored themes. The theoretical framework was strengthened with a more comprehensive discussion of financial literacy, and additional references were incorporated. We also clarified the definition of the target population in the introduction and corrected conceptual ambiguities related to the use of overlapping terms such as financial literacy and financial knowledge. Methodologically, we reorganized the structure of the Methods section for better readability and added a separate appendix listing the 44 studies included in the systematic review. The abstract and discussion sections were revised to reflect these changes and to highlight the practical implications of our findings for educational stakeholders and policymakers.
