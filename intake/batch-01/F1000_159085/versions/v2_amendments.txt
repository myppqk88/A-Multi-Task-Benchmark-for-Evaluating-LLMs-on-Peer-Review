Revised Amendments from Version 1
This revised version of the article incorporates several key revisions based on reviewer feedback. First, it clarifies the timeframe used for the literature search (2003-2023) and acknowledges the methodological limitations related to search terms and scope, which are now explicitly stated in the limitations section. Additionally, a discussion on the evolution of financial literacy into financial capability and financial well-being has been added, along with a justification for maintaining the focus on financial literacy. Relevant references, including studies from the APLUS project at the University of Arizona, are now acknowledged as potential gaps due to search constraints. Moreover, Figure 3 has been adjusted to ensure clarity regarding the study’s timeframe. Finally, spelling and grammatical errors have been corrected, including the removal of unnecessary hyphens. These changes enhance the clarity, scope, and rigor of the article while maintaining the integrity of the original research.
