REVIEW REPORT ON THE PAPER: “UNENDING DIALECTICAL POLITICS OF IDENTITY IN ETHIOPIA”
Article’s Summary:
No doubt, the article explores the persistent and evolving nature of identity politics in Ethiopia, emphasizing the dialectical tensions between ethnicity, state formation, and democratic governance. Drawing from historical, political, and philosophical perspectives, it examines how the Ethiopian People’s Revolutionary Democratic Front (EPRDF)’s ethnic federalism institutionalized identity as the primary mode of political representation and contestation.
The paper clearly argues that while the 1995 Constitution aimed to empower historically marginalized ethnic groups through self-determination, the federal arrangement inadvertently deepened divisions, incentivized ethno-nationalist mobilization, and delegitimized pan-Ethiopian unity. It also analyzes the post-2018 reformist momentum under the current leadership of Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, which challenged the hegemony of ethnic politics but also led to political backlash, inter-ethnic violence, and renewed calls for autonomy or secession by regional actors.
Thus, the article adopts a dialectical theoretical scholarship or lens, perhaps, mainly influenced by Hegelian-Marxist thought, to frame identity politics as a recursive, contradictory process where state attempts at resolution give rise to new contradictions. It concludes that Ethiopia’s political trajectory is trapped in an unending dialectic of fragmentation and unification, with neither a return to centralized nationalism nor a stable ethnic federalism providing sustainable solutions.
Paper Evaluation Based on Certain Criteria
1. Is the research question clearly stated and significant?
Partly.
Strength
The paper and/or article touches on a timely and important topic with profound implications for peace, state-building, and democratic governance in Ethiopia and Africa more broadly.
Limitation
Despite the obvious strength of the paper, it is not without limitation.
The research question is not clearly articulated as a focused inquiry. Theoretical ambition and narrative breadth may overshadow a clear hypothesis or guiding question.
Recommendation
The author should reframe the research question explicitly at the beginning. For example:
“How has Ethiopia’s ethnic federalism contributed to the persistence of identity-based political conflicts, and what dialectical tensions does this reveal in state-ethnicity relations?”
2. Is the theoretical framework appropriate and clearly applied?
Partly.
Strength
The use of dialectical theory offers an innovative lens for interpreting Ethiopia’s complex political dynamics.
Limitation
The theoretical underpinnings are vaguely referenced and insufficiently operationalized. The dialectic is more metaphorical than analytically rigorous.
Recommendation
The author needs to clarify the dialectical framework whether, as used in the context of the paper, it is derived from Hegelian, Marxist, or postcolonial dialectics. Also, effort should be made in defining the triadic movement (thesis-antithesis-synthesis) and show how it maps onto historical or contemporary events in Ethiopia. Incorporate political theorists like Mahmood Mamdani or Benedict Anderson to enrich the conceptual foundation.
3. Is the methodology sound and clearly described?
No.
Limitation
The article lacks methodological transparency. It is unclear whether it is based on primary data, discourse analysis, historical interpretation, or case studies.
Recommendation
Specify the method vis-à-vis:
If using qualitative analysis, describe data sources (e.g., government documents, speeches, interviews).
If historical analysis, clarify the temporal scope and sources consulted.
If discourse analysis, outline the corpus and coding approach.
This section is crucial for scientific rigor.
4. Is the analysis compelling and well-supported by evidence?
Partly.
Strength
The paper offers clearer interpretations of Ethiopia’s political history, especially in linking identity politics with constitutional arrangements.
Limitation
There is insufficient engagement with empirical evidence to substantiate claims. The article is more interpretive than analytic.
Recommendation:
Provide concrete examples (e.g., Tigray conflict, Oromia protests, Amhara claims) with references to policy documents, statistics, or fieldwork. Ground claims in data to avoid sweeping generalizations.
5. Does the article contribute new knowledge or perspectives?
Obviously, yes.
Strength
The dialectical framing of Ethiopia’s identity politics is intellectually original and offers an alternative to more static ethnic conflict models.
Recommendation
The originality of the conceptual approach is commendable. However, its contribution would be clearer if the theoretical novelty were better structured and substantiated with empirical grounding.
6. Is the writing clear, coherent, and well-organized?
Partly.
Strength
The prose is conceptually ambitious and intellectually rich.
Limitation
It occasionally veers into dense abstraction without sufficient explanation for non-specialist readers.
Recommendation:
Authors should do well by defining key terms early – terms or concepts like
dialectics, ethnic federalism, identity politics
. Besides, use subheadings to improve structure, and also reduce jargon and overly philosophical passages unless directly tied to the argument.
Constructive Suggestions and Must-Address Points
To make the paper scientifically sound, the following must be urgently addressed by the authors:
Clarify the research question and link it directly to the theoretical and empirical inquiry.
Define and apply the dialectical framework rigorously, not just as a metaphor but as an analytic tool.
Specify the methodology and provide transparency about sources and analytical procedures.
Include more empirical data or case illustrations to support arguments.
Improve structure and accessibility, including clearer transitions, key term definitions, and logical flow of ideas.
CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATION:
The paper presents an important and thought-provoking argument with real potential to advance debates on identity politics in Africa. However, to meet the standards of scientific rigor and clarity, the author must strengthen the theoretical framework, methodological transparency, and empirical grounding. Nonetheless, on the recommendation, major revisions required!
