Revised Amendments from Version 1
The revised version of the article incorporates important changes as response to comments and observation of the reviewers of the article and some minor changes are also made to the affiliation of the author. Some of the major changes are outlined hereunder.
Some concepts regarding dialectical nature of identity politics are added to the introduction. This to indicate the dialectical nature of identity politics and to enhance the theoretical base of the contested conceptualization of identity politics (universalism and particularism for instance) and to show how lack of complementarity between them leads to violent conflict which is manifested in Ethiopian history.
Two additional topics are also included in the revised article as response to the comments of the reviewer. One topic is “A Brief History of Ethiopia” which indicates the historical root of the identity based contestation in Ethiopia and the scholarly debates therein.  The second topic is “Theoretical framework” which aimed to indicate the theoretical bases of contestation that characterizes identity politics and the application of dialectical approach to show the continuous failure in Ethiopia to deal with clash of nationalism.
As per the comments of all reviewers, a detail of empirical evidences are also added in the discussion part of the article. This is particularly important to show the practical identity based political contestations among ethnonational groups in Ethiopia. The cases presented also shows how the contestations led to violent conflict between ethnonational groups and government in one hand and among ethnonational groups in the other hand.
Minor changes are also made to the affiliation of the author.
