Revised Amendments from Version 2
This revised version of the article incorporates substantial conceptual, structural, and analytical refinements in response to peer review. The Introduction and Literature Review were restructured to clarify the theoretical relationship between resilience and motivation, particularly within correctional contexts, and to reduce generic or overly broad theorizing. The revised text now explicitly frames resilience and motivation as interdependent, context-sensitive processes shaped by institutional, social, and cultural conditions.
The methodological sections were streamlined for clarity and transparency, including revision of the inclusion and exclusion criteria to remove redundancy and improve justification of language and temporal restrictions. Definitions and analytical distinctions — such as between author keywords and index keywords — were added to enhance conceptual precision.
The Results and Discussion sections were extensively revised to strengthen interpretation and critical appraisal. Descriptive reporting was replaced or supplemented with analytical discussion linking bibliometric patterns to theoretical, institutional, and epistemic implications. Greater attention was given to structural asymmetries in knowledge production, including the dominance of Western institutions and the under representation of research from the Global South.
Several elements were removed or reframed to improve coherence, including the deletion of out-of-context material and the refinement of gap statements. The Conclusions were rewritten to articulate more clearly the study’s theoretical, methodological, and policy contributions, and to avoid generic claims.
Overall, this version places greater emphasis on conceptual integration, contextual sensitivity, and critical reflection, while preserving the empirical foundation and bibliometric scope of the original study.
