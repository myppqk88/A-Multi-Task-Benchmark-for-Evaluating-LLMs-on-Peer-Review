We would like to thank the reviewer for their valuable observations, which have allowed us to strengthen the methodological transparency of our study.
Regarding the first comment, we understand the concern about the justification of the quality assessment method applied to the selected studies. We clarify that the PRISMA flow diagram was used strictly to document and guide the selection process of studies, but not as the instrument for assessing methodological quality. To evaluate the risk of bias and overall methodological rigor of each included article, we employed the AMSTAR-2 framework, a widely recognized tool designed specifically for the critical appraisal of systematic reviews. As shown in Table 1, this assessment included dimensions such as the justification of inclusion criteria, the transparency and scope of the search strategy, the identification of potential sources of bias, and the evaluation of heterogeneity and publication bias. Each article was independently rated across these dimensions to provide a comprehensive quality profile.
In response to the second comment, we confirm that a bias assessment method was indeed applied, using the AMSTAR-2 criteria to enable a structured and replicable approach to the evaluation of risk of bias. This allowed us to classify the methodological quality of the 63 studies and to identify both the strengths and limitations of the current literature on mobile payment adoption. We therefore consider that the study meets the minimum criteria required to be framed as a Living Systematic Review (LSR), as it includes a robust and transparent quality appraisal protocol that can be updated and expanded over time.
We appreciate the opportunity to clarify these aspects and have revised the manuscript to make this information more explicit.
