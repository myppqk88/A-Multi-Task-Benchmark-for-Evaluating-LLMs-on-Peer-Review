Revised Amendments from Version 1
This revised version of the article addresses several important points raised during the review process to enhance methodological clarity, scientific rigor, and analytical transparency. First, the exact dates of the database searches in Scopus and Web of Science were added to ensure consistency and reproducibility of the bibliometric analysis. Second, the introduction section was thoroughly revised with the inclusion of updated scholarly references to support key claims and reinforce the scientific foundation of the paper. Third, a clearer mapping between the analytical tools (Microsoft Excel, VOSviewer, and CiteSpace) and their respective functions was provided, alongside specific clarifications about the analyses depicted in Figures 7 and 8. Fourth, the PRISMA flow diagram was improved with the addition of explanatory notes detailing the criteria for "report not retrievable" and "record excluded," contributing to greater transparency in the selection process. Finally, two real-world examples of successful mobile payment systems were incorporated into the discussion section to enrich the theoretical reflections with practical insights. These updates significantly strengthen the overall quality and academic robustness of the manuscript.
