Response to Reviewer
2
Comments
We sincerely thank the reviewer for the constructive and insightful comments on our manuscript. We have carefully considered each point and revised the manuscript accordingly. Below is a point-by-point response to the comments:
Reviewer Comment 1:
Is the work clearly and accurately presented and does it cite the current literature?
Response:
Partly.
We have added a clear statement of the research gap and the study’s contribution at the end of the literature review to enhance clarity.
Redundant citations have been reduced, and transitions between paragraphs have been improved for better flow.
Studies are now synthesized thematically (hierarchical, functional, and matrix structures) rather than listed individually.
Reviewer Comment 2:
Is the study design appropriate and is the work technically sound?
Response:
Mostly yes.
We have included the sampling rationale and clearly defined inclusion/exclusion criteria.
Demographic characteristics of respondents have been presented in a new table for transparency.
Reliability coefficients (Cronbach’s alpha) for each construct have been added.
The discussion now integrates qualitative and quantitative results more cohesively, highlighting how each informs the other.
Reviewer Comment 3:
Are sufficient details of methods and analysis provided to allow replication by others?
Response:
Partly.
A table summarizing constructs, items, and measurement sources has been included.
A new subsection “Data Analysis Procedures” distinguishes quantitative (SPSS, regression, correlation) and qualitative (NVivo, thematic analysis) steps.
Ethical clearance details have been retained succinctly with key approval numbers and reduced repetition.
Reviewer Comment 4:
Is the statistical analysis and its interpretation appropriate?
Response:
Largely appropriate.
The explanation of R² and F-statistics has been expanded to interpret effect size and model strength.
A summary table for reliability, validity, and discriminant validity has been added.
All statistical reporting now follows APA/F1000 standards consistently (e.g., r = .53, p < .01).
Reviewer Comment 5:
Are the conclusions supported by the results?
Response:
Yes.
A new subsection, “Implications for Policy and Practice,” has been added to highlight actionable insights.
Limitations (e.g., small sample size, cross-sectional design) are now discussed, with suggestions for future research.
The manuscript ends with a concise summary paragraph reinforcing the main contribution.
Reviewer Comment 6:
Is the language and style appropriate?
Response:
The manuscript has been edited for brevity and clarity, particularly in the literature review and methodology sections.
Tables have been simplified, retaining only essential figures; detailed statistics have been moved to supplementary materials.
Tense usage has been standardized (past tense for results, present tense for discussion).
Reviewer Comment 7:
Overall Recommendation
Response:
We appreciate the reviewer’s acknowledgment of the relevance and strength of our study. All suggested revisions have been incorporated to improve clarity, methodological transparency, and analytical depth.
We believe these revisions have significantly strengthened the manuscript, and we thank the reviewer again for their valuable feedback.
