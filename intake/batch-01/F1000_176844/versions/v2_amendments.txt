Revised Amendments from Version 1
In the second revision, I address several of the specific inquiries made by Reviewers 1 and 2. For reviewer 1, these include the utilization of primary texts to defend several of the claims made in the study, clarifying the significance of the Laos-Vietnam bilateral relations (historically shaped), justifying how the case study of Cambodia is relevant in assessing Laos’ chairmanships in ASEAN, as well as explaining how significant diverging views are within the context of Laos. For Reviewer 2, the focus of the revisions is on the significance of the ASEAN Outlook on the Indo-Pacific, clarifying the discourses around Laos’ debt-to-GDP ratio, and the role of state-society relations within the context of undemocratic settings like Laos. The revisions undertaken help to build up the narrative that Laos’ ASEAN chairmanship’s neutrality can be deciphered by looking at a number of systemic variables (externally) and dynamics within (internally), which shape the unique responses shown in Laos’ foreign policies.
