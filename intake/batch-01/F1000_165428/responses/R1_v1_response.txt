We thank the reviewer for these insightful suggestions. We implemented the following targeted revisions responding solely to this review:
Introduction:
The Introduction was revised and condensed to remove repeated ideas and maintain a sharper focus on the central research question. Recent international studies (2022–2024) on academic leveling and gamification in STEM education were incorporated to update the theoretical framework and reinforce the global relevance of the study.
Methodology:
A new Statistical Approach and Justification section was created to clarify the rationale behind the selection of non-parametric tests and their alignment with the research questions. The total number of participants and the size of each subgroup (reinforcement course, gamified kit, and combined strategies) were reported to increase methodological transparency.
Results:
The discussion of p-values (Tables 6–7 and 9) was expanded to provide a clearer interpretation of statistical significance. In Table 10, Cohen’s d was added to report the effect size and strengthen the interpretation of the results.
Conclusions
: The effect of the gamified kit was described with greater nuance, emphasizing its smaller magnitude relative to the reinforcement course. The claim about scalability of the strategies was reframed as a recommendation for future research, rather than a generalizable conclusion.
