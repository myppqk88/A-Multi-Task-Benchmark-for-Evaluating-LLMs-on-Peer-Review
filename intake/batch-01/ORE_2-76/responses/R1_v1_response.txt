          Thank you for your thoughtful and constructive review. In line with your comments, we
          have made the following changes, largely for the sake of clarity:

          1. Clarify the three main research questions, and 2. Clarify the meaning of key terms -
          ‘prevention’, ‘health improvement’ and ‘health-in-all policies’ – and how they relate to each
          other. We reframed these questions (in the introduction) to provide a clearer way to distinguish
          between key terms.

          3. Explain that ambiguity relates to unclear problem definition, which resurfaces as a
          problem when policy actors seek specific solutions. We added a paragraph to this section to
          describe ambiguity in relation to problem definition and solution proposing.

          4. Clarify that our explanations may relate differently to different examples: some are
          primarily about joining-up policymaking, while others are about power. We made this point
          much more clearly in the capacity section, separating the Sure Start and industry examples.

          5. Consider if the COVID-19 experience is really ironic (isn’t a conflict between protection
          and improvement inevitable rather than unexpected?) and if our points are generalizable.
          We remove reference to irony. We use the WHO 2020 report to highlight a general shift from
          improvement to protection (while qualifying the language to describe ‘many countries’).

          6. Consider if the tension in governance, that we describe, is a general feature of
          policymaking or if it plays out in distinctive ways in public health. We clarify (albeit briefly)
          that we are describing a general effect that is discussed frequently in HiAP reports.
