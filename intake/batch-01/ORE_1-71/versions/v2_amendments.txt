      Amendments from Version 1
The new version of the article contains the following updates:
- Our claims about the generalisability of the framework and the
strength of claims regarding our results have been reduced. We
have also highlighted that our manuscript focuses on measuring
the performance of only one programme – the DMS Accelerator.
- More references have been added to support our claims,
particularly in the Introduction section.
- We have provided more examples of open innovation, and
have justified our decision to describe DMS and other similar
initiatives as examples of open innovation, rather than relying
on traditional notions of acceleration and incubation. We have
also clarified that the scope of our paper focuses on initiatives
that serve startups and SMEs, which are only some of the various
possible stakeholders that can be targeted by open innovation
initiatives.
- We have obtained additional data, which has increased our
survey sample size from 20 to 47 respondents across the three
DMS cohorts. We have also reported the new results using actual
numbers (rather than percentages) for transparency.
- We have now added a section on the “Limitations and future
work”.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
the end of the article
