Amendments from Version 1

In response to the valuable feedback received from the peer
review, the revised manuscript has undergone several key
enhancements to improve clarity, strengthen theoretical
integration, and deepen empirical analysis. The revised
introduction features numerical evidence to substantiate
the scale of post-accession Polish migration to the UK. It
also provides clearer empirical support for claims regarding
Polish nationals’ vulnerability to unequal labour practices,
and acknowledges the presence of Polish migrants prior to
2004 while precisely defining the “post-accession” period.
The theoretical framework has been elaborated, whereas the
methodology section offers a clearer rationale for selecting
specific employment sectors. The paper’s structure has been
reorganised for distinct separation between theoretical
exposition and empirical results.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
the end of the article
