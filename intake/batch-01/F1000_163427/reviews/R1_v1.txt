Thank you for submitting your manuscript. The topic is compelling, and the data presented is quite interesting. However, I have identified a few areas that need attention for the next revision. Please consider the feedback I’ve provided to strengthen your work.
#Comments to authors
Title:
The title could be refined further to make it more engaging and appealing to readers.
Abstract:
The abstract should be rewritten to include a more substantial emphasis on the research findings.
Introduction:
- Currently, this section only covers the research background and lacks a discussion of similar studies, which is crucial for establishing the novelty of this work.
- The research problem and objectives are not clearly articulated.
- A revision is needed to improve the logical flow and coherence of this section.
Methods:
- Please provide a clear explanation of the variables and parameters used in the study.
- Data analysis procedures should be added to ensure the research is reproducible.
Findings:
- Many maps require quantitative symbology, but this is not evident in Figures 2–4.
- More interpretation is needed to support and clarify the results.
Discussion and Conclusion:
- The discussion is too brief—it should thoroughly analyze the findings and directly address the research objectives.
