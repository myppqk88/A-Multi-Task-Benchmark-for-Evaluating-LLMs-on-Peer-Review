       Jaume Teodoro

       Dear Reviewer,
       First, I would like to thank you for your contribution to the review process of my article,
       resulting from the Erasmus+ EICAA project. Thank you for the conciseness and detail in your
       explanation. I agree with you that an inductive approach could have revealed alternative
       competency categorizations beyond those of the EntreComp Framework. Personally, I must
       say that there is sufficient evidence in the academic literature to suggest alternative forms
       that may be equally or more suitable. Particularly in the field of entrepreneurship education
       at universities, my research experience suggests other possibilities. However, since the
       objective of this work was to update and expand EntreComp, rather than develop a
       completely new framework, we deliberately chose to apply a deductive coding strategy
       aligned with the existing EntreComp categories. In the revised version of the manuscript, I
       will better explain this decision based on your comments. In the new text, I will add it to the
       discussion in the limitations section, reiterating that this deductive approach, while
       ensuring comparability with the original framework, could have limited the emergence of
       alternative categorical systems (such as personal, social, or domain competencies) or even
       masked certain competencies. In my view, and based on what has already been discussed, a
       more "design process" approach would have suggested other competency relationships
       and a more focused approach to practices such as challenge-based learning (CBL). I will do
       so. I thank the reviewer for highlighting this point, which helps clarify the methodological
       scope and limits of the study, and I remain at your disposal for any further assistance.
       Thank you very much. Jaume Teodoro.
