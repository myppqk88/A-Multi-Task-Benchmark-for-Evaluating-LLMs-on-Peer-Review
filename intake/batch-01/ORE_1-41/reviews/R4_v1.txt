       A very good, brief study report showing a method of bottom-up work within a multi-actor group of
       local stakeholders to obtain information about regional challenges, opportunities and long-term
       vision in a systematic and structured way, which can be used in the preparation of a broader
       framework for public policy programming.

       The results described justify and support the different research techniques: interviews, a focus
       group, and an online questionnaire used in practice, and allow the formulation of valuable
       conclusions. The study as a whole, together with the references to broader sources that provide
       more information on the research techniques of the study, can be regarded as a good reference
       for studies of a similar nature. This constitutes the universal value of the material presented.
