       Dear Authors,

       Many thanks for the opportunity to review your work.

       The reason I ticked “partly” in response to “Is the study design appropriate and is the work
       technically sound?” stems from your plain language summary approach, which has led to a paper
       that sounds quite opinionated and lacking references for strong statements e.g. page 3, second
       column, top paragraph; page 4, first column, bottom paragraph; page 10, first column, third
       paragraph.

       Please note the typo on page 4 in reference to Oliver & Cairney. I would recommend that your
       argument would benefit from the inclusion of Topp et al., 20181.

       On page 9, I recommend rather than referring to neutral, you refer to independence in relation to
       the role of EU agencies.

       Page 13 - I think your policy recommendations would benefit from the inclusion of the JRC’s work
       on competence frameworks for policymakers and researchers:
       https://knowledge4policy.ec.europa.eu/projects-activities/competence-frameworks-policymakers-
       researchers_en

       Some reservation about the lack of a caveat justifying the overall general conclusions that stem
       from considerably narrower fields.

       I hope this is helpful.

       References
