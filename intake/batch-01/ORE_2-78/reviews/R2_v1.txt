       Dear Authors,

       Congratulations for your work. It defines in a very good way what are the main issues to be
       considered in the field of evidence-based policymaking and in the complex relationship between
       policy and science.

       There is an urgent need after COVID-19 pandemic to allow a balanced and always connected link
       between these two actors, and this should be well understood at a general level in Europe and
       worldwide.

       The issue of trust is then crucial and a sufficient level of it from the public can be achieved only in a
       win-win situation for the two, policymakers and scientists.

       I have just a pair of comments to improve the article.
           ○  The first one is a better defined structure in shorter subparagraphs, especially in the results.
              This could facilitate the reading of a long article like yours and the citations in future works.

           ○   The second one is the inclusion of the issues related to the cultural peculiarities of a
               country, which could deeply affect the policy-science dialogue. There are mentions of it, but
               I think a general discussion is missing and could be improved.

       Is the work clearly and accurately presented and does it engage with the current literature?
