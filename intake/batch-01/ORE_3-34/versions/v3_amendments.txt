Amendments from Version 2

Version 3 of the paper has been amended to directly address
some of the very helpful reviewers’ comments to Version 2, for
which we are very grateful. The key changes included clarifying
the this dual study aims, with the general objective of assessing
the feasibility of using experimental data in agent-based models,
and the specific objective related to studying the impact of
immersion in the context of risky migration decisions. We have
also described the game design, clarified the experimental setup
and the process of recruiting participants, and added a summary
of the collected demographic information. In this version, we
also signpost to the follow-up study, which has since been
published (Modirrousta-Galian et al., 2024, Simulation & Gaming,
55(4): 716–735). The details of the revisions made are listed in
our responses to the individual reviews.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
the end of the article
