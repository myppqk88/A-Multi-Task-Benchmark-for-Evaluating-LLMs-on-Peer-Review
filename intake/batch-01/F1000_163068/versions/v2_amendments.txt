Revised Amendments from Version 1
This revised version of the protocol has been updated to improve clarity, methodological transparency, and alignment with realist review standards following peer review and editorial guidance. The overall aims, research questions, and design of the study remain unchanged, but several sections have been strengthened to enhance readability and rigour. The Background section has been expanded to provide clearer conceptual positioning of student–staff partnership (SSP). Definitions of SSP and related terms have been clarified, with greater attention to the diversity of partnership practices, including those involving professional staff and non-academic contexts. Recent literature on power, equity, representation, and partnership authenticity has been more fully integrated to reflect contemporary scholarship. The Methods section has been revised to provide more explicit detail on the realist approach. We have clarified the use of Context–Mechanism–Outcome Configurations (CMOCs), the distinction between outcomes for direct participants and indirect beneficiaries, and the planned development and refinement of programme theories. The search strategy, inclusion criteria, and data extraction processes have been described in greater depth, including the addition of an assessment of partnership authenticity during full-text screening. Alignment with RAMESES and PRISMA guidance has been made more explicit. Descriptions of advisory group involvement have been refined to clarify their complementary roles in theory development. The Irish policy context has been relocated to a dedicated section to distinguish the international scope of the review from the later UCD evaluation. Terminology has been standardised throughout, minor structural changes made, and referencing and typographical issues corrected. These revisions aim to make the protocol more transparent, accessible, and useful for readers undertaking realist inquiries into SSP in higher education
