Revised Amendments from Version 1
This revised version incorporates extensive clarifications, structural improvements, and additional context in response to the reviewers’ suggestions. The abstract was refined to specify that although OSF account creation increased substantially, no new registrations, datasets, or preprints were added during the 12-month follow-up. We also reinforced the lack of formal questionnaire validation and provided an open link to the full instrument on OSF.
The introduction and discussion were expanded to include a broader literature base on educational interventions related to open science, research ethics, and integrity across disciplines and countries. Terminology was harmonized to consistently define the target population as graduate students (Master’s and PhD), and Figure 1 was corrected accordingly.
The limitations section was strengthened to explicitly address the absence of a control group, the short follow-up period, participant heterogeneity, and potential self-selection bias. We added remarks about the limitations of using OSF alone to measure open science engagement and the need for broader, multi-platform behavioral tracking.
The discussion was restructured into subsections for clarity, and a new conclusion was added summarizing implications for future research and training. We now emphasize that educational initiatives alone are insufficient for sustained behavioral change and must be complemented by institutional and structural incentives.
Collectively, these revisions have improved the clarity, transparency, and overall rigor of the manuscript.
