       This work raises an important issue for research with vulnerable people, where it is difficult to
       recruit participants. The scientific community needs key elements to work in this field, and this
       essay provides important findings.

       In a "Responsible Research approach" it is essential to incorporate participatory processes
       throughout all stages of research, and this is especially relevant with vulnerable populations.
       Considering what is reflected in the section “Experiences with participant recruitment resources
       and strategies”, it does not seem that the different source groups have been involved in the
       investigation from the outset, being part of the design of the recruitment strategies themselves. It
       does not seem appropriate for the authors to claim that it was obvious that some strategies would
       not work, because if they did, they should not have been incorporated. These issues should be
       part of Discussion, as they may be part of the reasons for the poor success of several of the
       strategies. Nor does it seem prudent to make judgments such as “This kind of feedback raised our
       concerns because we consider these statements as stereotyping refugee adolescents, and
       therewith as unethical and unprofessional, especially when expressed from those who are in
       direct contact with the refugee community”, given that no research has been carried out on these
       issues.

       On the other hand, the authors should also provide more details on what the "Participatory Data
       Collection Strategy" consisted of, since it seems to have produced the best results.
       Some further reading is recommended, which may be particularly relevant in the context of this
       essay:
