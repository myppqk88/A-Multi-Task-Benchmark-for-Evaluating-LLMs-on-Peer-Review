      The authors provide an important example of how standard methods for recruiting participants
      into clinical research can be ineffective in vulnerable populations.

      The key finding was that trust was a critical factor in recruiting their target population, whom they
      labeled as vulnerable. However, the authors do not explore an extensive trust literature with
      vulnerable populations (citations added). Nor do they fully explain the vulnerable nature of the
      adolescent population. "The power of trustworthy people" in research is an important discussion
      point that should be explored more in the context of future clinical research approaches.

      There are several ethical considerations involving coercion of this population: 1. Utilizing peer
      pressure and 2. The value of gift cards to this population. These are reasonable approaches, but
      considering this manuscript is specifically about vulnerable populations, some explanation of
      these how these things were managed is appropriate.
