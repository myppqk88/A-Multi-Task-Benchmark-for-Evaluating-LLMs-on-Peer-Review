The study demonstrates that AI-ML models can predict bankruptcy in India's trade service sector with Random Forest performing best but critical flaws must be fixed:
1. charity whether SMOTE was applied before/after train-test split to rule out data leakage,
2. address suspiciously high accuracy rates through external validation or cross validation to prove results are not overfitted and
3. provide all model hyperparameters and implantation details for replicability.
