REVISED Amendments from Version 1
The article’s second version takes into account the review comments made in the open peer review process by Dick Helbing
and Reginaldo Gonçalves Gomes, whom I thank for their constructive engagement with the paper.
Version 2 features a number of changes:
* Typos and formal errors have been corrected.
* A total of 25 additional references were added to the bibliography and discussed in the main body of the article, which
reflects the two reviewers’ comments on how the argumentation could be further improved and grounded in additional
academic literature.
* The paper’s interdisciplinary character has been strengthened by grounding it in more literature from the fields of
philosophy and computational social science.
* In the introduction, the context of the paper was better explained.
* The engagement with the notions of authoritarianism, fascism, (digital) democracy, fake news/disinformation, factchecking organisations, and AI has been, as suggested by the reviewers, deepened.
As a consequence of the revisions, version 2 is now a somewhat longer paper than version 1, and I recommend that readers
read and cite version 2 rather than version 1.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at the end of the article
