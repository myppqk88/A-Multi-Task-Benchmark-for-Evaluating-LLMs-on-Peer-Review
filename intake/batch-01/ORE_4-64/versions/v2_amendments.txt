Amendments from Version 1

The revised paper addresses all of the reviewers’ comments and
incorporates the authors’ suggestions. The new version of the
manuscript is a much more coherent and consistent document
that focuses on the challenges of the fifth-generation university.
The essay is now much more focused, orienting it more towards
the peculiarities of universities. Furthermore, the business
model innovation literature has been used to demonstrate how
unicanvas can assist universities in responding to challenges and
trends. Consequently, the limitations of traditional instruments
used for strategic planning in universities have been highlighted
in relation to unicanvas. At the same time, it has been proposed
that unicanvas should be combined with other strategic tools,
as they also present certain limitations, which have been better
supported in the literature. The antecedents that review previous
research focused on the application of Canvas in the context
of universities have been improved in two ways. Firstly, new
references have been incorporated. Secondly, the usefulness
of this tool has been highlighted. We have incorporated a new
image of unicanvas that dynamically reflects its design and
the relationships between blocks in a numerical way. We have
also substantially improved some blocks, such as beneficiaries,
which responds to the debate in the literature about the role
of students, and revenues and achievements. As a result, the
conclusions of the document are much more mature and
complete.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
the end of the article
