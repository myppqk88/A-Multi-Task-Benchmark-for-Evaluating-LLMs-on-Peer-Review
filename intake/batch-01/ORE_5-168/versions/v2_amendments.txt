REVISED Amendments from Version 1
     
This revised version incorporates substantial clarifications and
expansions in response to reviewer feedback. The abstract has
been strengthened to better articulate the paper’s theoretical
contribution on digitalisation, transparency, and business model
innovation in energy communities. The methodology section now
provides clearer justification of the mixed-methods approach,
detailing the link between stakeholder engagement, validation
cycles, and data reproducibility. Section 4 has been reorganised
to distinguish the overarching business model and platform
architecture before the pilot-specific analyses, with additional
cross-case synthesis to highlight common innovation patterns
and transferable results. The discussion and conclusion were
expanded to explicitly connect findings to existing theories of
digital transformation, governance, and open innovation, while
introducing stakeholder-specific practical recommendations.
Minor editorial improvements were made for consistency,
clarity, and alignment with Open Research Europe’s formatting
requirements.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
the end of the article
