Topic:
The title "CEO Dimensions and Their Impact on Innovation Capabilities: An Empirical Study of Colombian MSMEs" is straightforward and informative; advisable, it is recommended to include "mediation" as a major contribution of the article lies in testing a mediation model.
Abstract
The abstract well written nonetheless, it does not clearly address the mediating function of essential constructions within the suggested model.
Introduction
This study focuses on MSMEs in Colombia, however there is a lacking of information about Colombian MSMEs. Moreover the problem statement does not adequately address the challenges  or issues and research gaps related to Colombian MSMEs.
The paragraph is well written and conceptually coherent; however, several cited sources exceed the commonly preferred 5-year.
Moreover, there is lacking discussion on transformational leadership as mediator.
Methods
The paragraph clearly states the target population (CEOs or top executives of MSMEs in Colombia) and appropriately justifies the use of purposive and convenience sampling given practical constraints. However, to strengthen the sampling justification and enhance the generalizability of the study, the authors are encouraged to provide official statistics on the total number of MSMEs in Colombia.
It is advisable for the authors to provide
more detailed information regarding the screening and inclusion criteria
, including the procedures used to identify and select eligible CEOs for the study
The description of SEM could be more precise by indicating whether CB-SEM or PLS-SEM was employed and why this approach was appropriate for the study’s objectives (e.g., theory testing vs. prediction).
Results
Advisable do not used word "WE". Can change "to this study" or "this research".
Advisable for correlational analysis explained in result not as a correction structure (figure 2)
Discussion and Conclusion
Well discussed.
Overall Comment.
:
The authors are encouraged to review the overall flow and organization of the manuscript. In particular:
(i) The placement of Section 2.6 (Ethical Considerations) may be reconsidered, as its current location may not best align with the manuscript structure.
(ii) The literature discussion could be further refined to ensure clearer linkage with the research objectives.
(iii) The statistical analysis and results sections may be combined into a single
Findings and Analysis
section to enhance clarity and coherence.
