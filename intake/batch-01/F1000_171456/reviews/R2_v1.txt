The article "CEO Dimensions and Their Impact on Innovation Capabilities" offers a relevant approach for Latin American countries, employs an appropriate method, and presents interesting findings that other researchers in developing regions might replicate. Some revisions must be made before acceptance.
1. Please, improve the storyline of your paper. The Introduction must be reviewed to present a relevant research gap.
2. Please, provide a paragraph with all the contributions of this paper and present citations to prove each contribution.
3. Please provide better policy recommendations or practical implications. The current version is weak.
4. Please review the entire Conclusions section. Rewrite more critically.
