Introduction
This study focuses on MSMEs in Colombia, however there is a lacking of information about Colombian MSMEs. Moreover the problem statement does not adequately address the challenges  or issues and research gaps related to Colombian MSMEs.
The paragraph is well written and conceptually coherent; however, several cited sources exceed the commonly preferred 5-year.
Moreover, there is lacking discussion on transformational leadership as mediator.
Change or Comment:
We have strengthened the Introduction section by explicitly discussing the structural and innovation-related challenges faced by Colombian MSMEs, including limited R&D investment, institutional constraints, and weak innovation ecosystems. Also, we have clarified how these contextual factors amplify the role of CEOs in fostering innovation outcomes, thus reinforcing the relevance of our research gap in the Colombian MSMEs context.
Additionally, we have included more recent (from 2021 onward) to support the research gap identification and give support to the research hypotheses. Nevertheless, we consider that some older research works provide a strong theoretical background, as well as some others are seminal works that are relevant to this date. Thus, we have kept some of them.
Reviewers’ Remark:
4.
Methods
The paragraph clearly states the target population (CEOs or top executives of MSMEs in Colombia) and appropriately justifies the use of purposive and convenience sampling given practical constraints. However, to strengthen the sampling justification and enhance the generalizability of the study, the authors are encouraged to provide official statistics on the total number of MSMEs in Colombia.
It is advisable for the authors to provide more detailed information regarding the screening and inclusion criteria, including the procedures used to identify and select eligible CEOs for the study
The description of SEM could be more precise by indicating whether CB-SEM or PLS-SEM was employed and why this approach was appropriate for the study’s objectives (e.g., theory testing vs. prediction).
Change or Comment:
To strengthen the sampling justification and contextualize the scope of the target population, we have added (in Subsection 2.2 - Participants and Sampling) official statistics indicating that Colombia has more than 1.7 million MSMEs, representing over 90% of the country’s productive units. This information highlights the scale of the population under study and further supports the use of purposive and convenience sampling given the practical constraints associated with accessing CEOs of MSMEs.
Moreover, we have added the inclusion criteria used for the CEOs screening process (in Subsection 2.2 - Participants and Sampling).
We have clarified in the Methods section (in Subsection 2.5) that a covariance-based SEM (CB-SEM) approach was employed using the lavaan package in R. We further justified this choice by noting that the study is theory-driven and focused on hypothesis testing and mediation analysis rather than prediction
