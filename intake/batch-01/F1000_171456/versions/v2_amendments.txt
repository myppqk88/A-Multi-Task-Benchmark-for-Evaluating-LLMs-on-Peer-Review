Revised Amendments from Version 1
The manuscript has undergone substantial revisions aimed at improving its theoretical clarity, methodological rigor, and overall coherence. The Introduction was significantly revised to strengthen the storyline, clearly articulate the research gap, and better contextualize the study within the challenges faced by Colombian MSMEs. Recent literature was incorporated to support the hypotheses while retaining seminal works essential to the theoretical framework. The mediating role of transformational leadership was emphasized throughout the paper, including a revised title and abstract that explicitly highlight mediation as a core contribution.
The Methods section was expanded to provide greater transparency, including official statistics on Colombian MSMEs, detailed inclusion and screening criteria for participating CEOs, and a clearer justification for the use of covariance-based structural equation modeling (CB-SEM) for theory testing and mediation analysis. Minor stylistic revisions were made in the Results section to improve clarity and consistency.
The Discussion and Conclusions were carefully rewritten to adopt a more critical perspective, clearly distinguishing theoretical contributions, practical and policy implications, study limitations, and directions for future research. Additionally, the overall organization of the manuscript was refined, including adjustments to ethical disclosures and stronger links between the literature review and research objectives. Collectively, these revisions enhance the manuscript’s rigor, readability, and contribution to leadership and innovation research in emerging economies.
