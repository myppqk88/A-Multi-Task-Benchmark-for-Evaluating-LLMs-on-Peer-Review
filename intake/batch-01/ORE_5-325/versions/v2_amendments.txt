REVISED Amendments from Version 1
The manuscript has been fully reorganized into a conventional research-article format (Introduction–Methods–Results–
Discussion–Conclusion), with a clear separation between evidence and interpretation. The Introduction now states the
research gap, aims/questions, core contributions, and provides a concise positioning within relevant scholarship. The
Methods section has been expanded to provide an explicit account of research design, sites, sampling and recruitment (N =
22; 17 men, 5 women), data generation and documentation (fieldnotes), analytic procedure (inductive cross-case thematic
analysis with abductive engagement), ethics/consent, and limitations. The Results are presented in structured thematic
clusters, while the Discussion synthesizes the findings in relation to return migration, moral economy, and Cuban transformations of work and business, making the argument and contribution explicit. The Conclusion has been rewritten to
provide a concise synthesis, implications, and future research directions, with a brief epilogue situating the analysis in the
present conjuncture. I have also standardized the abstract (continuous text), aligned sample descriptions across sections,
and tightened the academic register throughout.
