Revised Amendments from Version 2
Summary of Revisions
This Version 3 incorporates focused conceptual, methodological, and structural revisions in response to Reviewers 3 and 4. We clarified the article’s core contribution by adding an explicit statement of originality at the end of the Introduction, emphasizing: (i) a three-vector framework (institutional fit, doctrinal compatibility, Vision 2030 alignment), and (ii) the identification of a distinctive hybrid model emerging from the interaction between agency law reforms, Shariah doctrines, and investment policy.
To sharpen scope, Section 1.2 now states that the study’s primary focus is doctrinal and comparative analysis, with economic and policy material serving as contextual support. Repetitive discussion of digitisation, arbitration, and Vision 2030 has been streamlined, and the WTO/BIT paragraph in the Introduction has been condensed.
Doctrinal analysis has been strengthened by adding interpretive commentary after each reform axis in Section 3 and clarifying that cited case law (e.g., BoG 2451/1435H) is illustrative. Section 6.1 now more clearly links Shariah doctrines to specific contractual clauses and distinguishes documented practice from forward-looking expectations, with added references to fiqh al-muʿāmalāt and maqāṣid al-sharīʿa.
Section 4 now opens with a short comparative-method paragraph, and the UAE/UK subsections are reorganized along three dimensions: registration, termination, and dispute resolution. Empirical claims have been moderated to emphasize correlation, and all indicators are documented in a new Appendix Table A1. The Data Availability statement has been updated accordingly
