       Thank you for your feedback. We have answered your comments in order of appearance
       below.

       1. Thank you for your comment. Another reviewer highlighted the same concern, we
       therefore provide the same answer hereafter. We agree that FAIR principles are more
       complex than what is represented in Table 3.

       Our aim in Table 3 is not to reduce the complexity of the concept of FAIR but rather to show
       beginners that following FAIR practices can also be achieved starting from small steps. We
       believe that FAIR can be incorporated into the organisation of open science training
       sessions and that our training series was designed with these principles in mind. The Table
       is expected to summarise good practices we applied throughout the process. We revised
       the article acknowledging that FAIR is a complex and multifaceted concept, clarifying that
       the purpose of Table 3 is to provide guidance to beginners who do not know FAIR on how to
       start with simple steps.
