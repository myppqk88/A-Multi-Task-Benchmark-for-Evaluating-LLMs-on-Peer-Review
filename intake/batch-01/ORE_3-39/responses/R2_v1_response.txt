       Author Response 30 May 2023
       Lottie Provost

       Thank you for your feedback. We reply to the issues you have raised in order of
       appearance.
          1. Thank you for your comment. We agree that the article may be more relevant for
             beginners in open science training and support staff than for researchers. We chose
             to include our case study in the Research on Research Gateway because we believe
             researchers who study meta-research may find the materials useful. However, the
             article does indeed primarily intend to provide guidance to those who want to
             organise open science training but do not know where to start because of a lack of
             experience or resources. We revised the article to clarify this point and make it more
             relevant for our target audience.

           2. Regarding the online calendar of training events, we were not able to find such an
              occurrence in the article. Could you please be explicit about which part of the article
              you are referring to? We have provided two possible explanations below to clarify the
              use of calendar tools to organise theTRIPLE training series.

             (1) An internal calendar was used by team members to organise upcoming training
             sessions and share the tasks, it was not made public as it served for internal planning
             purposes only. By mentioning it, we wished to emphasise the importance of iterative
             communication and alignment of the training sessions with the project participants’
             preferences and timeline to make sure that the training events indeed met their
             needs.

             (2) Each training event was promoted online on a dedicated page of the website
             along with the date, time, location, and topic of the event. Once the event was over,
             the event was pushed to the bottom of the page under ‘Past Events’. The page still
             exists (https://project.gotriple.eu/training/) to make the training materials available,
             however it is not used as an online calendar of training sessions, as it is clearly
             mentioned that the training series is over.

           3. Thank you, we acknowledge that our discussion of FAIR in Table 3 is simplified and
