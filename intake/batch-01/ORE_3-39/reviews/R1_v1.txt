       This is a much needed and well written work providing both Open Education Resources for Open
       Science training, and freely reusable guidelines for organizing training sessions. Both these
       research outputs are extremely important to have if we want to help the community transition
       faster and easier towards more open research practices.

       I applaud the authors’ choice to deposit the materials in Zenodo to ensure long-term access and
       the choice of an open license.
       The authors take the time to explain everything in detail, but I do have two points that I would like
       to see tackled before final publication.
           1. The part describing the FAIR qualities of the material developed doesn’t seem enough to
              me. For example, Table 3. (Simple steps to manage training materials following the FAIR
              principles) is definitely a good starting point, but FAIR is much more than that, and I believe
              elaborating more on this would add immense value to the work!

          2. I also wonder if the authors have looked at all into accessibility features: are the files
             accessible to people with impaired visibility/no vision? I don’t see any HTML file, for
             example, which would be better to have in case screen readers were to be used.
       Some other (minor) comments:
           ○ Hyperlink in section 2 does not seem to work.

           ○   In section 3, when referring to the TRIPLE Training Toolkit, this reference is used: Di Donato
               et al., 2022, which points exactly to this article I am reviewing; this seems very odd to me,
               perhaps an error?

           ○   This reference comes back in the Underlying Data section, I would check that, too.
