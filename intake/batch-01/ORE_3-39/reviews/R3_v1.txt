       This report focuses on the online training activities on Open Science delivered within the H2020
       TRIPLE Project. The main goal was to address SSH research and training communities’ needs for
       enhanced competencies on Open Science and for stronger support in the Findable, Accessible,
       Interoperable, Reusable (FAIR) management of digital training materials. To do so, two reusable
       outputs were produced: first, a series of 12 openly accessible online training sessions specifically
       designed to provide skills and competencies to SSH researchers on FAIR and Open Science
       practices; second, the TRIPLE Training Toolkit, an open and reusable workflow containing
       resources for trainers (organisers and research performing organisations) to reproduce and adapt
       (so, to design and manage) to different training contexts in the future.

       Authors present a very good state of the art on digital skills for FAIR, Open Science and training
       resources. All the materials provided are a good proof of the effort they did for a common
       understanding of recent European Open Science advancements.

       The results presented focus on the delivery of competence-oriented training and publishing
       training materials as OERs: they can enable trainers in the SSH community to reproduce the case
       study that is showcased following best practice. All information concerning the 12 online training
       events held with a total of 600 attendees is available and easily findable.

       Surveys, learning objectives and learning outputs are presented in such a way that the skills and
       expertise required are perfectly identified: those necessary for open access publications; those
