         5. “gross enrolment rate increasing from approximately 60% in 2010 to 103% in 2020” I
         think I know what the author means but worth clarifying how it is 103%

         Response: We have included a footnote that clarifies why the gross enrolment is rate is
         above 100%. The gross enrolment rate as defined by the World Bank is the total number of
         students enrolled in the primary school regardless of age. Therefore, the gross enrolment
         rate includes students who are younger or older than the official age range for primary
         school and can exceed 100%. Rates above 100% means that total enrolment at the primary
         school exceeds the size of the official primary school-age population often due to grade
         repetition or late/early school entry.
