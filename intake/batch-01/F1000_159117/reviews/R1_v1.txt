The present article aims to examine the trends in research on women’s studies in Latin America.
Introduction:
The rationale presented at the introduction section is confusing. It looks like they are presenting articles as referents of the state of knowledge and to justify the present article, however, this style can confuse readers. In some point someone may think there are several aims. I recommend to improve the synthesis of knowledge for clarity purposes.
Methods:
It is not clear what authors mean by erroneous indexing, please explain.
They state that they focused on systematic reviews, but this is not mentioned before.
It is not clear how authors decided to exclude texts deemed of marginal relevance to the study. Please clarify.
There are other terms related to America Latina used in Mesh and Decs, like Mexico, Brasil etc.
Overall this is a interesting article, even the results can look very descriptive more than analytical, they provide evidence for further research. The conclusion are well sustained based on their results. I recommend to expand the implications beyond academic research and be more specific with the processes of translating knowledge to public policy. I think this section is the most valuable of all articles.
