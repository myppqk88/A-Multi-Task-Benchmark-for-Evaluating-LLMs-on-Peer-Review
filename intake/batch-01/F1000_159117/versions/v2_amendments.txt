Revised Amendments from Version 1
In this revised version of the article, several key clarifications and improvements have been made based on the reviewers' feedback. The introduction was refined to improve the synthesis of knowledge and ensure a clear justification of the research objective. The concept of "erroneous indexing" was clarified to avoid ambiguity, and further details were provided on the exclusion criteria used in the study. Additionally, we explicitly stated our focus on systematic reviews and clarified the applicability of document removal criteria.
Furthermore, we acknowledged the limitations of our search strategy, particularly regarding the use of MeSH and DeCS terms related to Latin America, and how an expanded search would require a new study. Finally, we enhanced the discussion of our findings by expanding the implications beyond academic research, emphasizing their relevance to public policy and the processes involved in knowledge translation.
