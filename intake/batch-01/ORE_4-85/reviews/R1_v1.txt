       The paper proposes a roadmap for age-inclusive workforce management. The literature is
       reviewed, and the authors compare and discuss age-inclusive management practices and policies
       based on six domain actions (organizational culture and leadership, work design and ergonomics,
       health management, knowledge management, intergenerational coexistence, and retirement
       pathways) at three different levels of intervention: international, country, and company. Data
       collection and analysis were conducted in eight countries: Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan,
       New Zealand, Slovenia, and the USA. Finally, several aspects are proposed for future research in
       the conclusion.
       The work is interesting and well-structured. The ideas and discussions presented in the paper are
       interesting, and the work is sound. There are some remarks/questions to improve the work as
       follows:
           ○  The main contributions/findings of the paper should be highlighted at the beginning of the
              work (perhaps in the introduction or end of literature).
           ○  The reference for the information given in Figure 1 should be noted and discussed more
              thoroughly in the text.
           ○  The authors should justify the three sub-sections proposed and explain the link between
              them. A short description could clarify what will be discussed in the three sub-sections.
           ○  I liked the idea of describing the link between each of the six domains as done at the end of
              sections 3.3 and 3.4. However, similar discussions are lacking for other domains. Authors
              can address this. Additionally, it should be clarified how the link (or position) of the six
              domains aligns with the three proposed levels. The analysis could be conducted this way,
              and perhaps Figure 3 can be improved (or maybe a new figure created) to show the
              position/link of domains and levels.
           ○  It is good to see how the 92 companies studied in section 4.3 differ. What are their major
              sectors? An analysis could also be done for the different types of companies studied per
              country.
           ○  Sections 5 and 6 are well-presented and include some discussions and future research
              focuses. However, in my opinion, they deserve more discussion and propositions regarding
              methodological and scientific contributions for the future. Several aspects are presented,
