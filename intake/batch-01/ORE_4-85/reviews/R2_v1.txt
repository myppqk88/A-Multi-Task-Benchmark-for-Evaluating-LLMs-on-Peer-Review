       The manuscript provides a wide picture of policies and best practices adopted in eight countries to
       address age-related workforce management. In particular, three levels of intervention
       (International, country and company) and six action domains are investigated to develop a
       roadmap facing challenges and opportunities that an aging workforce imposes.
       I think that this is an excellent piece of work related to a very current and relevant topic for the
       management of modern businesses towards more inclusive workplaces, which are a key element
       of the long-term sustainability for companies.
