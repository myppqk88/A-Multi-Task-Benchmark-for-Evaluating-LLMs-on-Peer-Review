The following limitations were identified in the study:
1.Why was the secondary school level specifically examined in the study? Why weren't elementary schools or other levels examined? Because such a problem also exists in other levels. The article needs to explain in more detail why secondary schools were chosen.
2. Why were studies conducted specifically between 2019 and 2025 considered in this research? Is there a particular reason for choosing 2019 as the starting year? It is believed that explaining the rationale for this choice in the article would be appropriate.
3. The interpretation of the following finding revealed in the research is important. Why are there more studies on this topic in 2023? Researchers should evaluate this.
“Interest in integrating mathematical competencies and critical thinking skills in secondary education has shown a steady increase between 2019 and 2025. Over the last three years (2022–2024), scientific output has increased by 67%, with 2023 standing out as the most productive year, accounting for 25% of the total with six studies.”
4. The need for such a study should be explained in greater detail in the introduction section of the article. At the same time, it remains unclear whether critical thinking influences mathematical competence or whether mathematical competence influences critical thinking. What kind of relationship exists between these two constructs? The answers to these questions should be discussed more comprehensively in the introduction section.
