Revised Amendments from Version 1
This revised version of the article does not introduce substantial modifications to the methodology, the systematic review design, or the overall structure of the manuscript. The changes made respond exclusively to the reviewer’s comments and focus on conceptual, justificatory, and interpretative clarifications.
First, the justification for focusing on secondary education has been clarified and strengthened, explicitly explaining why this educational level represents a key stage for the development of formal reasoning and for the integration of mathematical competencies and critical thinking.
Second, an explicit justification of the 2019–2025 time frame has been incorporated, linking it to the consolidation of critical thinking as a transversal competence in international educational policies and to the acceleration of active methodologies following the COVID-19 pandemic.
Third, a contextual interpretation of the publication peak in 2023 has been added, relating it to the maturation of post-pandemic educational innovation projects, increased institutional funding, and the growing emphasis on transversal competencies.
Finally, the conceptual explanation of the relationship between critical thinking and mathematical competence has been deepened, clarifying that it is a bidirectional and reciprocal relationship rather than a linear one.
In summary, this version does not extend the methodology or modify the results, but rather improves theoretical clarity, argumentative coherence, and contextual grounding, strengthening the academic precision of the manuscript.
