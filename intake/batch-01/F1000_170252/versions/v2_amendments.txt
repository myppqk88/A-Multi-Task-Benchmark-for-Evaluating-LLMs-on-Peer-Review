Revised Amendments from Version 1
The revised version introduces substantial improvements based on reviewer feedback.
Firstly, the abstract has been modified by adding specific quantitative findings and key gaps or future research directions.
The introduction has been revised highlighting the novelty in particular and phrasing the objectives as clear research questions.
Methodological clarity has been strengthened by elaborating on the screening process and how bias was minimized. Table 1 has been modified by including a column which contrasts previous systematic reviews in this field with the current study. A brief note describing the classification scheme of how levels were decided for the theory mapping has been added under the section "Key determinants of BDA adoption".
Quality of results and statistical rigor has been improved by adding quantitative measures like total links and link strength of clusters to enhance analytical credibility of the bibliometric analysis.
A unified conceptual model has been proposed and developed at the end of the results section to explain how stronger individual beliefs can support capability building within organizations and how those capabilities can lead to better outcomes. This integrative model visually links frameworks and needs to be tested by future researchers.
Discussion now explicitly revisits each research questions and summarizes how the review answers the questions by mapping them to the findings. Limitations including publication and language bias have been elaborated in detail. Discussion now better connects empirical findings for theoretical building.
Collectively, these revisions enhance theoretical coherence, methodological clarity and overall rigor of the study.
