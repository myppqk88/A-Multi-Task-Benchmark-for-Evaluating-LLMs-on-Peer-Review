Revised Amendments from Version 1
The revised version introduces substantial conceptual, methodological, and theoretical enhancements based on reviewer feedback. First, a unified theoretical framework has been developed, integrating the Theory of Planned Behavior (Ajzen, 1991), Social Identity Theory (Tajfel & Turner, 1986), and the Elaboration Likelihood Model (Petty & Cacioppo, 1986) into a cohesive conceptual model that explains the consumer journey from engagement to adoption. A new figure now visually represents the relationships among Instagram engagement, psychological and demographic factors, brand trustworthiness, purchase intention, and consumer adoption.
Second, methodological clarity has been strengthened by providing an explicit definition and category examples of “luxury products,” enhancing the precision of respondent understanding. The limitations section has been expanded to acknowledge the modest sample size (N=205) and its implications for machine learning model generalizability. A new subsection now addresses causality, endogeneity, and omitted variable bias, recognizing that observed relationships are predictive, not causal, given the cross-sectional design.
Additionally, the discussion and results sections have been refined to use associational language (e.g., "linked to," "predicts") rather than causal terms. Finally, the "Scope for Future Research" section has been revised to recommend longitudinal, experimental, and econometric approaches—such as field experiments, instrumental variables, and panel data methods—to establish stronger causal inference. Collectively, these revisions enhance theoretical coherence, methodological transparency, and the overall rigor of the study.
