This study explores the impact of Instagram on luxury brand adoption, analyzing data from 205 respondents to identify key drivers of purchase intention and brand trustworthiness. The findings reveal that psychological and social variables, such as brand trust, prestige, uniqueness, and personalized recommendations, strongly influence consumer behavior, while surface-level engagement metrics have limited predictive power. A Support Vector Machine (SVM) model accurately predicts adoption likelihood with 87% accuracy, and sentiment analysis shows predominantly positive consumer attitudes toward Instagram marketing strategies. The study offers actionable insights for marketers, highlighting the importance of authenticity, emotional connection, and targeted psychographic segmentation in luxury digital marketing.
