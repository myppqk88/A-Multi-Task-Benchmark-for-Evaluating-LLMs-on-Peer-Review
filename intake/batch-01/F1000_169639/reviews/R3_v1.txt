This study has five objectives. However, in the discussion, there are six hypotheses. What is the connection between H6 and the previous five hypotheses?
The five hypotheses appear to have no relation to one another. For example, the dependent variable in H1 is Consumer Adoption, while the dependent variable in H2 is Purchase Intention. What is the relationship between Consumer Adoption and Purchase Intention? Likewise, the dependent variable in H3 is Consumers’ Perception of Brand Trustworthiness. Furthermore, in H6, the term consumer sentiment suddenly appears. What is the connection between consumer sentiment and the variables from H1 to H5?
It would be better if the author started by creating a diagram of the conceptual framework of this research and explained the roadmap for the development of the research hypotheses.
The study uses luxury products as its objects, but the type and examples of luxury products used have not been explained in sufficient detail. Are they apparel products (bags, sandals, shoes), clothing, or something else? Since luxury products come in many forms.
In figure 2, it is stated that H1 is not supported. Why does the analysis suddenly use logistic regression in H2 with different variables? What is the reason for using logistic regression to test H2 instead of multiple regression? Looking at the variables, the independent and dependent variables for H1 and H2 are completely different. What is being conveyed by using two different models for the same study?
In the testing of H3, the analytical tool is path analysis (different from H1 and H2), with the dependent variable being Brand Trustworthiness. From figure 3, it can be seen that the engagement variable acts as the dependent variable, and not Brand Trustworthiness. Could you please explain this?
as a review summary, we would like to ask why so many analysis tools are being used (Multiple Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Path Analysis, Support Vector Machines (SVM), Decision Tree modeling, and Sentiment Analysis) to test the research questions. please explain the sequence of using these tools according to the existing roadmap
