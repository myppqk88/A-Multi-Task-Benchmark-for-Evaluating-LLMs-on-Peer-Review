It is recommended to include a broader theoretical background section that contextualizes the study within the Latin American literature on university entrepreneurial behavior. This would highlight the gaps addressed by the research and strengthen its theoretical contribution.
The sample size and selection should be explained in greater detail, including the justification for the number of participants per country, the voluntary sampling criterion, and potential limitations of representativeness. Including a statistical power analysis or a theoretical reference would strengthen methodological validity.
It is suggested to clearly separate the Results and Discussion sections, as empirical descriptions and theoretical interpretations are currently intertwined, making the analysis difficult to read and understand. A structural division would provide greater rigor and clarity of argument.
The Discussion section should delve into the added value of the study, explaining its practical implications for universities in the region and comparing the results with relevant international research. This will highlight the unique contribution of the work.
It is recommended to add a section on limitations and future lines of research, addressing aspects such as the cross-sectional design, the use of self-reports, and cultural differences between countries. This section would enhance transparency and provide guidance for future studies.
The introduction could include explicit research questions or hypotheses, which better articulate the objectives with the methodology and results. This would increase the internal coherence of the manuscript and facilitate the assessment of theoretical validity.
Although the use of raincloud plots is a visual success, it would be advisable to complement them with descriptive tables (means, standard deviations, and effect sizes) to allow for more precise interpretation and possible replication of the study.
