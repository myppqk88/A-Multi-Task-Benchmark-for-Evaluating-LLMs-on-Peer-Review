Revised Amendments from Version 1
This new version of the article incorporates revisions based on the reviewers’ comments. The theoretical background section was expanded to more clearly position the study within the Latin American literature on university entrepreneurial behavior.
The methodological description was strengthened by providing a detailed explanation of the sampling strategy, the justification of sample sizes by country, and the inclusion of a post hoc statistical power analysis that supports the adequacy of the total sample. Additional clarifications regarding voluntary sampling and the multinational recruitment process were also included.
The structure of the manuscript was improved by clearly separating the Results and Discussion sections. The new Discussion section contrasts the findings with international research and deepens the analysis of their practical implications for strengthening university entrepreneurship ecosystems in Latin America.
A dedicated section on Limitations and Future Research Directions was incorporated, addressing issues related to the cross-sectional design and cultural heterogeneity across countries.
The Introduction now includes explicit research questions that guide the interpretation of the results. In addition, descriptive statistical tables (means, standard deviations, standard errors, and effect sizes) were added in Appendix A to complement the raincloud plots and enhance the precision and replicability of the analysis.
