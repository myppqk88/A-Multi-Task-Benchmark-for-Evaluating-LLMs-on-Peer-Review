Revised Amendments from Version 2
In this revised version, the manuscript was strengthened in response to the reviewer’s comments, focusing on improving theoretical clarity, methodological transparency, and practical relevance. The Introduction was expanded to explicitly articulate the study’s purpose, identify a key gap in the Latin American transnational entrepreneurship literature, and clearly specify the theoretical and practical contributions of the research. In addition, the research questions were refined to better reflect the analytical scope of the study, including cross-national comparisons and the examination of entrepreneurial intention and the distinct phases of entrepreneurial action.The Theoretical Background was refined through a more explicit description of the relationships among psychological, cognitive, and contextual variables, supported by recent empirical literature, along with a synthesis linking the conceptual framework to the empirical analyses.
The Abstract was revised to clearly present the study’s objectives, methodological approach, and main findings, distinguishing between entrepreneurial intention and the discovery and exploitation phases of entrepreneurial action, and highlighting the practical relevance of the results for curriculum design and the strengthening of university entrepreneurship ecosystems.
Methodological transparency was further enhanced by expanding the Methods/Participants section to clarify the sampling strategy, justify the selection of ACBSP-accredited universities as a criterion for institutional comparability, and describe the multinational recruitment process.
Although confirmatory techniques such as SPSS/AMOS and mediation or moderation analyses were suggested, the exploratory and cross-national comparative design of the study was reaffirmed. In line with the ordinal nature of the data and the descriptive–explanatory objective, non-parametric analyses, correlational techniques, regression models, and visual analytics using raincloud plots were retained, with mediation and moderation identified as directions for future research.
Finally, the manuscript now presents clearly separated Results and Discussion sections and includes recommendations for university management and the design of context-sensitive institutional policies.
