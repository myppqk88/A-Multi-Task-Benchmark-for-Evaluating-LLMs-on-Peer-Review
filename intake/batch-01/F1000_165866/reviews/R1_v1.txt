Dear Editor and Authors,
This study is a robust bibliometric analysis of 10 years of scientific research on motivation and job satisfaction, spanning 2014-2023. The analysis is detailed. However, the article could be further improved. My suggestions for improving the article are as follows:
1. The structured abstract mentions the methodology in the background. Add background information to the abstract.
2. “Hertzberg posits that employees’ level of job satisfaction is truly influenced by both internal and external factors in their working ambience. Job satisfaction and motivation are related to each other; if one is affected, the other will also be affected. Few studies have been conducted on this topic.”
Add a footnote for Hertzberg in this sentence. Also, there are numerous studies on this topic, and a significant portion of motivation and job satisfaction research utilizes Hertzberg.
This sentence (“Few studies have been conducted on this topic.”) is incorrectly worded or does not seem appropriate. It should be rechecked.
3. Check the following statement after the definition of motivation: “Motivation is a psychological concept that cannot be applied to employees.”
This statement can lead to misunderstandings, as if motivation were entirely internal and unmanageable. Therefore, it contradicts modern theories such as Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory.
Please refer to the following source on this topic:
-"Motivation is influenced by internal and external sources and basic psychological needs. Managers can both increase employees' external motivation by regulating external conditions and create a favorable environment to foster internal motivation. Therefore, motivation fundamentally stems from the individual's internal processes; however, managers can significantly influence employees' motivation levels by providing appropriate external conditions." Please refer to source 1: Karakaya, A. ve Ay, F.A. (2007), Çalışanların Motivasyonunu Etkileyen Faktörler: Sağlık Çalışanlarına Yönelik Bir Araştırma, C.Ü. Sosyal Bilimler Dergisi, Cilt : 31 No:1, ss. 55- 67.
4. Literature Review
This section presents information on methods used in previous research, such as Biblioshiny, VOSviewer, and BibExcel. However, before this information, the study should include a literature review, primarily focusing on motivation and job satisfaction.
5. Checking the sources is necessary. For example, check the following information in the article:
“Kannan, Kulandai, & Ramachandran (2024) in the bibliometric analysis revealed key factors associated with motivation, job satisfaction, and retention of nurses in their respective working places. Moreover, the study significantly contributes to the understanding of nurse job satisfaction, retention dynamics, and assorted facets of banking on the findings of earlier studies.”
Upon examination of this research (Kannan, Kulandai, & Ramachandran (2024)), it was found that it did not include a bibliometric analysis, although this was mentioned in your explanations.
6. In line with the purpose of the study, only scientific studies were reviewed. Explain the relationship between motivation and job satisfaction, supported by the literature, for high impact.
7. Table 2 appears to be missing data for other years.
8. The statement regarding data availability can be reiterated. As you stated in the article, “Publication data on job satisfaction, ranging from 2014 to 2023, were extracted from Scopus.” (Your data consists of these studies).
9. Add limitations to your study.
10. Utilize the most influential articles included in your findings.Support your analysis of motivation and job satisfaction with substantial supporting literature, and add practical implications based on your article.
Best regards.
