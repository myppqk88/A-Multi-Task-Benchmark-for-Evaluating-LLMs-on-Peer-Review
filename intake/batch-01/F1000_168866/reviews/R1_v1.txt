1. The qualitative procedures are well described, but additional detail on sampling size and coding reliability would further improve replicability
2. The study is qualitative and does not rely on statistical analysis
3. The article is methodologically appropriate, clearly written, and well positioned for education, curriculum studies, and financial literacy journals. Revisions are mainly needed to sharpen theoretical framing, methodological transparency, and analytical depth, rather than to correct fundamental flaws.
4. Results could be slightly more specific (e.g., explicitly naming “risk management” and “digital finance” as gaps).
5. Explicitly state research questions (e.g., RQ1, RQ2) near the end of the introduction.
6. Briefly anchor the analysis within one or two curriculum or learning theories to strengthen the conceptual foundation.
7. Criteria for selecting Universitas Jambi and Universitas Pendidikan Indonesia should be clearer.
8. The discussion largely restates results rather than deeply interpreting them.
9. Recommendations are broad and sometimes generic.
10. Reduce repetition with earlier sections.
