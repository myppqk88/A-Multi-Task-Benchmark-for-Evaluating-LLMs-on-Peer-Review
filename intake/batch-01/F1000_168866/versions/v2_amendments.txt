Revised Amendments from Version 1
This revised version addresses the comments raised during peer review to improve the clarity, methodological transparency, and analytical depth of the study. Several revisions were made, including the addition of explicit research questions in the Introduction, a clearer explanation of the theoretical perspective supporting experiential learning approaches in financial literacy education, and expanded methodological details regarding sampling procedures, data sources, and coding reliability. The Results section has been refined to more explicitly identify curriculum gaps, particularly in areas such as risk management, investment diversification, and digital financial tools. The Discussion has also been strengthened to provide deeper interpretation of the findings and more specific recommendations for curriculum development. Minor edits were made throughout the manuscript to reduce repetition and improve overall clarity.
