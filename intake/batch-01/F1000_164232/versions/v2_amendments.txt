Revised Amendments from Version 1
The revised version of the manuscript incorporates the corrections suggested by the reviewers. The “methods” section was expanded to describe in greater detail the data extraction and thematic coding process, given that the review was performed manually.
The recent version includes a more operational description of the protocol, including the criteria used for extraction, the coding and thematic synthesis procedure, and the logic used to link these results to the development of conceptual networks. This expansion seeks to strengthen transparency, reduce the possibility of arbitrary interpretations inherent in manual processes, and promote, as far as possible, the reproducibility and auditability of the method.
Essentially, the “methods” section was expanded and strengthened to include a detailed description of the data extraction protocol and the parameters of the software used to generate the bibliometric and thematic maps, and explicit mention was made of the Bibliometrix software used in the study.
Likewise, the analysis of the bibliometric and thematic maps was strengthened in the introduction and discussion of the article.
Finally, it should be clarified that this adjustment does not modify the results or conclusions of the study; it only reinforces the clarity, methodological consistency, and rigor with which the extraction and coding stage that supports the findings is reported.
