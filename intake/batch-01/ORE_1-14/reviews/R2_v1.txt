       The article provides new data on women's underrepresentation in senior career positions and
       decision-making positions in two Higher Education institutions in Tunisia. Data in both institutions
       depict a similar picture and provide evidence for gender equality policies supporting women's
       promotion and counteracting gender prejudices and traditional gender norms - for both women
       and men.

       The article builds on this to elaborate specific recommendations for these institutions and Higher
       Education in general in Tunisia. It is an article which provides valuable data, reflection and
       recommendations to advance gender equality.

       Minor aspects which could be addressed to improve the article:
          1. "Another important issue is sexual harassment in Universities. In 2016, the European
             Institute for Gender Equality published an inspiring comprehensive study in support of the
             elimination of sexual distinction, to ensure gender equity and gender equality in
             academia".
             The reference to "sexual distinction" is not fully clear. Bibliographical reference is missing.

           2. Women/WeMen: it seems a highly inspiring initiative. It could be further developed.
