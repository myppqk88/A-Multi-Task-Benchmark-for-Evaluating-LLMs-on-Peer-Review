       Dear, Thank you for allowing us to adjust our paper. Below is the list of issues you asked us
       to address and our responses. Weaknesses:
           1. Lack of Exploration of New Intersections: While the paper provides a comprehensive
              overview of the challenges faced by Syrian refugees in Lebanon, it does not explore any
              new intersections or phenomena in their lived experiences. The findings seem to reiterate
              existing knowledge without offering any novel insights into the complexities of Syrian
              refugees' experiences in Lebanon.
           2. Absence of Intersectional Approach: The paper lacks an intersectional approach, as the
              findings are presented in a rather generalized manner, and the population of Syrian
              refugees is homogenized. There is a missed opportunity to explore the intersecting factors
              that shape the experiences of different subgroups within the Syrian refugee population,
              such as gender, age, socioeconomic status, and educational background.
           3. Qualitative Findings Need Further Development:While the qualitative findings provide
              valuable insights into the challenges faced by Syrian refugees in Lebanon, they could be
              further developed to offer more nuanced and 'new' approaches to the research question.
              The paper primarily focuses on highlighting challenges matter-of-factly without delving
              into the underlying dynamics and complexities of the refugee experience.
       Your list of weaknesses made us reconsider the way we presented our analysis. So, we have
       reworked the discussion and conclusion of our paper, adding on the dynamics of the dual
       power relation between political inability and Syrian social agency in the shadow of the
       formal structure of the city and rural areas. We highlighted the presence of patterns to
       navigate the difficulties in daily life and the role of the spatial location of the displaced in
       their capacity to cope with the struggles and their aspirations for the future.
       Recommendations:
           1. Develop New Intersections: The authors should endeavor to explore new intersections in
              the lived experiences of Syrian refugees in Lebanon, moving beyond existing knowledge to
              uncover novel insights into the complexities of displacement and forced migration. This

          This study didn’t address the individuality of the refugee experience. Instead, we assessed
          how the Lebanese political decision of denying legal status to refugees, determining the
          spread of Syrians across the country, has resulted in unequal treatment and increased
          marginalisation and vulnerability of the displaced based on their localisation. The study
          revealed that communities located in ITS in rural areas benefitted more from UN assistance
          than Syrians living in the city’s apartments. Some info on gender has been added in the
          section on aspiration and the reworked discussion and conclusion.
               1. Adopt an Intersectional Approach: The paper would benefit from adopting an
                  intersectional approach to analyze the findings, recognizing the diversity within the Syrian
                  refugee population and exploring how intersecting factors influence their experiences and
                  vulnerabilities. This would provide a more nuanced understanding of the challenges faced
                  by different subgroups within the refugee population.
          While we recognise the importance of the reviewer’s recommendation, we want to
          emphasise that our study used a case study approach aimed at assessing and highlighting
          the importance of the type of settlings and their location on the well-being, disparities in
          treatment, marginalisation and vulnerability of Syrians in Lebanon. Therefore, we didn’t
          look at individuality but at the community and geopolitical differences and how they relate
          to the governance of the migratory phenomena and development aid.
               1. Use Qualitative Findings to Develop Nuanced Approaches: The authors should utilize
                  the qualitative findings to develop more nuanced and 'new' approaches to the research
                  question, moving beyond merely highlighting challenges to uncovering the underlying
                  dynamics and complexities of the refugee experience. This could involve conducting further
                  qualitative analysis to explore themes and patterns in greater depth and developing
                  recommendations based on these insights.
          Thank you for your insightful comment. To answer your recommendation, we identified
          themes and patterns in the discussion and conclusion and developed new
          recommendations based on these insights.
