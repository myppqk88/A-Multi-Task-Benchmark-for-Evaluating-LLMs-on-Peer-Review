Amendments from Version 1

Acknowledging the importance of the reviewer’s comments and
recommendations, we have reworked our paper to respond to
their concerns and suggestions.
The major revisions in our paper are concentrated at the
beginning and the end. We have completely rewritten the
introduction, better emphasising that our study adopts a case
study approach to assess and highlight the impact of different
settings and their locations on the well-being, treatment
disparities, marginalization, and vulnerability of Syrians in
Lebanon. Additionally, we have reorganized the discussion and
conclusion sections for better coherence and flow. In this part,
we have identified themes and patterns as results of our analysis
and developed new recommendations based on these insights.
Small changes have been made in the results section, where we
have added paragraphs to the ‘Respondents’ overall conditions’
and to the ‘Aspirations and priorities of respondents’.
Figures, data and authors have remained unchanged.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
the end of the article
