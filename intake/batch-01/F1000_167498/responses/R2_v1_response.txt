Dear Prof. Indra Prasetia,
We sincerely thank the reviewers for their insightful comments and constructive feedback, which have greatly improved the quality and clarity of this manuscript. Below, we address each point in detail and describe the corresponding revisions made
Comment 1:
This work is presented clearly and accurately. However, there are outdated data reviews in uncovering research issues. (e.g. The education system, which once showed progress with enrollment rates increasing from 71.3% to 97.5% between 1999 and 2013, has since experienced significant setbacks. By the 2015/2016 academic year, around 1.8 million children were out of school, and over 1,100 schools had been either destroyed or repurposed for emergency use.27). More updated data support is needed to reinforce current issues.
Response:
Thank you for your valuable and constructive feedback regarding the use of outdated data in the introduction. We agree that current statistics are essential for accurately framing the research problem within Yemen's severe and ongoing humanitarian crisis. In direct response to your comment, we have thoroughly revised the introduction by connecting the outdated with the most current, authoritative data available from major UN and humanitarian organizations (2024-2025) (Two references were added [28, 29]).
Comment 2:
This study uses an integrative approach to explore and priorities obstacles in the context of education monitoring in Yemen. My analysis shows that this integrative approach generally refers to research using mixed methods. If this is a mixed design, it needs to be explained in detail with clear references (a diagram of the stages also needs to be provided).
Response:
Thank you for your constructive feedback. In response, we have revised the Methodology section. New paragraphs describing the framework were added, a new diagram was provided, and the entire section was restructured to explain the detailed steps of the proposed framework in a clear sequence, supported by appropriate references.
Comment 3:
The methods and analysis have been sufficiently explained, but there are a few shortcomings. The stages of theory-based data analysis (literature review) have been applied in the research, and a number of important themes or variables such as B1, B2, … B11 have been identified. In my opinion, this is content analysis that produces themes (is my opinion correct?). If this is correct, then the literature data and analysis need to be presented, leading to the conclusion of the themes found (B1, B2...B11), so that these themes are supported by accurate data and analysis.
Response:
Thank you for your insightful observation. In response, we have revised the section to explicitly frame this methodological step. The 11 core barriers (B1–B11) are now clearly presented as direct outcomes of the analysis, with each theme succinctly defined and immediately supported by its foundational citations from the literature. This directly links the conclusions to the supporting data, ensuring the themes are evidence-based. To maintain a logical flow and avoid duplication, the detailed interpretation and synthesis of these findings are elaborated in the dedicated Discussion.
