1.    Is the work clearly and accurately presented and does it cite the current literature?
Comment: This work is presented clearly and accurately. However, there are outdated data reviews in uncovering research issues. (e.g. The education system, which once showed progress with enrollment rates increasing from 71.3% to 97.5% between 1999 and 2013, has since experienced significant setbacks. By the 2015/2016 academic year, around 1.8 million children were out of school, and over 1,100 schools had been either destroyed or repurposed for emergency use.
27
). More updated data support is needed to reinforce current issues.
2.    Is the study design appropriate and is the work technically sound? [Please consider whether appropriate controls have been included]
Comment: This study uses an integrative approach to explore and prioritise obstacles in the context of education monitoring in Yemen. My analysis shows that this integrative approach generally refers to research using mixed methods. If this is a mixed design, it needs to be explained in detail with clear references (a diagram of the stages also needs to be provided).
3.    Are sufficient details of methods and analysis provided to allow replication by others? [Please consider whether the authors have adhered to relevant reporting guidelines]
Comment: The methods and analysis have been sufficiently explained, but there are a few shortcomings. The stages of theory-based data analysis (literature review) have been applied in the research, and a number of important themes or variables such as B1, B2, … B11 have been identified. In my opinion, this is content analysis that produces themes (is my opinion correct?). If this is correct, then the literature data and analysis need to be presented, leading to the conclusion of the themes found (B1, B2...B11), so that these themes are supported by accurate data and analysis.
4.    If applicable, is the statistical analysis and its interpretation appropriate?
Comment: Already in accordance
5.    Are all the source data underlying the results available to ensure full reproducibility?
[We require that all data underlying figures and tables are provided (unless there are issues of confidentiality) as part of the article or deposited in a public repository. The data should be described in a usable and accessible format]
Comment: The data based on the literature reviewed in Section 2.1 needs to be presented. (I suggest presenting it in a simple table format.)
