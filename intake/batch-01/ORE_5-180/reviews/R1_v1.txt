       Thank you for the opportunity to review this interesting paper on adoption and perception of
       agroecological practices across nine European countries.

       This is timely research and the authors do well to contextualize the study within more current
       events (eg farmer protests across Europe). The objectives of the study were very clear, as was the
       overall organization of the paper. The use of tables was effective and helped to clearly show the
       paper’s findings. I especially appreciated Table 8/future avenues of research, and it illustrated the
       complexity of the issue and the various ways it could be explored

       There are a few areas where more detail would help strengthen the paper’s overall contribution:
           ○ Methods: Providing a few more details re: EcoStack would help contextualize the research
             and ground the paper better
           ○ Methods: How and why were countries chosen?
           ○   Methods: The authors state that they recruited participants on “the basis of convenience
               and professional networks.” – what exactly did this entail?
           ○   Table 5: the different colors used in the table, especially the light green, are a bit difficult to
               see. Perhaps more contrasting colors could be used, or perhaps non-black colors could be
               bolded for contrast?
           ○   Discussion: “There was a striking contrast in the perception of agroecology between the
               participants and the research teams, with differing terms of reference between science and
               practice.” (pg 8) – more explanation/detail here would be helpful
           ○   Table 6: did reasons for adopting agroecological practices differ considerably across
               country? I’m specifically curious about “subsidies” and whether this was identified across all
               sites, or whether it was concentrated in a few countries.
           ○   In general, it would be interesting to see more discussion disaggregated by country

       Finally, there are two parts of the paper still in draft form and should be attended to before
       indexing:
