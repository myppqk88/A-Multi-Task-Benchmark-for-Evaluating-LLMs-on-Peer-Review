       1. The manuscript has been well-developed with strong data support. However, several sections
       require additional elaboration, including agroecological concepts, research objectives,
       methodology, and conclusions. Furthermore, literature older than 10 years should be avoided.
       2. The technical execution of the work is commendable. However, the research design requires
       several improvements, particularly in the methodology section.
       3. The research design section warrants special attention, as several components have not been
       comprehensively addressed, namely: research paradigm, data sources, data types, and data
       analysis procedures.
       4. While the research data is of good quality, the foundational material supporting the findings
       requires additional exploration, particularly in the introduction, objectives, and methodology
       sections.
       5. Given the nature of this research, it clearly does not require statistical analysis. Therefore, I do
       not have the authority to comment on this aspect.
       6. The conclusions are adequately supported by the research findings. However, the presentation
       remains overly lengthy and should be more concise with brief and substantive justifications.
