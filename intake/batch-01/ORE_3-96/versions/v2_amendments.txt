REVISED Amendments from Version 1
     
In this version, we improved several sections to provide
additional clarity. First, we separated the literature review from
the introduction and added economical expectations formed
from the literature regarding our hypotheses on the demand,
the impact of demand on the ne issue premium (NIP) and the
impact of demand on the formation of large orderbooks. Second,
we provided additional information regarding the dataset
preparation. Third, we explained the “tapping” mechanism
of a bond and the definition of the tenor buckets motivated
by market considerations better. Last, we added an outlook
with directions for further research: We are interested in how
ESG aspects are influencing issuer risk and investor demand.
Furthermore, we are working to extend our analysis to the
secondary market. Finally, a larger combined dataset from more
public issuers could enable multivariate analysis for the demand
across issuers.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
the end of the article
