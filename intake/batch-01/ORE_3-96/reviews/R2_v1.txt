       I appreciate the chance to serve as a reviewer on this paper. The paper is well written and suits the
       scope of the issue. I urge the author(s) to consider the following comments and improve the paper
       accordingly.
           1. At first, I think the authors should make clearer the economic meaning of their research
              starting with an economic motivation.

           2. I think the introduction is too long which somehow makes the reading difficult. Therefore, it
              is better for it to be divided into two sections (introduction and literature review).

           3. I would like the authors to make a prediction about investor behavior in the near future in
              similar financial conditions where green bond issuance has been overwhelming.

           4. It would also be useful for the audience and future researchers if a guide for the future
              research is provided: how could this research be used concretely to open new pathways? Is
              it possible to provide some examples and possible directions for future research?
