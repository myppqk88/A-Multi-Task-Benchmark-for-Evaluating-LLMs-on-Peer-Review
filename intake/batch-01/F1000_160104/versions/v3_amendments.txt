Revised Amendments from Version 2
This revised version of the article incorporates substantial refinements in response to reviewer feedback and further author evaluation. First, the abstract and introduction were expanded for clarity, with improved framing of the research problem, updated references, and stronger justification for integrating sustainable business values (SBV) and the role of universities (UR) into the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB). The literature review and hypotheses development were reorganized to enhance logical flow, include additional recent studies, and better highlight the contextual distinctions between developed and developing economies. In the methodology section, details about population, sampling, and instrument development were elaborated, ensuring transparency in the research design and pretesting process. Tables were updated, with redundant or low-loading indicators removed (e.g., items from PBC, SBV, UR, and SN constructs) to improve reliability and validity. The results section now presents clearer reporting of measurement and structural model assessments, including HTMT ratios, Fornell–Larcker criterion, and discriminant validity, supported by additional justification for indicator deletions. The discussion was expanded to explain the theoretical implications of significant and non-significant findings, particularly regarding the rejection of PBC and UR hypotheses, and to position these results within emerging economy contexts. Practical implications were broadened to suggest competency-based curricula, experiential learning, and stakeholder engagement for universities and policymakers. The conclusion and limitations were refined to acknowledge sample size, geographic scope, and the need for future longitudinal and cross-country studies. Overall, this version provides greater conceptual clarity, methodological rigor, and contextual depth compared to the earlier version
