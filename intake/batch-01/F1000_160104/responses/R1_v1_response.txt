Author Response
First, in the abstract section. In the background section, I see that this section needs to identify the global issue of entrepreneurship education in higher education in developing countries that is associated with the importance of sustainable entrepreneurial intentions among Indian higher education students so that from this description, the focus or objectives of the research can be formulated. Then, in the method section, it is enough to focus on describing the method used in this study; there is no need to describe the results of the study. In the method section, it is also necessary to describe the population, sample, and sampling technique. The description of the results in this section is very problematic. In this section, it is better to focus on conveying the results of the research based on the objectives of the research. Finally, in the conclusion section, this section is also very problematic because it does not match the title of the study. Focus on providing reinforcement related to the intention of sustainable entrepreneurship in developing countries, and with these findings, what are the theoretical implications and practical implications? This abstract section needs to be reformulated to be more systematic.
We sincerely thank you for your kind response and advice. We hope that the current improvements to the article allow for an easier understanding of the topics discussed. We still hope that the revised version would be considered suitable for publication.
The background section has been revised to explicitly highlight the global issue concerning the effectiveness of entrepreneurship education in higher education, particularly in developing countries. (Page: 4)
The importance of fostering sustainable entrepreneurial intentions among Indian higher education students has been clearly articulated to establish the context and relevance of the study. (page:5)
The methodology section has been revised to strictly describe the research method without including results. (Page: 13,14 &15)
Details regarding the population, sample size, and sampling technique have been explicitly included to enhance transparency and replicability. (Page: 13,14 &15)
The results section in the abstract now strictly reports the findings in alignment with the stated research objectives. (Page 20 & 21)
Overgeneralizations and vague interpretations have been eliminated to maintain objectivity and focus. (Page 20 ,21 & 22)
The abstract of the study has been modified as per the suggestion. (Page 1)
Second, the introduction section. This section has been formulated quite well. However, there are several areas that need additional descriptions. There are several theoretical perspectives that can be used to view the intention of sustainable entrepreneurship other than TPB. However, the author chose to use the TPB theoretical perspective; this is, of course, necessary to obtain theoretical reasons, especially when associated with TPB for the case of developing countries. In addition, this section should be supported by the presence of research questions that are continued with a description of the focus or main objectives of this study.
The introduction has been modified as per the suggestion provided. (Page 3).
The theoretical perspective of TPB and sustainable entrepreneurship has also been re-examined in the current paper by considering the context of developing countries. (Page 3 &4)
Third, the literature review and hypothesis development section. TPB is used as a theoretical perspective in this manuscript, but there is no specific explanation of how the TPB perspective is derived into research variables. As a special note, in developing this section, the author did not consider looking at theoretical gaps or previous findings related to how TPB is used to view the intention of sustainable entrepreneurship in developing and developed countries. Why is it important? Because I see the main contribution of this manuscript is in the use of the TPB theoretical perspective to measure the intention of sustainable entrepreneurship in developing countries, including India. Are there any previous cases or research in India or other developing countries related to this theoretical perspective? The hypothesis has been developed quite well, but almost the entire hypothesis development section has not looked at the relationship between variables using the TPB perspective.
The theoretical perspective of TPB and sustainable entrepreneurship has also been re-examined in the current paper by considering the context of developing countries. (Hypothesis development)
Fourth, the method section. There is a serious problem with sampling. This study claims that this is the case for a developing country, India. However, I see that the sample used is only 234. How many populations are there? Do these 234 represent the entire population for a country with a large population like India? In addition, I did not find specific indicators of each variable studied.
We have incorporated the changes and have provided the justification in the methodology. (Page 14)
Sixth, the discussion section. In the early part of the discussion, there is no need to explain the method used, because this is just a less substantial repetition. In addition, this section only repeats the results section and only compares its findings with other research findings. My suggestion is that a more critical discussion needs to be made, especially on new findings that provide a new perspective on the TPB. Provide reinforcement regarding this.
All the suggestions have been incorporated in the revised manuscript.
