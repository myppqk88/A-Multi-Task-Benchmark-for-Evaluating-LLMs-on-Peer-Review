Thank you for the opportunity to review the manuscript ‘Cultural barriers in women's entrepreneurial education: Challenges, perceptions and transformation strategies’ submitted to F1000Research. I enjoyed reading the manuscript and found it informative on entrepreneurial education.
The manuscript has great potential, but the reasoning does not flow naturally. The paper must be improved in line with the comments below:
1. The title is very descriptive but not overly wordy. It needs to be broader to better reflect the study's aims.
2. While the authors provide purpose in the initial paragraphs, research gaps and significant contributions are not clear! How is this study different and unique?
3. In the introduction, this study needs to be enhanced with more arguments exemplifying the importance of entrepreneurial education and this research. There is a lot of published work in the 2020s to strengthen the narrative on this, such as Barba-Sanchez et al. (refer to reference no. 1), Cardella et al. (refer to reference no. 2) or Atienza-Barba et al. (refer to reference no. 3).
4. An article must be self-contained, i.e. it must contain all the information necessary for its comprehension. Table 1 currently lists only the title, authors, and year of each study. However, this information is already available in the reference list, so the table in its current form does not add substantial value to the review. It might be more informative to include elements such as the main themes addressed in each study, the methodology employed, the sample or context, and the key findings or conclusions. This would enrich comparative analysis and offer readers a clearer understanding of the contributions of each selected paper.
5. In the methodology section, the manuscript mentions that ‘The information was organised into pivot tables that facilitated comparative analysis and the generation of descriptive graphs aimed at visualising trends.’ While tables can be useful for organizing information, it may have strengthened the rigor of the analysis to use specialized qualitative analysis software—such as ATLAS.ti or NVivo—to support coding, categorization, and thematic synthesis. Employing such tools is generally recommended in systematic literature reviews because they enhance transparency, replicability, and analytical depth. Please explain.
6. Given that the authors propose a Conceptual framework of female entrepreneurial education, they should also identify the propositions that stem from the systematic literature review. This would reinforce the conceptual robustness and clarify how each proposition is grounded in the qualitative synthesis of prior research.
7. The discussion needs improvement and lacks significant theoretical reference to the findings.
8. Theoretical, methodological, and practical implications should be further elaborated and explicitly tied to the identified gaps and the novelty of the investigation.
9. The authors should develop the limitations and future work. Please do not list the future research lines; integrate them into the text.
I hope you find the above comments useful, and I wish you the best of luck with developing the paper further.
