REVISED Amendments from Version 1
Major Differences and Enhancements
Introduction and Conceptual Framing: Reviewer 1 requested a more concise introduction and clarity on how upSkill.Map
complements existing frameworks. The introduction was condensed, highlighting how upSkill.Map advances ResearchComp, Vitae RDF, and OECD frameworks, supported by a comparative table. Reviewer 2 suggested engaging with literature
on bridging research–practice gaps; references to Lenfant (2003), Greenhalgh & Wieringa (2011), Phipps et al. (2016), and a
Special Issue were added, positioning upSkill.Map as a bridge between individual development and systemic co-productive
research ecosystems.
Methodology and Study Design: EFA with a small sample (n = 40) was justified with relevant literature; parallel analysis using
1,000 Monte Carlo simulations was included. The DBR process was expanded to detail iterative cycles and decision points.
Recruitment bias was addressed: initial institutional outreach yielded 10 participants (<2%), followed by peer-activation
snowballing adding 23 participants across faculties, demonstrating latent eco-outwards engagement.
Instrument and Qualitative Methods: The original 32-item instrument was included in an appendix; four items removed
based on communalities <0.30 or cross-loadings >0.47. Qualitative methods were expanded to detail coder roles, procedures, and audit trail. Thematic analysis highlighted communication skills, co-definition of societal challenges, policy
engagement, and participatory research as central competencies.
Conclusions and Recommendations: Conclusions were reframed to reflect preliminary validation. Recommendations now
integrate societal engagement, co-designed professional development, participatory research, and structural supports.
Additional enhancements include a schematic model of CARE–KNOW–DO and 8Cs, implementation guidance, and
expanded literature and DBR descriptions.
