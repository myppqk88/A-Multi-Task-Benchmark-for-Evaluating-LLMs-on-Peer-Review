REVISED Amendments from Version 2
This revised version incorporates substantial changes in response to detailed reviewer feedback, improving clarity,
coherence, and alignment between objectives, methods, results and conclusions. The Abstract and Introduction sections
were revised to more clearly articulate the specific objectives of the paper in relation to previous publications on the ECSA
Characteristics and to strengthen engagement with literature emphasizing the diversity and plurality of citizen science
practices. Language referring to the “professionalization” of citizen science was reframed to emphasize inclusive support
structures and legitimacy across diverse practices. Additional text now more clearly links national and international
organizations to their contributions in delineating the field and better integrates scholarly perspectives. The Methods
section was clarified through a new opening paragraph distinguishing prior work from the novel methodological contribution of the paper, particularly the identification of gaps in the ECSA 10 Principles and how the ECSA Characteristics can
address them. In the Results and Discussion, the paper clarifies its novel contribution in relation to earlier Zenodo outputs,
explicitly references previously published statistical analyses, expands descriptions of applied frameworks, and strengthens
links to relevant literature on non-strict definitions and plurality. Applications of the ECSA Characteristics were retained and
framed to demonstrate practical relevance. The Conclusions were reorganized to highlight the core contribution of the
paper, disciplinary framing, and reinforce the added value of the ECSA Characteristics compared to the ECSA 10 Principles.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at the end of the article
