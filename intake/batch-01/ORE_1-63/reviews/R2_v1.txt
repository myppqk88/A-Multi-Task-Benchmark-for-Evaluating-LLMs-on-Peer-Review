       I am not very familiar with the scope and aim of the journal to which I was invited to review. Thus,
       I may not be pitching my response at the right level, as I opted to provide a review on the article in
       light of the questions posed to me and my knowledge of the area I am reviewing.

       The article engages with some of the relevant literature to support the claims made, but it does
       not make it clear how does it build on that literature, what gaps it fills and how does it contribute
       to existing knowledge. I wonder if a comprehensive survey of existing literature regarding gender
       and leadership types has even been made. I also wonder about the extent to which expertise from
       the disciplines of sociology, gender studies or other social science backgrounds have been
       involved into the research of this interdisciplinary theme.

       It is not clear what kind of empirical data supports the piece. The authors are clear in stating their
       commitment (as a group) to promote gender equality in engineering schools and learning for
       sustainability, and to fight the under-representation of women in leadership and senior positions
       at the engineering education systems. But we miss the information on what study / research was
       conducted and to what aim. Rather than being supported by a study, the article comes closer to a
       manifesto. The content may be convincing and I (as probably many other readers) sympathise
       with the goal of gender equality in managerial positions and the education machinery, support
       values proposed and agree with multiple other claims made (e.g. that gender equality strategies
       are context sensitive or that leadership has typically had a masculine construction.). However, the
       article would need to significantly more precise in various points to qualify as a research article. As
       a side note, for analytical purposes, I would suggest writing in slightly longer paragraphs where a
       certain thought is developed, rather than in one sentence paragraphs (Discussion, Conclusion
       section)

       Is the work clearly and accurately presented and does it engage with the current literature?
