1. Methodology
Search criteria are less specific: Search keywords are too generic. Add more systematic keyword combinations using Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT).
Publication time span is too wide (2000-2023): Consider focusing on the last 10 years to get findings that are more relevant to the current educational context.
Risk of bias assessment is mentioned but the results are not explained in detail. Add a more comprehensive table or explanation.
2. Data Analysis
Only 20 articles out of 202 were used - this ratio is very small (9.9%). Need a more detailed explanation of the exclusion criteria.
No quantitative meta-analysis: If possible, add effect size or aggregate statistics from the studies reviewed.
The categorization of the study (3 for engagement, 8 for skills, 9 for performance) overlaps and needs to be clarified.
3. Results and Discussion
The prevalence data (Figure 2) does not have an explanation of how the percentages are calculated. Was it from the 20 studies reviewed or other sources?
Contradictions are not discussed in depth: Holmes & Hwang's (2016) studies that found no significant differences need further analysis – what distinguishes their context?
Overgeneralization: Conclusions are too general when only based on 20 studies.
4. Technical Weaknesses
Inkonsistensi citation style: some use "eg." some use  "e.g."
Typo: "BjBL was fought" should "thought" (page 7)
Figure 1 (PRISMA diagram) - The numbers are inconsistent - need to recheck the exclusion flow
Tidak ada informasi About Inter-rater Reliability in the Article Selection Process
5. Contextual Gaps
The geographical context is unclear: From which countries are these studies from? Can the findings be generalized to the Rwanda/Africa context?
Education level is not consistently specified (elementary, junior high, high school mixed)
The duration of PjBL implementation in the reviewed studies was not discussed
