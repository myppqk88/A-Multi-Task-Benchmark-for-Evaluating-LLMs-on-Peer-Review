Revised Amendments from Version 1
We thank the reviewer for their constructive comment, which has helped us strengthen our article.
The reviewer observed that while our study examined correlations between demographic factors and academic staff performance, the analysis did not fully explain how these demographic characteristics influence performance. In response, we made the following revisions:
In the Results section (pp. 6–7, after Table 3), we expanded the analysis to show how gender, age, educational qualifications, academic position, teaching experience, and field of specialty shape staff performance. For instance, we now interpret gender differences in terms of disparities in professional growth opportunities, and qualifications in terms of enhanced research capacity.
In the Discussion section (pp. 7–8), we added interpretive passages that demonstrate how demographic characteristics are not just correlations but determinants of academic staff performance. For example, we discuss how higher qualifications and senior positions expand access to leadership roles, while different fields of specialization channel staff contributions toward either teaching or research priorities.
These revisions ensure that the study now fully addresses the influence of demographic characteristics, making the analysis consistent with the title and strengthening the study’s contribution.
We are grateful for the reviewer’s thoughtful feedback and hope that the revisions improve the clarity and value of the paper for readers.
