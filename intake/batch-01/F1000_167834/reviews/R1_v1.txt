The paper is valuable in providing insights into The Influence of Demographic Characteristics on Academic Staff Performance in Private Chartered Universities in western Uganda. A few improvements would be helpful to enhance this work
Title: the title highlighted the influence of Demographic Characteristics on Academic Staff Performance in Private Chartered Universities in western Uganda, but unfortunately, the study confined on examine correlations between the demographic characteristics, and has not examine how these demographic characteristics influence the academic staff performance?
I recommend the authors to expand their analysis to include how these demographic characteristics influence the academic staff performance? (these has been presented in the literature review but has not been examined the analysis section).
In the background what has been done is valuable. In addition, it is recommended to present what distinguishes your study from another previous study.
