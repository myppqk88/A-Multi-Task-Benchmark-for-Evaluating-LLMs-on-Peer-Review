REVISED Amendments from Version 2
     
In this third version of our article, we have implemented the
following changes in response to the reviewers’ feedback:
• Expanded the introduction to include additional
references to current literature on demographics and
motivations for participation in citizen science studies,
providing a more comprehensive contextual foundation.
• Introduced the research questions and outlined the
scope of the paper earlier in the introduction, alongside a
preview of the paper’s overall structure to guide readers.
• Improved the organisation of the methods section by
relocating the statistical analysis of the sample from
the results section to the materials section for greater
coherence.
• Enhanced the discussion to improve the logical flow and
emphasise key findings.
These revisions aim to improve the readability and logical
structure of the paper, making it more accessible and engaging
for the audience.
Any further responses from the reviewers can be found at
