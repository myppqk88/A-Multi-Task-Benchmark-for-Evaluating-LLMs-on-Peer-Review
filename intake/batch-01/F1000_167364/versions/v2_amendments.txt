Revised Amendments from Version 1
This revised version incorporates substantial changes in response to the reviewers’ comments, aiming to strengthen the clarity and methodological rigor of the study. First, we clarified the adaptation of the PRISMA 2020 protocol for use in a systematic literature mapping, complemented with the guidelines of Petersen et al. (2015), ensuring transparency and reproducibility.
Second, we contextualized the temporal concentration of studies (2019–2024), explaining that it reflects a genuine academic trend rather than a methodological limitation.
We also corrected and clarified the PRISMA flow diagram, detailing the identification, screening, and eligibility phases. In addition, we justified the exceptional inclusion of one study without explicit use of technology (Ciftci & Savas, 2018) due to its conceptual contribution.
Percentages and tables were reviewed and corrected, with an explicit note that categories are not mutually exclusive, which may result in totals exceeding 100%. Geographic inconsistencies (e.g., classification of the United Kingdom) were also corrected, and the multifunctional nature of tools such as COIL was explicitly acknowledged.
Finally, the conclusions were revised to reflect the coexistence of structured tools (LMS, COIL) and flexible approaches (social media), while broadening the discussion of research gaps and future directions.
