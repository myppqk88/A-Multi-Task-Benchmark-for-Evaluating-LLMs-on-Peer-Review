Revised Amendments from Version 1
In this revised version, we have made substantial improvements to enhance the clarity, depth, and contribution of our manuscript based on the valuable feedback provided by the reviewers. The key revisions include:
Restructured the Introduction and Abstract: We refined these sections to emphasize the originality of our study, clearly articulate the research objectives, and provide a concise overview of the methodology and findings.
Updated Literature Review: We incorporated recent references (2022–2025) from international peer-reviewed journals to strengthen the theoretical foundation and align the study with current research trends in digital payment adoption and knowledge-sharing perspectives.
Expanded Methodology Section: Additional details have been provided on the sampling procedure, justification of sample size, and validation of the survey instrument to enhance methodological transparency.
Clarified Statistical Analysis: The SEM approach has been explicitly detailed, including a clearer explanation of model fit indices and the relationship between statistical findings and the research hypotheses.
Strengthened Theoretical Contributions: We elaborated on how the integration of Trust, TAM, and TPB advances existing technology acceptance models and positioned digital payment adoption as a knowledge-sharing process in emerging economies.
Expanded Discussion and Practical Implications: We provided a deeper interpretation of the findings, linking them to theoretical contributions and real-world applications. Specific recommendations for policymakers, financial institutions, and technology providers have been added.
Added a Limitations and Future Research Section: We explicitly addressed the limitations of our study, including geographical constraints and methodological considerations. Future research directions, such as cross-country validation, longitudinal studies, and additional psychological constructs, have been proposed.
Updated References Section: The reference list has been revised to include the latest studies and ensure proper citation formatting.
These revisions have significantly improved the manuscript's clarity, depth, and contribution to the field of digital payment adoption. We appreciate the reviewers' constructive feedback, which has guided these enhancements.
