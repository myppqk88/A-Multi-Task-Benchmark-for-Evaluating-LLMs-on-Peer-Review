The purpose of the study was to examine the correlation between sensory sensitivity (ranging from very low to very high sensitivity) and preferred dot speed. We therefore recruited participants with a wide variance in sensitivity, from below average to highly sensitive, regardless of the presence or absence of a clinical diagnosis.
