This paper is about an interesting topic of the speed of bubbles and how its relates to interoception and visual discomfort. Here are a few comments :
In the introduction also mention the work on sensory hyperreactivity and anxiety such as Green et al., 2010 and 2012, MacLennan at el, 2020, 2021, Verhulst et al., 2022.
Please comment on why healthy adults were used for this study. The  introduction covers anxiety, autism and mental health conditions and thus it might have been more informative to use one of these groups rather then healthy controls.
